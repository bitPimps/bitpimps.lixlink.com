If it's a diecast body, you're gonna need a tri cell and maybe even a dual motor setup to push it at any real speed.
Then there's the width of the car body. (driver side to passenger side) You may need to fabricate an extension for the front knuckle pins or you might be able to just use some brads. You would also need to extend the rear axel.
You'ld also have to extend the zip chassis to length.
Good luck, it's definatly do able.
