Alright, I'm back.
Sporttracguy wrote:Ive got a question How can i start writing for the magazine and start scoring all these great play things?
Just keep doing what you're doing, enjoying the hobby, posting about it on forums. When you feel you have something to offer, try to get their attention. They are all over the internet, MySpace, Facebook, Twitter, You Tube, all that crap.

When I got their attention, he asked to see what I had to offer, I had several, tutorials, write ups, and reviews that I wrote here, that I linked him too. So all them hours posting on the forum paid off.
ok, so some serious questions, what is to the right of the micro t (bug)? its yellow, orange and purple, an rc18t maybe?

Im also curious about the black ride between your mini LST and the xmod hummer
The black one is actually Cheezoom Teal, and that is an OG Iwaver 04, basically an exact copy of a MZM. I added bearing, the faster Perfection motor, alloy shock towers intended for the Overland, and a Lexan Chevy Colorado body pulled by DogBreathRacing. By this point it's being held together by strapping tape and superglue, but it's still going.



...and yep you guessed it,a RC18. It was a B that I converted to a T. The Body is a Proline Crowd Pleaser. I dropped a LRP 18 turn mod in it, put a heat sink on the speed control, and borrowed the motor heatsink from the Tailwhip. It's really fast, and it's simple design is efficient. My only bitch is the open spur... got to get one of them Driven gear covers...

EDIT: I was just browsing the RC Driver website after posting this, and found the exact motor I put in my RC18 has just been re-released for 2009
http://rcdriver.com/newweb2/articles/fusion370.shtml" target="_blank


sidewinder wrote:I'm interested in the truck with the Semi body, top center.
Ahh The Exceed Mad Beast. One my favorites. It is comparable to the Savage at a much lower price, smaller than the XL, but still larger than the 4.6, 6'75" tires, and a 15" wheelbase, with the same size 4.6 motor and a 2-speed. While longer wheelbase makes it more stable, you can't do all the crazy wheelie stunts you see people do with Savages on YouTube. It is big and fast and will plow over most anything. This is the car that I crashed into a steel pole at top speed, mid-air, and the only damage was the lower shock mount on the suspension arm. That's pretty tough. But although the parts and fit are top notch, it hasn't really been tuned from the factory, the suspension needs tweaking, and the carb needed quite of bit of adjustment to get it running right, which is probably why these cars get such a bad rep among newbs, but with lots of tuning advise from Payaso, I was able to work through it no prob.
