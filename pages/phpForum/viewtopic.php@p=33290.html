I have an R-Spec II, and in a direct comparison with the 3.8 I found it to be very similar to the 3.8, but with tons more midband torque. Both motors are dogs off the line compared to the Perfection motors, but kill the Perfection motors in top speed, despite Perfections higher number rating.

With a single cell unmodified car you kinda have to use 12:1 gearing with these motors, but they take to FETs great, because they are power hogs, and FETs give them the juice they need. In a FETed car both the 3.8 and R-Spec II will dust the Perfection motors.
