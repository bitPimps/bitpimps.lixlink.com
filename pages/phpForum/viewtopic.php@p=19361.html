What's up TB, I like the Nakamichi Racer, as I said earlier it is a 2 speed car though not On Demand. It has a switch for turbo mode which is pretty cool cause while you're in Turbo mode, when you make a turn the car slows down to normal speed (like a brake) and soon as you straighten up the turbo kicks back in. Also reverse is normal mode.

The only draw back is that it's not upgradeable. Motor is hardwired, rear wheels are in an enclosed box attached to the chassis that is a bitch to get off. Body is not compatable with any other Micro so you can't change bodies with anything but another Nakamichi. PCB board is huge! But the car needs no mods, it runs strong, excellent range, steers sharp, tracks straight, & slammin wheels. I'd get them just for the wheels alone! The bodies aren't very detailed but with a nice paint job, it's a contender!
