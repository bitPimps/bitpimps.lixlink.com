i was looking at all my car bodys in my collection and i noticed that the rx8 looks pretty crappy compaired to the rest. so i decided to spice it up a bit. i took the silver spoiler fromt he 2f2f kit that came with the lacer. i took a drill and a drill bit(for get which size but its not that hard to guess) and i drilled new holes for the spoiler. i have to tell you, it looks GREAT, the silver matches the tail lights the little silver things on the front wheel well and the head lights. i suggest everyone do it, you wont regret it. and since it doesn't mess up the other holes, you can switch back to the old spoiler if you want.
