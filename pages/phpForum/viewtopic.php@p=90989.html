Well after seeing those crazy germans and their xmods i knew i had to try something myself. Here is one that really inspired me.

http://549.rapidforum.com/topic=102180190936



Onto the project


So, after seeing the new iw02's that are coming out soon and seeing that they have removable servos that got me thinking.

I used a cll light kit port from a old xmod board soldered it all on and got it to screw on. If i do say so myself it looks very clean.






Heres what the basic setup should look like when done but all cleaned up and seated properly of course.

As you can see i have changed to a lithium setup, i plan on running 2 cells in parallel so i keep it around 3.6 volts but still keep a good amount of torque and power.




Bottom of chassis ooo, ahhh This lighting doesnt do it justice though........




Body shots








Sorry for the somewhat crappy pics i know they arent up to my usual par lol, I just got home and am very tired. (long day) Didnt feel like setting up the whole photobooth thing and the light over my workspace is out

The basic cutout of the chassis was made by a friend. He was very generous and donated it to me. Its my job to put it to work and get it working properly with AWD etc. And i can say its coming along very well.

So far the specs are

Mini-z convert body

Cf decks

Custom chassis with crazy C.O.G.

Bearings

Awd

Ball diff.'s

Iw-04 board

custom wired hs-55

2 Lithium cells wired in parallel

Nylok nuts

Custom exhaust

X-speed motor




But i want your guys's comments go ahead post pimps!

I think thats a post i can end my night on.
