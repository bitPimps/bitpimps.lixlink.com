Post by ph2t » Thu Jan 22, 2004 6:03 pm

The perfection 3.8 series are a good motor. Very fast and good torque. THe perfection 4.2/4.2PROS are a mixed bag. I've found the perf 3.8 to be consistently better than the 4.2's

If you can score any extreme micro motors then get them. (www.extrememicros.com is no longer a retail shop). The EM38 Vanquish is the best motor in my stable.

Also the BitCharg-motors motors are good as well. I just got the bitcharg-motors 3.8 and I've heard great reviews about them. They are meant to be the most consistently fast motor on the market. You get them at tinyrc.com.

All the other perfection series of motors are crap, just get the 3.8's. they seem to be in a league of there own when it comes to the perfection brand.

ph2t.
