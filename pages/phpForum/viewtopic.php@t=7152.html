I bought this boat off of eBay and got a heck of a deal its a 1950 Richardson’s Mahogany boat based on the Richardson 35 foot Cabin Cruiser. I plan to refinish it and bring it back to its original glory.

This boat is a little bigger then what I am used to working with guess the scale is about 1/10 I show a micro RC on the boat so that ya’ll can get an idea of the size. The ship is a little over 24” long and about 8”wide. I am thinking of buying an already built RC boat and then taking the guts out of it to put in this boat!

This boat will put my ship building skills together with my RC skills and well we can only wait and see what comes out of it!

If anyone has any ideas for some RC boat building please let me know any help would be appreciated!
