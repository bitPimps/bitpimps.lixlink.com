Good luck finding something to fit. For millitary, your scale is 1/72 or 1/35. From time to time you can find a little bit of 1/43 millitary, but it's VERY rare and detail isn't always there.

Just scratch build it! It's a millitary vehicle, they're all flat plains anyway, no complex curves!

Search online, get a free "Papercraft" halftrack file, use your computer to scale it to your tank tread size and front wheel, print it, may have to print a few times to get the size you need, and you've got a 1:1 pattern for making it. Pick up a sheet of styrene, a couple blades, CA or good styrene solvent, some files...and you're gold!
