cheers all!, this one was fun to build. real fun! at every level of construction a new idea would pop up and eventually i had to stop. pity its based on a sbcg chassis, otherwize i woulda sent it off for some fetting and maybe some dual cell power. it runs pretty well stock tho with spring suspension(the usual way) and a 4.2 motor.

the flames were done by hand using small brushes and games workshop acrylic paint. that and a lot of time. i'm lucky i did all the choppin' and painting first before i gave it the monster treatment.

as for handling, each of the tyres need to be trimmed in order for it to work. about half on the front and 1/4 off the back pair.

those world industries devilmen are my favorites as well.

superfly, you must have seen my camo Bb? its got roofracks, a 50cal, custom trailer and a cannon in tow. even got magnetic mines strapped to the sides of the trailer in case some smart arse tries to jacknife it, the mines fly off cos they're attracted to the steering setup.
