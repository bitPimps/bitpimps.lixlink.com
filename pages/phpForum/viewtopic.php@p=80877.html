the tires are pretty hard to change on those wheels, they are fairly hard and hollow. The rims have tabs that stick out and fit into the tire. So far with the off road testing and the cement this bit is for sure going to be a dirty bit for small jumps and alot of grit and grime.

IF you wanted to switch the entire wheel combo out to something different its as easy as switching wheels and tires on a bit as far as removing the axle pins up front and the wheels from the rear axle and just sticking whatever you want in there. I chose to keep these wheels and tires to complete the look but i still had to re drill them and mount them to the other axle mount and the front mounts.

having said that you can remove the tires and wrap them in other shoes but finding tires with that large of a diameter may be hard. Im thinking that if i do need a softer compound i can re tread them with some thin rubber or some sort but it works great for what i built it for. any major corners at dual cell speed on a kitchen floor would probably cause her to tip although i cant say for sure until i give it a try

Ok, one more update for the night, the suspension is finished, the solid antenna is mounted, the on/off switch is mounted and the truck runs awsome on the smoother cement and in the dirt(not so great on the kitchen floor though) I still have alot of work to go before its done but its getting there. Tomorrow ill try and make an aluminum skid plate and sides(coke cans work awsome for that) get the bumper mounted, lights, front and rear skid plates, baja style spair tire lock, and a few other ideas that im toying with. ill get more pics tomorrow pimps
