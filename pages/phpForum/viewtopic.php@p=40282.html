i used

1: a piece of cardboard
2: a soldering iron and some solder
3: a few pieces of wire

i soldered a wire to the positive end of one of the batteries. then i soldered to the negative on the third and ran a wire from the positive end of the third cell to the terminal in the battery compartment and put the cardboard inbetween the wires. the stock charger doesnt like to charge 3 cells however so get ready to rig that as well.
