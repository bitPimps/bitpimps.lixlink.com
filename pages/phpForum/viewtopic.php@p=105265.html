ok so while I am waiting for my gold rims 2 get here... I made a start on changing the colour of the chassis decks... at frist I was just going 2 re- paint them black... but after I started sanding down the decks... I though why not just take them black 2 the silver alloy and then wax the alloy to get it shinny to match the rest of my GPM silver parts... lol...
