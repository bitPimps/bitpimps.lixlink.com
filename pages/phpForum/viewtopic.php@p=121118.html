Re: Lithium Ion or Lipo?

Post by betty.k » Sun Feb 01, 2009 9:40 pm

all lipo cells regardless of size are 3.7v.

all lipo chargers charge at 4.2v (for a single cell).
Astro Flight Lithium Battery Charger/Discharger.
FEATURES: Charges 1-9 cell (4.2V-37.2V) Lithium Polymer battery packs Cell sizes can range from 140mAh to 8000mAh
basically any lipo cell you get that's 140mah and above will charge fine on your charger. you can charge lipos a little bit above 1c but they won't charge faster, you'll shorten their life and it's a potential fire hazard.

my charger doesn't charge below 100ma and i've charged 80mah lipos without a problem. so you could probably get away with charging a cell as low as 100mah lipo on your charger.

just never leave a charging lipo unattended.
