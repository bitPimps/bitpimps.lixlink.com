What's up pimps? It have been a few months since I have really done any modding. I decided to work on a project I started aways back. Anyway here is what I got so far. Starting with a Chevelle ZZ body, looks very similar to a 70 Nova.


The hood and grill came off of a Pro Rodz 70 Nova diecast.

The wheels are off a Dubs Muscle car (just sitting under body)

and the axle gear is from a shitty cheap slotcar set we sell at the ratshack. The gear will allow me to tuck the fatties under the body.

I still have alot of work on getting a chassis modded to work with the gear setup. I have to make a new axle mount and motor mount since the motor will now need to be moved. Plus figure out how to fit it all under the body.It still has a long way to go.
