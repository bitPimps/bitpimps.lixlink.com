Beleive me, it wasn't easy but once I got Da Plan right, it all fell into place.
Just hadda let the paint dry thoroughly before adding the next stage so it did
take a while. It was just a lil difficult masking due to the body angles and
keeping the tape down in the corners so a couple angles are a lil off.
Body work comes easy for me, just that I hadda grind down the insides
so the chassis would fit dropping it this low. I was gonna add box flares to
the rear to even up with the wheels/tyres but decided not to do too much....

Again I hadda lotta fun doin these very different looks to da TVR, I really like
this body. I looked at quite a few racers doing this one. Most of the body works
were the same with variable differences, of course paint schemes were all
together different so once I thought of a paint scheme, I just did it
