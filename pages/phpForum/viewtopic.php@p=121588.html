Motorized steering was a big improvement over Coils because it actually gives torque to steering rather than the more vague feel of the coils. It also didn't have the twitch of the SE barely propo.

4 series Nicad was the absolute upper limit on the earlier generation Bit/Zip boards before they start to release the magic smoke, not sure if these are rated any higher. So a 1S lipo like that should be ok, but all the normal warnings about lithium batteries are still in effect.
