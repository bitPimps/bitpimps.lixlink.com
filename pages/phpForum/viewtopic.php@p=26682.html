ok i just got my hands on one of these things and it is strange. mostly a good strange but its different than any other bit ive ever seen fersure. i gota say the mechanics are top shelf. i havnt had a chance to open her up on the carpet yet but its a lot quicker than ahhshit made it out to be(in my opinion). its quicker than a stock bit with maybe a little less low end.

the controller is gona take getting used to but its not bad so far. still unbelievable that its totally propo for that size and price. the springs are just right.

the only bad ive discovered(other than the ugly body) is there is a slight twitch in the steering but i think that has something to do with the trim which i havnt had time to figure out. and actually the enifi shell isnt as ugly in person as it is on a pic i think. its about as ugly as the original fnf zz shells.
