Think us older guys are bad?

Post by extrememicros » Sun Aug 03, 2003 10:20 am

Other day at my shop, a guy came in about mid to late 60's, and he asked what micro rc's I carried. Immediate thought was, Grandkids birthday...well to my surprise, not the case, him and 3 of his older buddies play with these things!!! They get together once a week and play Micro Pool he called it. They take ping pong balls and number them and put them on a pool table...and play pool!!! Your car acts as the cue ball. You are allowed to tap, slam or push the ball into the pockets. If you push, you are allowed to push until the ball strays from your car. That is your shot.

He said they'd be interested in racing so, I told him to come back monday and we'd talk racin.

He was sittin here telling me about buying Shen clones at Chuck Homiers sale and crappy clones at a local grocery store, as well as telling me how they tried to fix the steering/return spring on the junk clones to make them better...;o)

I was loving it...so now, as I sit at 29, I don't feel so bad....and I may have a few new older pimps to play with once a week...!!!
