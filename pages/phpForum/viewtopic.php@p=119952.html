Surfin' season may be over... even though, let me show you my newest bit of modding stuff I just finished.

body by raceleg (again)
CRW-alurims (handmade by a dude of www.rc-micro.de)
selfmade decals (Quicksilver, Burton, Speck, Calvin&Hobbes, licence plate surfboard, VW-Logo on spare tire)
airbrush paintjob
spare tire mounted with a thumbtack
rack on the roof made from 1mm-welding rod
travelbags from a revell-kit, painted to be looking old
surfboard airbrushed and tribal-decal
cables on the roof are electrowires
jerrycan on platform from the same revell-kit
driver handpainted
inside of the car is real wood
exhaust pipe made from a wire-end sleeve
microlamps (1,5V, no LEDs) on front and rear are soldered to the motorcontacts, so they light up when driving
