  • Quote

Post by kvik369 » Fri Jan 13, 2006 8:02 am

Don't know if anyone has seen these at Wally World, but I had to grab one. They're 1/20 scale propo four whell drive rc's. Mini Crushers from Zap toys. Price was $29.99

Don't have any pics yet but here's a link to the company.
http://www.zaptoy.com/product/MiniCrusher/main.htm



Only had time to try it for a moment. Uses 4 double a batts and one 9v for controller. Could use more power. A little slow. But its a crawler not a racer. On the box it shows that there's upgrades available. Shocks, motors, bearings and tires. But of course there were none in the store. It may have seven steps in the steering (not sure). Have to try it on the gravel later. It's slower than the SuperSlick but its propo and 4-wheel and for ten dollars more, possibly a better modifying option. They also had 5 different type bodies in the store. Mounts pretty much like a MZM.

Well talk is cheap so I'll try to get some pics up, but I might not be able to until next week
