Check it fella's been workin' on this suckah for a while now.

Kinda parted my ways with the mini stuff, got back to my roots with the Tamiya's and their 1/10th scale chassis.

I have been working on this TB-02 for about a year, and been swapping bodies back and forth trying to figure out which one is going to be the one to capture many eyes at the track.

Well after the Carrera and now this, I still don't want to take my chances and actually run these bodies for demolition lol!

Basically with this car it's simply the fact that I fell in love with Lamborghini's and I am actually going to start my Murcielago pretty soon. I wanted this body to represent the chassis well. The whole concept of great handling, tight turns, and the width as well. Black to me was best suited because it clearly demonstrates how bad ass those Lambo's can actually be, and with the chrome rims and bluish lug nuts, a little bit of pimpin actually went a long way.

On a side note for some detials, if you notice the spoiler and some parts of the body skirts are customed. The spoiler is actually the Carrera GT Tamiya spoiler, I had an extra one layin around and thought.....why not?

Well Pimps, enjoy!
