Ok the update ya'll have been waiting for "The Bathrooms"
I built the instide of the bathrooms I have small tile walls and large tile floors all white (for now) soon to be vary dirty!

Here are the toilets notice the toothpick thats the scale they fit the 1/64 homies perfect. I even hav the lids on the toilets I have yet to paint them and paint the lids black. The sinks are packaging material and will have faucits and drains they are wall mounted.


Here are the bathrooms you should be able to make out the tile walls and the tile floor. I am going to higlight the tile better and add lots of things to these bathrooms.



Here are the windows and doors I built frames and then added the clear polly so that when the painting is all done I can just put the windows in place. they are not attached in this picture just put in so ya'll can see what they will look like. (the door in the picture is actualy upside down) DOH!


Here are the windows and the small ones are the transome windows that go above the door they will be sitting partialy out if the frame like vents.


thats it for now more to come I am working on the base and the chainlink fence. I got the light and will even have a lighted coke machine.
