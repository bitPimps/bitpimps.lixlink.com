And... OK, here it is... For any xmod owner who is planning on getting a Woah Nelly... You have to ditch the chassis heat sink that is in there to help cool the stock fets... with Nelly, it is just dead weight anyways... With that gone, you have enough clearance to run the wires out from under there... Be sure to route the ribbon wires(the control logic wires that solder to the stock fet pads) away from the switch and out towards the rear... You will also need to make some sort of a spacer for the switch(hence the name... switch gasket/spacer thinger lol). Without that chassis heatsink in there, it is left loose with enough play to flop around... I used a folded piece of cake mix box on 219s, with a little rectangular switch hole cut through it with an exacto... When hog called and asked, I advised him to use a match pack, folded and cut...
