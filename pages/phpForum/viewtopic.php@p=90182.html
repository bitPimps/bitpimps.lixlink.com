Post by HirotoR34 » Mon Feb 27, 2006 12:08 am

I'll start off by saying, I've got a lot of shit....container after container of old bit, ZZ, and other assorted parts just sitttin around collecting dust. I figured since I can't really afford to buy new rc's, I'll make em out of the stuff I already have.
Now since I've done many bit modifications, I wanted to play around with Zip Zaps more (MT's by far my favorite) and mod some shells. I stopped by RatShack the other day and picked up a Corvette past and present set for two fifty, and then a Mustang past and present set for about 35 cents less (not sure about the logic in that but oh well...)
Being the owner of very few ZZ chassis meant that I would have to make my own. Of course being a pimp, I knew where to go for the info on a chassis extender and found one example from Hue.....
This project is far from done, but I'll keep posting updates as I go along.



clone chassis chopped to shit
zip zap packaging plastic
dual MT batt pack
bit pcb and steering wires
Super Slicks steering return spring
ZZ springs
Super blingin rims

More to come....... [/img]
