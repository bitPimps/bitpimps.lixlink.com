sm2osley, this is one of the best and hardest mods to do.

Check the following links for info:

Aussie implementation

Original German version

Mod Results

More info with video results

Also, check Weird Science shortly.....

Unfortunately there is no very in-depth tutorial for this mod. The German page has the info that you need but the steps are hard to follow. Most people that have done this and posted info have done it on a higher, more results driven level. Another issue is the fact that the FET's aren't cheap and only have limited suppliers.

You ned tobe very good at soldering and have lots of patience. Many people have fried their PCB's doing so......
