Post by SPORTCAR » Wed Dec 31, 2003 8:02 am

I've got an SE on its way to MicroAmps, when it gets back, it gets a king 4.2, 3.5g Lithium polymer battery (3.7V), lightened, the steering improvement mod (did this with my other SE: it makes a HUGE difference), lowered body (as much as possible: still need to figure out how to locate the LiPoly cell), Bit Sprinter 6mm wide aluminum rims, graphite dust lubrication, and the X2 chrome body (internally hollowed). I'm also looking at making a bronze bushing for the drive axel to prevent plastic from melting and to keep the floppy rear axel perfectly against the gears.

Lastly, I'm putting in my red gear ball differential. More on that:
http://tinyrc.com/forums/showthread.php ... post152364

For the charging connection, A HO train 4 prong female connector mounted in the rear window: 2 pins for charging the Li Po battery, an extra as the "on", an extra as the guitar E-string antenna connection.

How the connector will work:
You'd plug in the "antenna connector" and it will be the resonant length (1/32 wavelength = 6.7" @27.5 MHz) antenna attached to a male 4 pin plug. A second pin will be the connection between ground and "on".
With the antenna connector out, the battery is disconnected so I'll be sure to be shutting down when I put the car into its case (that's too short for the antenna without folding it up...). Also, with the antenna out, you'll now be able to plug the peak charger in.

Yeah. Like that.
