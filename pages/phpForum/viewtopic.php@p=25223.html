The biggest changes I've noticed that driving outside on concrete is more feasible now.

Before I would drive and the car would go all over the place. Then I'd hit the crack in between the concrete slabs and whamooo! Car flips and it's all over.....

Taking out the sway bar and replacing with springs actually gives you independent font end suspension which is quite cool(sad).

I now find that the car handles the rough and tumbles of the concrete a lot more in its stride than before. I won't say that it's perfect, but it's a lot better than before.

I mean sheesh, when you hit a "hole" that is 150% your wheel diameter then your buggered now matter what you do.

The perils of 1/64, ahh well......
