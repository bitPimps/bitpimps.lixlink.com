Well guys...Not got around to reviewing the Bonzermat pro yet, not 'cos I'm a lazy arse but because I've been working on this....

Started out with one of the 2 TVR bodies that are in the Bonzer racing TVR set as so......


Took off the rear spoiler and tab lowered the body. Junked the wheels in favor of some rims from Hot Wheels Accelerators (The hard plastic tyres needed to be removed carefully from the rims and the rims were then narrowed...a right git of a job the took about 1 hour a wheel but it was worth it).

Real TVR's have front projector headlights so I couldn't resist putting in 4 LED lights to complete the look. The multiple rear lights were drilled out to emphasise them and painted on the inside of the body.


I originally wanted to use mirage colour change spray for the body but couldn't find it in the UK so settled for a pearlescent purple instead which I think suits the lines well

Well I hoped you like that and I hope my pimping is improving (still a way to go before I reach the dizzy heights of you pro's out there)
