Post by crazydave » Sun Jan 23, 2005 4:13 pm

Got the Mini-X Beetle lookin' body from TinyRC. It works out ok, except it is cheap polycarbonate, only thicker than the original Iwaver body, but it seems pretty durable. Another draw back is the holes for the body post and antenna are pre-drilled, too bad since I wont be using them. It came with some window mask made with cheap sticker paper. I tried them for the heck of it, and as expected got paint bleeds on the from windshield where it had to flex. It'd be a good Idea to just use them as template formaking your mask out of tape. Overall I'd say it was worth $4.

Since I was picking up some Pactra paints, I had to get the CheZoom Teal, since it was there. You know, that's a Boyd Coddington color. I tried to trim it to look as much as possible like a baja bug, cause you guys know I like baja bugs.
I think it came out pretty good. Too bad I got a Tahoe body showing up in the next day or two.
