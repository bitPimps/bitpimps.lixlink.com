Got most of the front end done. Frizzen called it when he said it would be a PITA to attach a pinion to the planetary reduction motors I had planned on using for steering. The reduction was 20:1 on the motor x whatever the stock reduction was. My hydrosteer mod puts the reduction at @200:1 if I remember correctly so I'm sticking with that for now.

Stock cover plate for steering assy.


Trimmed bottom of motor slot flush and reduced nubs where pins fit by about 1/2 top and bottom.


Shaved down 3 gears for steering. Remove material to thickness of gear and shave backside of gear flush as well. Basically just making as much room as possible to fit 2 extra gears w/o compromising gear mesh area.


Pic with stock gear for comparison.


Gears added ready to be installed.



Back together again.
