I bought one of those Super Slicks Monster Trucks today and well I like the thing! Immediately I took it out of the package and started doing some mods on the thing. I got rid of the stupid wing on the back, removed the decals, took off the tires and got some real monster tires on it then to finish it off added the racing stripes.

I like the way it handles and it is not really as top heavy as it looks in fact it took me some time to dump it over. This little monster rocks!
