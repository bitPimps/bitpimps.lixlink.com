Post by LBRC » Tue Aug 09, 2005 10:27 am

SZ wiring
No problem simplicity itself. Just exactly like your epoch; one wire to each tab on the potentiometer: Center to PW on the circuit board, Right (on the SZ) to PV and left to PG. S1 & S2 to the servo motor.

Mosfets
The Epoch does not use mosfets it has a motor driver IC, which is why the external turbo, 7317’s work great for one.

I have a love hate thing going with the Epoch TX, don’t like it but find myself using one more and more for the modified cars on the track. It’s the silly high low trigger tension switch, worthless for a stock car but the tension of the low setting makes it easier to keep the modified cars on the track.
