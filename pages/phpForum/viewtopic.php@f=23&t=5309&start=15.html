All jokes aside, it's been pretty interesting. We've been shot at with mortars, rockets and small arms on a regular basis. Casualties are limited, but no deaths as of yet. A few of the casualties are pretty bad, but I'll keep it light and mention some of the funny things.

We were on patrol about 5 days ago when an insurgence popped around a corner and shot our point man from about 10 feet away with an AK. The funny thing is we have these armor plates in our flack vests. The Marine was hit in the gut, but the bullet was stopped by the armor. It looked kinda funny really. When he got hit, all that happened was it pushed him back 2 steps, then he unloaded on the guy with his SAW (light machinegun). The SAW is one of the largest machineguns that is man portable. It's a pretty impressive weapon.

Speaking of armor working. One night our outpost that we were staying at was attacked. One guy got hit twice in his helmet by 7.62 rounds. None penetrated. The same night a rocket bounced off his side and detonated about 10 feet away. Still he's OK. I tell you, this guy must have an angel watching over him. Nowadays, he stays pretty low. He's being more cautious. We gave him a new nickname; Bullet Sponge.
