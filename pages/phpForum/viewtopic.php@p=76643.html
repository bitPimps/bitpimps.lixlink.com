midget_man1387 wrote:Is that a motorworks tank? I've got one and its fun except the treads on it are too large so it doesn't turn because the tread stays and the wheels spin...
I had that same problem. I cut the tread, and removed about an 1/8th inch. Then I superglued them back together. Superglue bonds superbly to rubber.

They were a little too tight after that, I had to stretch them out until they were perfect.

That's ironic ynad, I took picture of my modded tank at night too. It was because I wanted to take them outside, but it was night, and I couldn't wait, but I thought it made a cool spacy effect. Brought out the red clay in our soil, and made it look like Mars.

I hopped mine up with FETs and a SE dual cell. Pretty damn quick, for a tank and all. With a Microspeed stunt car controller, you can spin the treads opposite directions, and do nutty fast 360s. I'd make ya'll a cool ass vid of it, but I lost my idler gears when it was apart, somehow.

I gotta get another one of those.
