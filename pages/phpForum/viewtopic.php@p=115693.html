Post by z-beam » Thu Jan 03, 2008 12:48 am

i got a grasshoppr sent to me from osaka.

drove nicely, the suspension is great and could really get over a lot of obsticles.
the clearance on them is awesome for a stock micro car and the motor is pretty beefy.
pity the rechargable battery in it died in less than a week

it never got air. it was considerably underpowered for its size and weight. knowing how much punch the micro helicopters have in thier tiny lipos the micro buggies could have been godly but instead they are kinda failiures.

mine could have just been a dud and i will probably buy another to make sure im not all wrong. they sure do have a lot of potential or modding (lipos as mentioned, a propo board and steering) but then again the costof those mods could buy a micro-t easy.

good thing the motor is compatible with q-steers, whack that blue ended beast in with some bit high speed gears (just the middle one not the axle) and youve got a seriously fast q-steer.

if you have a track to race on give the q-steers a go. ive had one out of 9 be a dud out of the box (bad steering) and the one and only buggy was a stinker.
