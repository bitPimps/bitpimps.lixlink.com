Don't fret you didn't miss much. As far as I'm concerned LXX had the worst QC out there. They just looked the Most similar to Bit Char-G. As far as Bit Char-Gs go, other than the alternate frequencies, and some body styles, Microsizers are virtually the same car, but I think the Bit Char-G had more issues. Some runs had horrible range. My Skyline set had the crip cap on both the car and Tx, range was much better after I removed them. I found some of the earlier runs had kinda hard tires, and most of my Bit Char-Gs have had to have the steering spring tweaked to get the car to track straight within the range of adjustment provided.


I've had good luck with Microsizers though (except for later runs of Pros and Sports from TRU that had uneven steering due to one arm being a mm shorter than the other.)

But now that I really think about it I had the most fun and spent the most time with my tricell ZZ that I called FrankenZap. I mean, shit, I named it... When you're giving cars nick names, its gotta be love.
