Post by Clint » Mon Jul 17, 2006 3:04 am

FYI. The brushless motor wont work with the novak spy, but you can use the spy for 1:28th scale... (would be fun on the hummer *wink* *wink* )

If you are going mamba 6800.. get it here with the ESC combo. best price around. http://www.b-p-p.com you may also want the usb-link. CC has file updates for the mamba every so often.. they make a big difference.

HS 81mg works well, That's what i'm using. When it breaks, i have an hs 85mg ready to drop in. Make sure you have a servo saver on it.

If you stick with nimh, you need a gp1100 based battery pack with the mamba.. better is to go lipo. 6800 is pretty sick on a 3s lipo.

Mamba is 20mm in diameter, so there are many heatsinks out there for it.
ebay, tower, rcmart..etc

Also you may want an aluminum motor mount. the atomic aluminum motor mount/heat sink combo is nice since you can screw a mini fan on it
for even more cooling.
