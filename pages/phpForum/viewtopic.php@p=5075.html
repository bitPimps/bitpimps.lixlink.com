DDS Data Tapes are tha bomb!

  • Quote

Post by ph2t » Thu Apr 17, 2003 8:42 am

Fellas, I had a rare brainstorm at work and raided some storage cupboards and found some really old DDS tapes(4GB to 8GB sized tapes used to backup data from servers). At first I was thinking that maybe I could pull apart some floppies and use the spring from them. But then I thought, why not try the DDS tapes. And would you believe it the springs in these mofo's are perfect!

So here's what you need to do, first source a tape!



Then pull it apart and look for two springs sticking out of the plastic backing. Refer to where I have the needle nose pliers pointing for the springs, you can see them but they're a bit fuzzy.



Below shows the springs removed from the DDS tape unit and placed next to a clone wheel so you can see the size of these. A little bit long but otherwise purfect!



One third along the length of the spring the coil changes and becomes compressed (as you can see in the previous pic). I cut this portion off since it's too long anyways...... Below shows the springs cut to a good size.



Obviously you need to remove the cover of the steering assembly to be able to put the springs on. Notice the clearance given by the springs to the pivot to allow the cover to sit back on without overcompressing the spring and therefore inhibiting steering ability.



And your done..................



So all you pimps who work in IT get those DDS tapes now before they disappear!
