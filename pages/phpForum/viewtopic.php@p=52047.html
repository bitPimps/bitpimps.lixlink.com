I bought this set too. I like the added blue LEDs in the circuit for underglow. The bodies suck major however. They are HUGE compared to other Zips and look out of place sittin next to them. The bodies are also heavy as a brick. Not good on an already weighed down SE. And they do sit high enough on the chassis to look just plain silly. The underglow is a cool feature added to the existing SE light harness though. I ripped the lights and light pipes out for other projects and threw the bodies into the trash. And the stickers suck too. It would take lotsa clear to cover those clear tape pieces-o-shite. Water slides would've been much better.
If it ain't broke, tear it apart and see what makes it tick!
