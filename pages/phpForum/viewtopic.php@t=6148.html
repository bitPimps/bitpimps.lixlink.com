I saw this Ferrari that was moded by Miva Well many here thought the car was awesome and well Miva disappeared before the car was finished. I happened to get the same body off of eBay. I really wanted to see what Miva’s Ferrari would look like finished so I started modding it based on his work.

I have cut out most of what needs to be cut away and sanded down the body for the first sand.

Now I am going to attach the new side panels and cut out the top and sides letting the windows down (like the are rolled down part way) I will also have to drop the body with a tab lowering job (like Miva’s) once I get to the point Miva left off looks like I will have to wing it.

Will see……..
