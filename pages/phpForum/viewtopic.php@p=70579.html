No not the last one I am going to make but this is one I call “Last Ride” its going to be a scratch built Hot Rod Hearse. I started with an idea and moved to putting it into effect.

I drew it out using the chasse as a model then cut it out and put it on the plastic I am going to use. Next cut that out (both sides) and then I taped it to the chasse to see what I have so far.


This one is going to be a lot harder then the Hot Rod Model A but I think the ride will look real cool when done. Especially since it will hold a miniature casket and have a back door that opens to push it in an out off!! I will post more pics as I go along…

Ok well did not have a lot of time this weekend to do much but I did get a little done. The roof, back and the sides are on. And I test fitted it to the chasse several times but still have to make adjustments.

This is made out of Plastic stock about 1/32" or less I also have some real thin stock I will use too mostly evergreen styrene you can get at any hobby store.

I was going to try the hearse in monster garage but it was a 50's model and much more rounded (kinda bubble like) and that is vary hard with sheet styrene so I am going for more the late 60's more strait lines.

Morbid oh man I hope not I am not into Goth or anything but I like the unusual and this is defiantly that! I hope to make it more a Hotrod then an actual Hearse but I like the Hearse Idea because well its not been done yet!
