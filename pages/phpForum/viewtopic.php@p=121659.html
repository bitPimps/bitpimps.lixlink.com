I'm in St Augustine, about an hour south of Jacksonville. Pics are from Matanzas inlet, about 1/2 hr. south of me. Sunny Acres is merely a state of mind... albeit a slightly warped state of mind, there's a lot of retired folks around here.

Murdoch wrote:hey... do I see a lizard down near the bottom here?
No lizard, just a rock. There are a shit ton of them in the area but I've never really seen them out on these rocks probably because they're below the tide line.
