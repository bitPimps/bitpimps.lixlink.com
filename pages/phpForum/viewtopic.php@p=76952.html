Trying my hand at some flames for my mini t

  • Quote

Post by Sporttracguy » Tue Aug 09, 2005 7:09 pm

well a month or so i picked up a mini T on ebay for cheap, it was compleatly stock and sort of fun but slow. Recently i upped the power to a gp1100 7 cell (8.2volt) pack with a big block motor, speed bearings, and some street tires. This baby flies but the used beat up crowd pleaser body was something to be desired so i picked up this EXT body and today got some free time to try my painting skills. I must say painting from the inside is a hell of alot harder than from the outside but the end result turned out pretty nice. What do you think?

For the flames i just used masking tape and an xacto knife, taped her off and painted,, then i removed the tape and painted the whole inside metalic red,, i left the headlights clear behind the semi clear stickers so i can put some LEDs in there later

Ill get some shots of her on the T as soon as the paint cures, its still a little tacky at the moment.
