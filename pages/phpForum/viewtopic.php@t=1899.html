ok first night i: took the damn supercharger apart and glued just the blower to the hood and i took it apart and put springs in it. man are these front ends tricky to put back together. there is less verticle travel so the springs are really stiff. after getting rid of the front weight and making the suspension l/r independant it drove straighter than any car ive ever driven. just by doing that i guarantee it would beat any bit on a technical track.

last night: i cut out the rear window and installed my handy dandy second cell. and let me tell you this is the most power output ive ever seen on anything this scale. it would actually skwawk the tires in fwd!!! it even drove straight. having a physical steering system is so much more reliable than an electromagnet.

and again this is the only micro rc where adjusting the trim actually makes a difference.
