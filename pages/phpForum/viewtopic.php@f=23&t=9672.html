I was so impressed with the pictures of DT’s Scion XB that I dug up an XB body that I had purchased from Biohazard and got it to look sorta like DT’s ride.

I thought it would be easier then it was but I did not realize how many coats of paint were on that body and when I went to paint it black it started orange pealing on me!!!

So out with the break fluid and striping 4 yes 4 coats of paint from the body. Then the break fluid messed up the putty job that was on it so I had to re-do that also. But I got it re-done and painted up and finished the detail work this morning at 3:00AM!

So here are some pictures of DT’s new ride that will be sent out to him this week! Sorry the pictures are so bad man but it is 3:00 in the morning and well ya’ll will just have to forgive me.

I hope you like the ride DT I will try and have it in the mail to you by the end of the week! Oh and this is a surprise ride DT did not know I was working on it. So shhhhhh don’t tell!





Ok now back to work on my end of the year ride........
