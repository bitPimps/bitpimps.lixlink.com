Dave, you have a neighbor who's a racer, ask him if he'd mind doing some mild welding. Get a piece of 1/4 - 1/2 steel plate cut into about a 18 inch square then have him take a piece of tubing like he has for his roll cage, most likely it will be 1 1/2” O.D. x .095 steel tubing, cut it to about 4-5 ft length and weld it in the center of the plate, you'll have a cheap tamper that you can use for years. Oh, and you might want to give it a quick coat of paint.
