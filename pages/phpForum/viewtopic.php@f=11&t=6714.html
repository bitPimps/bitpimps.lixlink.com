Post by MotorGong » Wed Sep 14, 2005 2:14 pm

Get both!!
When I picked up my first Bit back in April/May, I got a 2-Speed AutoModellista set from Fred Meyer.
The 2-Speed feature wasn't the only attraction for me, but what a very cool feature indeed!

Personally, I like the 2-Speeds better.

Where are you racing?
A small track (kitchen table, =< 4'x4', etc.) I would go with a 2-Speed.

A large track (open floor, =>8'x4', etc.) I would go with either. On the 8'x4' oval track I have, we're almost at top speed the whole time when we race.

I am a little bummed about how the Tomy micro 'B' motors perform in the 2-Speeds without the turbo boost on. I have much more performance with non-tomy brand motors. But this talk might be for after you get some Bits.

First, get a Bit, then start digging in to this wealth of information that is at your disposal here at BitPimps.

Get the pro and do [this mod] to it. It's the Anti-boost mod, it inverses the function of the boost button. So that when you press it, the car slows down . You will have full throttle all the time and have about half when you press the "brake".

This mod gives the best out of any 2-speed bit. After that, mod the bit to the maxx and you can still drive it at a controllable speed
