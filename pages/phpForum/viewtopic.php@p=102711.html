well tonight i started working on one of these buggers and i must say they have alot of potential. I removed the charge plug from the floor and stuffed two cells in there. I then mounted a yellow zzmt motor and geared it down a bit(only slightly, i used a blue gear but i may try and mount a zzmt gear in there instead) Then i put another steering spring in there to tighten the steering up and installed a solid antenna. Then i ground down alot of the useless plastic on the underside and mounted the charger on the inside of the back window with a small hole through the back window to stick the charger plug through.

Next im going to try and cut the truck into 3 pieces, (front, middle, and rear) and make a custom overland style suspension for this(just as soon as i find some really soft springs)

until then this thing spanks the stock truck, it has lots of torque for climbing and good all around speed as well,,,im quite happy with how its turning out.

also, because of the extra cell i had to modify the charger/remote. I took it apart and wired in another AA cell that i mounted on the back of the charger and it works great.. ill try and get some pics a little later
