Maybe you're getting closer to the car than your camera can handle. Notice that the blinds and the lamp in the background are what your camera is focused on... that might be your minimum distance for shooting. If you have a macro mode (usually has a little flower icon) make sure you are using it. If not, just back up until the car is in sharp focus, you can always crop the shot later to get the car bigger in the frame.
