I was hoping to get a couple of decent pics of my "bird effect" convertible project to update the thread with the shaping I have done to the front end since I posted the pics of it originally... but, it wasn't meant to be. The pics came out terrible.
So, while we were messing with lighting and distance trying to get better pics, I threw my bedtime budget beater Bug on the table and snapped a few... and whaddayaknow, they actually came out half decent.
There is absolutely nothing special about this car, other than it's the car that I drive around on my bed at night while I have my bedtime cig on the nights that my missus is still up chatting with her soap opera friends.
It is a Thundercharger, totally stock, with a ms VW Beetle body that I got at Target for like $2 a few months back, gave to a neighborhood kid to have something nice on his clone, and then bought it back(got a bunch of Homies and a whooped clone chassis in the deal, too) after he beat it up a bit and markered the windows... I threw it on the chassis, slapped a few stickers on it, and now it's one of my favorite beater cars.





Definately nothing special, but a damn fun little beater none the less.
