I thought I already posted this, but I guess I just hit preview or something, oh well, anyways...

Right after I get the FETs in, my Countach body disappears, can't find it anywhere. I blame the kitten my wife brought home.

This car is kickin' butt so I need a bad ass body for it. I run down to Target to see what I can find. It came down between the Supra, or 350Z, I decided the the Supra looked just a little more 'bad ass', but the Orange paint job had to go. So I went to work on the clear body, and here's what i came up with;

Medium charcoal flames faded in over Charcoal, for a stealthy effect.






It reminds me of the one I called 'Franken-Zap',


So I named it 'Lil Frankie G.

Ironically I realized after the fact that the PCB that I burned out the steering in the original Franken-Zap, ended being revived for this car. So I guess I subconsciuosly brought Frank back from the dead.

I digging it, but it aint no Countach. I want my Countach back.
