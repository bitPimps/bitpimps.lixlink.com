Ya know, the more I think about it posts like "I saw something on another board, so it must be true" are pretty worthless. I don't *know* that it works, I can't prove it.

So, I dropped $2 on 2-pack Handi-foil oven liners. They have wired edges, waffle texture, and mic about 0.005" I happen to have an Oz of ShoeGoo in my pitbox, a sharpened chopstick on my desk, and NO ACTIVITY on this board to distract me...





Rubbings done on an unmolested Nikko body, shoe-goo'd parts together, used the wired edges for structural in the A-pillars, above and below windshield, rear bumper, and rocker guards.

Ok there's a bit of a learning curve, and I don't have anything RC with a 7" wheelbase.
