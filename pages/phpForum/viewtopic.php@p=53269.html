So I got some FETs and a 2.4v SE battery in it, it's pretty fast and fun. Since I'm a lover not a fighter, I took the gun off. I'm just kidding, really I was just cutting weight. I don't need no gun, and I especially don't need no gun that don't work.

So then I filled the hole, and leveled the raised section that was under the turret. It looked pretty sleek and cool. I was thinking what I could do with it. I almost painted it white, and put a NASA logo on it, but then I thought, "If crazydave don't put a flame job on a tank, then who will? So I did it, I flamed out my tank.

I also detailed in all the little tools, gave it headlights, tailights, and side indicators, so it'd be street legal.







I call it a SUT (Sport Utility Tank).
