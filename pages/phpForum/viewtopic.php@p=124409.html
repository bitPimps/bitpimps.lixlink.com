A while back I started on a half track. These 1:1 conversions are reasonably popular in Japan during the winter months, which is what I based mine on.

I used a Super Slicks front end and a Takara tank. The main thing that surprised me was that it actually drove just like a car. If I moved the battery over the tracks, it would understeer, and when moved forward it would oversteer

Unfortunately the rubber tracks cracked after a few weeks so I never did finish it.
