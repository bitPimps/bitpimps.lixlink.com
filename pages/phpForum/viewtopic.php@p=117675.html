Post by BilboBaggins » Tue Jun 24, 2008 7:30 am

It definitely looks like a 24th scale slot car motor (found a simular design on a scratchbuilt slotcar site.) Fairly old one at that, I never ran one like it.

The 65 might refer to the nmber of turns on the arm, the more popular arms have 50 turns. It might be good if used on a tire truer lathe.

Have you put power to it? Slotcars run on tracks running between 12 and 14 volts but will start turning at 1.5-2v. Before you put power to it maybe clean it (spray electonics cleaner will work) and reoil the bushing/bearings.
