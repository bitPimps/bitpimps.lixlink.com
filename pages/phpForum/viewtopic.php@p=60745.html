I grabbed them off of a West Coast Customs Die-cast.

Yes, I agree that the wheels are a bit small with the body, but I really wanted to use them on this so I did. I lowered the tabs about as far as I can so that it wouldn't look like an SE. I figured if I lowered the body with those small wheels, it would almost look stock.

As far as the body needing some V-8 juice to straighten it out...I think I just put it on badly. I didn't notice it till I looked at the pics and really didn't feel like re-shooting.
