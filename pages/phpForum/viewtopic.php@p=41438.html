your fortune is: you will die a terrible death.
your gear ratio is: 8.4488:1.

ratio is easy to figure out;

count the teeth on the gears of the first 'gear down', in your case;
18, 7. divide them to calculate the ratio.
18 / 7 = 2.5714.

do the same with the other 2 gear wheels.

23 / 7 = 3.2857.

multiply these two figures to find the ratio :1

2.5714 x 3.2857 = 8.4488
= 8.4488:1that just refers to revolutions, 1 revolution of the rear wheel = 8.4488 revs of the motor. different wheel diameters make a difference overall to the final speed and torque.
