technically, the closer to 1:1 you get the faster you would be, one revolution of the motor would equal one revolution of the tire.( hence a 4.2 motor turns 42,000 revolutions per minute[rpm] and at 1:1 the tire would also have 42,000 rpm.)But realistically, you lose alot of torque and the car actually wouldnt be able to really move i dont think cause of all the torque loss. Higher gear ratios 12:1 for example are slower top speed wise but will give the car greater torque.
