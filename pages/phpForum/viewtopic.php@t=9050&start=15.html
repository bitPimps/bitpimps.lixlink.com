Post by dogbreathracing » Wed Mar 28, 2007 9:37 pm

Yeah I guess my store was trying to make floor space to lower the price to that level....but I feel they are still a good deal at $99....most of us already have chargers and even if we don't there are plenty of options that are cheaper than the rat shack priced batt and charger.

The RS optional parts are a bit steep but then again there are plenty of other options there as well....the only thing I have found that will NOT fit the PP is the RC18 shock towers... they will not bolt to the PP gear covers, you would have to change the covers as well to use RC 18 towers. RS does have the PP towers (uses the same front and rear) for $10 (includes two towers, body mounts, and hardware)
The RC18 arms will fit and the RC18 arms are much cheaper than the PP replacements.
RC18 and Mini-T rear wheels fit just fine so you're not stuck with the over priced RS parts there

One thing I did notice as I worked on the car is that the plastic is not plastic but a nice nylon, less brittle than toy grade plastic and much tougher.

My Pulse with Mini-T chrome disc wheels and Mini-Smasher tires....Truggy-licious
