I bought one a few days ago. Its pretty fun!! I don't really think this car needs a whole lenghty reveiw, but I'll give the basics.

I bought the Red Mazda RX-8

-Fully Loaded Super Slick
-6 different freqs. 3@27mhz and 3@49mhz
-Lights, front and back. They do not shine thru the body that I have.
-Simulated Drift function. Turn the body around, flip the swith on the controller and it steers from the rear and is front wheel drive. Lights still work properly.
-Conveinent Turbo button.
-Supposed to be Quad cell. Not sure, I haven't opened it up yet. I think it is because they upgraded the charger to 4 AAA's from 3 AAA's.



New, left --- Old, right

Notice the different placing of the motor and front steering box. Both have moved to the extremes.

Different contact patterns on board covers. Allows the body to be turned around and the lights to continue to work.

Bodies are interchangable between old and new.

I was way curious about these after SG's nice little review. So after work I stop by ToysRus to check out the new SS's without any intention of buying. First impression....cool packaging, some of the shells are pretty nice including the RX8 that SG picked up. I'm thinking meh, I can pass for now and wait to see what comes out in the near future.

Then...as I'm turning...out of the corner of my eye, I catch a flash of blue and gold from way back on the SS shelves...I hesitate and think Ralley WRX? Nah, haven't seen any mention of them for the SS. Sure they had the WRX for the orginal version, but not the ralley car. So I reach way back and sure enough I pull out a Fully Loaded WRX Ralley Car. How the hell could not buy that...staring back at me all shiney and purty like.

Interestingly enough. They actually have two versions of the WRX Ralley Car....one for each frequency. The differences are minor, but one is number 8 (27 MHz) and the other number 7 (49MHz), as well as a few minor differences in sponsor decals. These appear to be nice replicas of WRX's that ran races in two different parts of Europe. Very cool....and I was wishing they sold a body kit for the other shell I didn't get with my starter kit.

Anyway, SG and Bird were right on....these things are real fast right out of the box and really fun too. I like the controller/charger setup. The chasis seems to have lots of extra contact points other than those used for the lights...suggesting other features to be added in the future? Maybe, but I'm guessing its to make sure other future shells don't have problems with the position of the headlight and tail light contacts.
