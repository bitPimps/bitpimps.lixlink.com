Post by DarkTari » Sun Sep 28, 2003 12:41 am

crazydave wrote:That is nice. It's got kinda of a cross between a hot rod, and a lowrider influence. Like a mix between performance and class.
Thanx Pimps!
Dave, that's exactly the look I was going for!
As I said earlier, this thing went thru so many changes while modding. I decided to chop the top to make it different from other bB mods so I took it a step further and extended the roofline to form a visor, one piece! Then I decided to put an L88 style hood scoop to massage in more muscle. The rear wing went through a lot. I didn't want to use one from another bit so I made it, formed it, changed it until I got the look I wanted. It turned out great.
How did you pull off the visor?


When I cut the back and removed, I cut the top off, chopped down the windshield & side windows. Then shorten'd the the side windows to extend the bed, slid the roof forward, fitted, shaped, glued, & puddy'd. That was one of the things that took so long. I had never worked with puddy before so it sat for a while til I got more experience modding. I'll post some indoor pics to show more of the detail, the sun kinda took away some with that color. Did the same thing with Deep Purple. Something about those Violet family colors ????
