Q. How much is a complete EVO car?

A. A complete Evolution Car kit right now it is running $159.85 including $13.97 S&H. If you know others that have any of the EVO Cars get them in and you could save $30 or more off that. Only 1 part (the Diff) is at 35% savings.

Q. When can we expect the parts to hit the States?

A. Based on the first GB delivery time is going to be around 2 1/2 months from the date the order is submitted.

Q. How do you submit the order?

A. The process goes like this.

1. Parts are requested.
2. I'll let you know how much they are a few days later.
3. When we get enough to save money I'll ask for you to PayPal me with a new total.
4. When I have enough in the PayPal to cover the order with all fees. I'll let GPM know what we want.
5. A new round will start on the requests.
6. GPM will tell me the total and I'll Wire them that total.
7. A week or more later GPM will let me know what is available and ask if I want those parts or wait. Longer for Diffs
8. A week later those parts would arrive with me.
9. If you are in the USA you will receive those in 5-7 days after I send them out.
10. Repeat 7-9 with a delay of at least 1-2 weeks between parts from GPM.
