Post by bdebde » Sun Nov 02, 2003 4:23 pm

I'm with payaso on the size thing. I wanted a micro that ran like my 1/10th cars. The fetted SE does that, it is by far the best micro rc now. I won't be buying any xmods, I don't have the space for good racing with those, and I have a couple of 1/10th cars that I can run outside.

Superfly,
I have fetted bits, ms, zz, and now an SE. The fetted SE has a little less torque than the others, but it is faster. The torque with red gears is still better than stock with blue gears! The lower torque may be due to pulsed signal, or more weight, but I think it has more to do with the smaller cells in the SE. I tried running a fetted ms board with some smaller cells (about the same as SE's) from a nakamichi racer, and the torque was lacking with that set up too. Max discharge rate for smaller cells is 210ma while the larger bit/old zz cell is 360ma (did some research).

Cabo,
Yes, a chunk of the cost of the mod goes into shipping back to you (from micro_Amps). The speed of the SE with mosfets is nearly that of a dual cell (it is dual cell, just smaller cells)

Voodoo,
If charging with AAA's you won't get very good results after just a couple of charges. They run down way too fast. I get 10 min. run time with 2 - 3 charges in my fet modded one with green gears and purple NX motor, using my AC adapter, here:

http://tinyrc.com/forums/showthread.php ... adid=13862

In conclusion, I gotta say I love the SE fetted. In fact I sent a couple more to micro_Amps to have them done as well.
