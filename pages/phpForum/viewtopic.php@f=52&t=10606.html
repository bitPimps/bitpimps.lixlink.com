sidewinder wrote:So, how does it crawl? Stock vs modified? They do look like fun
Crawls decent out of the box. But really I just can't leave "decent" alone.


I put Micro-T Soft White springs on the back and used fuel tubing as a collor, it can slide up and down the shock body to adjust pre-load. I also made some lower links, that I plan to bend.

The lower links are made from Associated RC18 Link Ends, 2-56 All-Thread, and 1/8" aluminium tubing. I plan on bending them, and also I plan on putting on the Micro-T Shock shafts and bodies.
