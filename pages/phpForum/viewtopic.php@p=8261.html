Here's just a little sneak peek at a few products from www.extrememicros.com


I haven't yet had the time to extensively play and test any of the motors, though I will be this weekend.
I have broken in the motors and did 1 quicky with each motor and so far I can tell you the Vanquish Rotor motors seems to be what I'm most attracted to right now. I'll have more information and feedback on these puppies soon for you guys.

In the meantime - these motor tubes and the Fatty Tube are frickin awesome! I want a motor tube for each of my motors, I can't say enough about these motor tubes, they're just cool as all hell.
The wires are a must have - you can never have too much of this stuff. Perfect for dual cells, modding controllers, pcbs, etc. pretty much anything dealing with the wires on the bit.
