Thanx Pimps!
I started this to look slightly different, then I went surfing and found a
few pics of a real 360 Spyder so I had to change the mod a lil.
This is what I was going for:


Then I saw the roll bars and said fuck it, I ain't moddin this mug no mo!
I tried to make it as authentic as possible while adding a touch from
Team DarkTari (body kit). I did get the exhaust & wheel caps right!
I also altered the body kit to look more conformed, molded in so to speak.
There is a tuner called HaMann that does Ferrari after market tuning
so there are body kits for these things!
Oh and I like blood red interiors with silver.
Glad you Pimps like! Appreciate the Cudos!
