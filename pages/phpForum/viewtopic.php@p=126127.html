haha, I come back after a 3 year hiatus to see "Q Steer" rofl.
It's amusing to hear the asian announcers in the promotional videos say "Q Steer" lol.
Made me realize that there will never be an "L Steer" BAHAHAHAHA......ahem..sorry. anyway....

I made a recent micro-hunt excursion to try and catch up with the hobby.
I remembered a post by frizzen about Mario Kart Q-Steers at target, and was able to snag the only one
that branch had, Wario.

Ripped that shell off quickly lol. It's not that I don't like Mario stuff or whatever, I just want him on my
TV and not on the hood of my r/c car.

Pretty slow, even with the turbo boost. It has a white end motor. These motors are shorter in length than
the bit/microsizer motors, so no easy switch there without some sort of modding.
These being just that much smaller than a bit, the way it handles, and it's speed makes it a perfect
desktop racer. It creates sort of a "i don't have to clean up shit i can just play right here in front of my keyboard" type of atmosphere. Awesome!

Well this is cool. One can put in the green end motor from the Terrain Titan Buggies.
Same length motor, I just used the original motor pinion that Q-Steer comes with.
Better yet, those newer Air Hogs Terrain Titan Monster Trucks have a red end motor that's
compatible as well.
I put that sucker in, and lemme tell ya, this little Q-Steer came to life. Very punchy and fast but
still controllable with turbo engaged. The 'normal' speed is great as well, doesn't crawl.

I was enticed enough to order a couple Q-Steer cars from overseas.

EDIT: I think frizzen and some others recommended visiting the dollar store for the LR44 button cells.
Good advice and thank you, they have these 8 packs of 'em for 1$! The few I tried so far
seem fine. I saw One/single Energizer LR44 at Walmart for 4.14$ on sale, so, again, thank you lol.
