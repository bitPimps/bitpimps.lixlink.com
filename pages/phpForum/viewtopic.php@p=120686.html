I've noticed a lotta new Pimps askin about "Rare Bits"
1st lets get a few things straight. In the Micro RC Tomy world, Rare are the "Limited Editions".
Collectable are the "Special Editions". Now with that being said, I've re-comprised a list of
"Rare" & "Collectable" Bits so all your inquiries should be answered right here !!!
But remember "Rules" don't apply to all.......

This is what is considered the "Rare":

Limited Editions...

The "NUR" The fastest Bit ever. Only one to come with the "Lucifer" motor (3.0)


The bB Milk Wagon


White Box Honda S2K


The XBox Mitshubishi EVO


The XBox Subaru WRX STi


The Didney Resort Bus (Disney)


The Didney Steamboat (Disney)


The Voltz


The Calsonic


R34 GTR Silver / Gold Bodies


Honda S2K Silver / Gold Bodies


I don't think these were Limited Editions but are Very Rare !!!!

Buttank Blue


Buttank Camo


These are not considered "Rare" but are Very Collectable....

Special Editions......

The Lupin Set


Speed Racer Mach 5 GoGoGo


The TDF Pointer


The MAT Vehicle


The AE86 Sprinter


Skyline History Set


Skyline Black Set


Skyline Special Edition Sets


Auto Modellista Set


Initial D set


Mercedes AMG CLK55


Lamborghini Set


Ferrari Set


Now in my book, Rare & Collectable doesn't always pertain to Tomy's.
There are other Micro RC's that "I" consider Rare or Collectable.
Those that are promotional and/or were very hard to find:

Collectable / Rare Clones......

Nakamichi Hummer

I don't know of anyone that has seen never the less owned one, but They had to have been out there !

Scion xA / xB

There were a limited number of these made just for the US launch of the Scion xA & xB !

Honda Element

Presented to Honda dealers as part of sales information packet for the
2002 introduction of the then brand new 2003 Honda

UPS Truck
