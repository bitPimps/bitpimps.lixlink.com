Post by Pork_Hunt » Sat Mar 22, 2008 5:07 pm

You can solder another ZZMT cell "in parallel" to the original one, +ive to +ive, -ive to -ive. That will double your run time and put no extra strain on the electronics. Let it charge for about twice as long to "fill her up". You won't get any extra speed or torque, just longer run-time. Note that the ZZMT is already dual celled, so you would end up with a quad celled ZZMT...
