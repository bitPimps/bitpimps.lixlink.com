  • Quote

Post by crazydave » Wed May 12, 2004 8:18 pm

Man, I love this thing, so I just had to show it off.

There's not too many minuses on mine so I'll cover that first.

The only major defect on it is a warped axle gear, and crooked wheel. Despite this it's not too noisy, and runs straight. I always rebuild my rear axles anyways. My other bitch is that it was advertised 5 min. charge 20 min. runtime. In the package it says 3 min. charge 10 min. runtime. I hook it up, it charges for 45 secs. Nice guys. Aparently numbers mean nothing over there.


On the plus, these sit damn low, look cool as hell, have nice details, handles great, and are fairly fast out of the package. I actually made several laps, thinking it was ok, but not great, until I remembered it was a 2-speed. So I hit the button, and the bitch kicked in. I turned the boost fuction off, and it was all downhill. The clear chassis, and lights are cool.

I think after I fix the axle this will be my new favorite car.





I don't know why I can't take a decent picture of it though
