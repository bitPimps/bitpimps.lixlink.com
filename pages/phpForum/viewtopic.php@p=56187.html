Hey, MM, you're not alone in this observation. I have SE batteries in several cars and they do indeed occasionally makes a popping, crackling noise when I put them on the charger. Scaring me into thinking I damaged the battery, but they work fine.

I wouldn't worry about it too much. Those SEs batteries seem to be tough. I've one set that I've over charged, and dead shorted them, causeing voltage reversal 3 times, and they still snapped back.

It's normal for the batteries to expand and contract when charging, and that's probably all it is.
