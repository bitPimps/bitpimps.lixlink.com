The last you'll see of this Charger

  • Quote

Post by crazydave » Thu Nov 20, 2003 8:25 am

Ok, so I got this idea to build up this super modified car, and sell it on ebay. Just to see what people would pay for it.

I had stretched a Micro Speed chassis to fit a Zip Zap body. Painted the chassis. Dual celled, and FET modded it. Then painted a body to top it off. It was nearly ready, and I had only taken one pic. Then I went back to fine tune it, and make it perfect, when the body had fallen on the floor, and I stepped on it, not realizing it was down there.

So this is the only evidence I have of that body, and it's a shame too, because you can barely see the flames, and I had thought they had some of the best flow of any flames I've done.



After all the time I put into it, I decided to keep this car. It wears the Camaro body now, since I broke my last Charger.
