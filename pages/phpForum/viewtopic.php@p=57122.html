Well, I managed to get two cars from DT on the auction. Now I'll show them off.

First up is the EVO. All I did to this was give it a guitar string antenna. I didn't want to risk fucking it up.




The next up is the RX-7 Spider. Now on this one, I felt it needed more speed. I fet modded it and put in the ZZSE bats (dual 1/4aaa). I also switched the gears for kit 4:1 gears. runs sweat.


The brass bar is a brass tube around the axle to help stability. The regular axle runs inside it. Takes away were and tear concerns on the back axle. The clear stuff around it is glue from a hot glue gun. I use it to keep the tube from wiggling. Ball bearings are better, but this is much quicker.


A special thanx has to go out to DT. Also to Cabo and everybody else who made the auction a success. You guys should have one again someday.
