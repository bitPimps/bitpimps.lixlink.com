Post by crazydave » Sun Dec 03, 2006 1:11 pm

Both my cars had corrosion, but it wasn't like the usual battery leakage corrosion. It was just on the positive side. I took it off gently with a plastic brush, and the dremel, and the finish was eaten all the way off, and the metal was pitted. The corrosion went all the way up the wire to the PCB. I had to replace the wire and battery terminal because solder wouldn't stick to them. I cleaned up the end of the battery with sand paper, and it works great now.

That was just the Lambo, the Ferrari had some corrosion, but not as bad, I'll have to access the damage sometime when my kid is not around. Still a great deal for $10 though, I forgot how great these cars handle.
