*Panther Chariot(are these what you meant by "Panther" above?)
I think it might be the same Panther brand, but they're totally different cars.

Panther's were one of the better quality dome clones back in the day, some would say better than Bit Char-G. The Panther Chariot is Zip Zap sized, with a full size pistol grip controller and a rather crappy 3 step throttle.

You know, Cannonball's are Bensu, and I believe Rally Racers are Bensu. (Were Rally Racers mentioned yet?). Maybe we should work out subcategories for all the various Zip Zaps, and like Charg- N Go and Thunder Charger were made by Mega Toys as I'm sure a few others were. Oh yeah and Microspeed didn't see that on there. They had Microspeeds, then later Microspeed Extremes, that also included motorcycles and stunt cars. The Microspeed controller is identical to the Hot Wheels one, which makes me think there is some kind of connection.
