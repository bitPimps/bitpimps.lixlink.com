Well, I went to an LHS today that I hadn't been to in years, and saw something that caught my eye. I had seen this little thing on the self for a good 3-4 years, and always avioded it because it was 100 bucks or so and I felt a micro wasn't worth it. Today I went in, and noticed it with a new pricetag: 28 bucks. I figured I've spent more on junk 1:64s, so what the hell, at least it's a yokomo and probably decent.

So I decided to open it there and discovered a few nice things:

1. FM 27 Mhz WITH MODULE. Very surprising to me.
2. Fully propo.
3. Perfectly scaled.
4. (You're gonna love this) An actual rear diff in a 1:6X car!

What kinda sucks is that it has small wheels, which makes it useful for only desks, smooth, clean floors, and VERY low pile carpeting. It's definitely worth the 28 bucks.
