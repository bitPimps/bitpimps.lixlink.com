I was confused by your statement about leaving 2 batteries in while the 9v is running. What are the second set of terminals attached to? I'm nervous about digging into my only controller without some good advice. TIA.
(slight correction) The TX portion uses 3.6v-4.5v(3 cells), the
charger uses 7.2-9v(all 6 cells). The 3.6v terminal leads are hidden under
the circuit board. Look under the pcb to determine which 3 cell positions
run the tx. (I cant recall which 3 slots it was offhand)

So, if you just leave the 3 batteries in for the tx, and then disconnect the
leads that use all 6 cells, and re-attach them to the 9v adapter, you will
be all set to charge on the 9v adapter and fly on the 3.6v AA batteries.
