When dealing with nitro, it'a always hard to tell how the engines were treated when talking about "used" goods.

Both those rigs have great engines in em, and that's a plus.. The spare parts are also a +. Not much of a Losi fan these days, but I know their stuff keeps a decent resale value.

I'd say, and this is my opinion, but the T Maxx should be in the market of around $350, and the Losi, well, it's not my style, but if you could get it for around $150 - $175, you'd be doing a killer deal.



Did you ask how much fuel has been through the engines? People always say "Less than a quart on rebuild" lol

If you are comfortable with a simple rebuild, then jump on em. You'll love that Maxx truck. Your old lady, and neighbors won't, but you will...

OS, and sirio both make killer engines.

Cool, when you said that you trusted him, I had to take as good of a look at that pic as I could (hard to tell from 1 pic), but I noticed he's got some blue aftermarket shocks on it, and he lists the bulks just as "Traxxas Bulks" when they are the billet ones. Just those 2 items can cost 200 + to put on, and make building these trucks expensive, but nobody ever gets what they put into it out of it when reselling it. that's just the way it is. You can check on ebay to see all the used Maxxes for sale with a shit ton of hop ups selling in the 300 - 500 range, and these are $1000 trucks.
