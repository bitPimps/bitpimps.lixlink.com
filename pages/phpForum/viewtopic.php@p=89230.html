Well, I helped out the LHS owner a few days last week, including Saturday, where he had his busiest day ever, so today I walked in and he said, "I have a present for you. Take the mini inferno [MZM], and take a speedy05 motor since the one in there is shot. You've been helping out a lot this past week, so I'm hooking you up." He's had it for around a year, drove it twice, broke a body mount off, and hasn't driven it since (I drove it once like 2 weeks ago when I got curious and dug up some batts). The speedy motor in there is fun as hell, and the truck has nice bearings in it too. All in all, I don't think it's worth 140 (what they are new), but I'd pay 80 for a new one. Pic time:
