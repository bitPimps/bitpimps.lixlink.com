Not bad for your first paint job. Sounds like you have the right attitude. That's all any of us can do, is learn from our mistakes. I've made pul-lenty of mistakes.

I don't really reccomend using any pen for large coverage like the hood. I'd reccomend that you mask and spray something like that. Small details it's best to use a detail brush, and some paint, but you could get away with a permanent marker. Black Sharpies are kinda purplish, but I've found these ones called "Ink Tanks" that are nice and black, and if you use them when your clear coat is still fresh, they stay on really good.
