There have been some impressive xmods crawlers but they are like any other RC. The more time and money you put into them the better they work. That truck in the video looked pretty close to stock, I'm wondering how they did the gear reduction. One of the best things I've seen for these is swapping the drive motor for a servo motor...google: xmods, crawler, servo motor conversion...You should be able to find some helpful info.

Here's a basic build-up on the atomic mods site, its not the most over the top build I've seen. Just a nice little common sense build. http://www.atomicmods.com/Categories/Ar ... awler.aspx

BTW: If you can still find the trucks for $15 buy a couple and hold on to them for a while...they should seel for a good profit on ebay in a year or so. Unless they introduce new trucks in the future...I guess its a crap shoot.
