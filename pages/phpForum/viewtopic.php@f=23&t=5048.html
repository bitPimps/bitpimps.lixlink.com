I got some of those busses. I've been wanting to pimp one out. They're more just keep-sakes cause I rode on allot of those exact buses when I was in Japan. I think it's humorous that they call some of them “limousine-buses”. They’re just freakin buses. There's nothing limo about them, a little nicer than a public bus, but nothing special.

Anyways, I took the Kokusai bus and modded the chassis to accept a 3 volt lithium battery. I remember it was really fast, but I had to slow way down to turn, cause it would flip. Not so much because of the bus body, but because the 3 volt battery would only fit on top of the chassis and made it top heavy. That battery was 3/4 the size of the car. It was pretty cool, but I put it back the way it was cause I like to be able to turn. I've still been contemplating ideas for the buses though. There's oodles of room under that shell.
