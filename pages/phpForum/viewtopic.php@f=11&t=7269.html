It's not really Flossin' but it's a ZZ mod, so I'm putting it here instead; if it's wrong just yell at me.

While I was gone, I picked up a DB5. I was playing with it and remembered how much I hated the SE controller.




Overview: This steering wheel projects about 1/8" from the TX body, tread is about 3/8" wide, tread is fairly grippy rubber, OD of knob is basically same as stock. Just farther out and wider, I find this to be much more controllable. If I do anything else, it'll be extending the Pistol Grip.
