I've been flying the Jet and Bi plane for about 2 weeks now and have been having a BLAST.

Earlier in the week though i had got is stuck in a very thick tree back in the brush about 20 Ft. up, it took a water bottle tied to a kite string and about 2 hours to get it down last time.
