rccrawlking wrote:i am making another se servo crawler today
Why?

Honestly if you want a good crawler (since you already have a crawling suv) you should go on ebay and invest (I use the term loosely) in a ZZMT. Since you are going to be using an SE servo, which most people don't come by too often in numbers greater than 2, you might as well make it count. Buy the ZZMT, articulate it, do the servo mod, put some bigger tires on it and it'll be unstoppable. Whereas with these, as soon as you roll over a twig and go to back up over it you get stuck. Don't take this as an angry post, just some advice.
