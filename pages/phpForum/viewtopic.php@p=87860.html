I suppose it depends on what kind of modding you want to do and how much money you want to spend. I don't have an iW04, but I do have a SSMT.

If you have some money to invest, want to mod by being able to buy and install alloy or cf parts, have something that can go fast and be fixed when it breaks, don't mind 2WD, go for the iWaver.

If you're into scratch-building parts, swapping pcbs (if you want proportionality), either scratch-building or being SOL if something breaks, spending less money, want a 4x4 crawler or house runner, go for the SSMT.

Post by Sasquatch » Wed Jan 18, 2006 7:49 pm

The "old" Iwaver 04, the clone, is more of a crawler. Solid front and rear axles, decent articulation, a nice truck. The Firelap 04, the "new" one has independant front suspension and a solid rear. Not a lot of travel and articulation, more of an off-road monster truck style racer. It has a hard shell body, which adds weight and helps with roll-overs, but looks much better than the lexan bodies on the originals.

I'm not sure if they have the "old" 04's left in stock, so if you want the clone, you might have to find a used one somewhere. The Firelaps are pretty cool, decent speed and they like to do front flips.
