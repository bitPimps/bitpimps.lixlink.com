Post by canabits » Tue Apr 06, 2004 4:39 pm

Robert @ Radical Decals does some great generic and general purpose decals. Overall quality is excellent.

www.slotcarworld.com is a good US source for Patto's Place decals out of Australia. HO sheets typically go for about $7 but have enough to do 3-4 cars on average, sometimes more. Patto's has a very large selection of decals that are mostly unavailable in HO scale. The downside is like alot of 'printer' decals they sometimes lack the crispness you get with screen printed decals. If you are after the right 'look' they work pretty well, if you are after show stopping looks, you'd be better off looking elsewhere.

Looking for law enforcement decals? JBOT decals (www.jbot.ca) out of London, Ontario (Canada) offers an excellent variety of decals from different states/provinces in HO scale. Quality is excellent. They offer both 1/87 & 1/64 scale in addition to larger scales.

There are a number of other sources... Jays Race Place (www.jaysraceplace.com), Wizzard High performance (www.wizzardho.com), Scale Auto (www.scaleauto.com) all offer a variety of NASCAR decals (Scale Auto also has a couple good sheets of Can-Am based sponsor decals). Quality is excellent as these decals do not need to be cut from a single large sheet as they are screen printed on individual carrier films (like 1/25 scale model kits).

In addition, wizzard High Performance also carries a line of Beach Customs paint masks for HO cars. Among other things they have pre-cut flame masks to help make that next paint job a whole lot easier. Some samples are here http://www.wizzardho.com/BeachCustoms.htm
Sheets of the masks sell for $5US but have 4 complete sets on each sheet...and with a little care they can be used a few times before throwing them out.
