Betty and I managed to get down to the Templestowe track today.

Had a fair bit of fun. It was such a nice day we thought it would be busy down at the track but alas no! Was all ready, just waiting for us!

Here's some shots of the pits, lol...




Betty and I raced our mini-z's for the day. I gave my mini cooper some track time. Up until now it was a noob Z where it only got to run inside on the floorboards, pffft, fuck that...... Time to open her up. Lol, and open up she did. Leaving betty's McLaren for dead in the straights, lol.....

That's not much of a accomplishment since it was runing my own wound 26 turn motor vs. the 35 turn PN S04 that bet bet was runinng....

Still it was bloody funny to see a Mini Coop take out an McLaren, lol....

I also got some time to give the 40Mhz FM conversion a good beating.... I recently made a quick install lipo pack for it. It's so easy to switch between 4 cell nimh and this lipo pack since the dimensions for it are a perfect fit for the battery trays, without any mods to the trays needed....

It went fast but I personally did feel it was AS fast as previous lipo powered mr-02's I've built. Now back at home I pulled the PN Saber motor apart and found that the brushes were close to death and the commutator has started that "inverted hump" in the surface, ruining it's performance even more....



Time to buy more motors.....
