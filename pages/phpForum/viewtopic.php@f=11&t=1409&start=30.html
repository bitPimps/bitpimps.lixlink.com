Post by crazydave » Wed Aug 06, 2003 12:37 am

Got mine today. Took exactly a week. I love ToyEast.

Both cars look damn nice, the Jota is just a sexy bitch, and I like the FD3S a lot more than I thought I would. It's cool the vents are cutout on the front.




I was really impressed with the power and handling of the cars, and the 4.2 is really fast, even though the first one I tried was squeally. I also bought a 4.2 pro I haven't tried yet, so we'll see if that is torquier as promised.

I race in a small areas, so range seemed ok. It did glitch a couple times, and my car started off in crazy circles. That was pretty annoying.

My biggest bitch is that they don't fit my charger bases, and I don't like to charge off my controller to keep its batteries fresh.

I guess I'm going to have to come up with something. Maybe I'll just put a jack on my controllers so I can plug my power source in when I want to charge.

Still, all in all, I'd say it was money well spent. I'm happy.
