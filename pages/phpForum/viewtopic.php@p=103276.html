As far as I know, the smaller ones (1:43) are out of production. The larger ones (1:24) are still available at Toys R Us for either $14.99 or $19.99, but either way they are a great deal. It's fairly easy to drop an iWaver board in there and have a proportional 4x4 monster truck. They are much better suited to crawlers than bashers, though.
