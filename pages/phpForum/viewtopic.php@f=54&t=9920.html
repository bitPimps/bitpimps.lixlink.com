Post by payaso » Wed May 21, 2008 11:54 am

Shit dude, that's a total score. Nice friend you have there.

I have some tricks and tips for you, but alot of is going to be learning as you go.

Nitro is really fun, but can be finicky as the tune on your engine changes with tepms and wear on the engine itself.

How much compression does that little 2.5 have left? How hard is it to turn the fly wheel over by hand?

There are a ton of after market parts available for the Maxx line of trucks, so makin it a one of a kind is no problem. I would just drive it the way it is till you get used to it. Or start breaking parts on it. Once you do, you can replace the broken stuff with better parts. There are actually a bunch of new parts on the new 3.3 Maxx that will drop straight on to your truck. Like the sealed diffs, servo saver steering rack, beefy air filter, axles, tranny, ect...


You know you need to keep the air filter clean, or change it out after about every half hour of run time.

ARO = after run oil. This stuff will extend the life of your little engine and keep it from rusting on the inside. The methonal <sp? in nitro draws in moister and will wreak havoc on your internals.

A 5 cell hump pack to replace the double A batts for the Rx is a must. Much longer run times and better servo reaction.

You can do a spring mod for your brakes. This will keep you pads real close, but not rubbing, so no brake drag. Use a spring from a ball point pen. I'll post pics if you want to do it.


As for the EZ start. They DO NOT like being held on constantly. That will burn up your OWB (one way bearing). Once you get the fuel line primed, use the EZ start in 1/2 sec bursts. Starts alot easier and saves your starter batt and OWB.

I know the Maxx line like the back of my hand, so if you have any more Qs, just let me know.

Now go find a track and enter your first race...

Well as an update, it turns out that the 4-Tec required/s alot more work than the S-Maxx did. All I had to do on the S-Maxx was clean her up and tune her for the area. With the 4-Tec I couldn't get the piston to turn by hand (via the metal gear thing...) or with the EZ start. So I ended up taking the whole engine apart including the carburetor and EZ start.
  • First the EZ start hoping that the pinion was stripped and just not turning the piston over. Wrong.

    Then I went to the engine hoping that my friend left fuel in the engine and the oil just gunked up the piston. Right.

    Then I put it all back together and tried it out. Couldn't get fuel to go from the tank to the carb. Tried sucking it out to see if was the carb or fuel line. Stupid.

    Next I moved to the carb hoping that fuel was just gummed up in the intake. Wrong. Ended up swapping with a spare carb. Right.

    Got her to run a little today for the first time. Ran out of fuel and after refilling couldn't get her to start again. Something sounds funky after a few turns and I think exhaust is escaping from between the heat sync and cylinder.
But today I'm packing it all up and moving to my duplex for college. So in a few days I should have time to finish working on her.
