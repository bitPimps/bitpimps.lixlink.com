Heres the tri cell i entered in the speed contest, just wanna spread the word. Its manily fits best in Bb trucks with no bed cover.

Materials/Tools needed:
3 of the some type of Bit/Zip/MS batterys
Wire
Electrical tape
Soldering iron/pen/gun
Solder
something to remove a battery (EG:Smal screwdriver,guitar pick, and so on)

See link below for diagrams

http://pic7.picturetrail.com/VOL206/130 ... 077300.jpg

Step 1: Solder a wire between the - end of cell 2 to the + end of cell 3
Step 2: Solder a wire to the + end of cell 2
Step 3: Solder a wire to the - end of cell 3
Step 4: Tape (or solder then cover with tape for extra hold) the wire from the + end of cell 2 to the - end of cell 1. Make shure the whole - end of cell 1 is covered with tape.
Step 5: Slide the cell 1 into the battery campartment CAREFULLY.
Step 6: Use a slim screwdriver or something real skinny and hard to push the wire coming off of the - end of cell 3 in between the tape and the - tab for the battery compartment.
Step 7: Modify your remote as mentioned here http://bitpimps.lixlink.com/articles/20 ... -cell-mod/
Step 8: Drive liKE a maniac!
