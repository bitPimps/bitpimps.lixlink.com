CaboWabo wrote:I wish I could import the .wmv's, maybe there's a plug-in out there I'm missing.
You could probably use RAD tool's Bink to convert them. It's freeware and it seems to convert anything into almost anything. It's what I use to covert my .mov files to .avi's, and if I pull them straight from the camera, it actually cleans them up, compared to the blocky looking originals that Quicktime Pro gave me.

I'd prefer you use raw footage, as you lose a lot of image quality when you convert to .wmv, and optimizing for the web, and I'd imagine you'd lose more quality coverting back and forth. Also if you have the raw footage, you can cut the clips where you want to fit with your soundtrack.
