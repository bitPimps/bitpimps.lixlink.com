AML SUGGESTED REGULATIONS FOR RESTRICTED COMPETITION:
Note: Names and reg's are based on MPH for GT series, there are no
restrictions on body, suspension, heatsink, or tires on any race
series. There may have to be restrictions on battery type in the
future unless all cars are restricted to the same charge times.
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
"ONE MAKE" Is a competition of wits and skill, restricting all engine/
gear combinations to match from car to car. For example, if
the competition calls for a 1.0 motor and 12:1 gears, all cars
must meet that criteria to participate. This can also apply to
MOSFET, DUAL CELL, and DUAL MOTOR modified cars to keep things fair and competitive.

(a)"GT-300" Is a stage I competition class for standard range
performance, limiting motor/gear combination to a speed range
of 3.00 MPH to 3.99 MPH. The following combinations are
eligible for this series: 1.6H, 2.2L, 2.2M, 2.6L, and 2.8L.
Novice hobbyists are suggested for this race series.

(b)"GT-400" Is a stage II competition class for enhanced range
performance, limiting motor/gear combination to a speed range
of 4.00 MPH to 4.99 MPH. The following combinations are
eligible for this series: 2.2H, 2.6M, 2.8M, 3.0L, and 3.4L.
Average hobbyists are suggested for this race series.

(c)"GT-500" Is a stage III competition class for high range
performance, limiting motor/gear combination to a speed range
of 5.00 MPH to 5.99 MPH. The following combinations are
eligible for this series: 2.6H, 2.8H, 3.0M, 3.4M, 3.8L,
and 4.2L. Expert hobbyists are suggested for this race series.

"UNLIMITED GT" Is a competition for all comers and all skill levels,
though pros are best in this category. Any combination of
parts is allowed here, from batteries, tires, wheels, bodies,
heatsinks, motors, gears, suspension, chips, and dual setups.
