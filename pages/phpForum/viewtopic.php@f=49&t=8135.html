To get a decent amount of interior into a bit you'll have to do alot of re-arranging. I think it was sat4life that moved his battery back and mounted the PCB under the car . Exterme stuff like that is needed to get really good interiors. Most people just glue the tops of the seats and steering wheel to the PCB cover and paint/detail them.
