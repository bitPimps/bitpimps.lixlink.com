That's one is cool, probably one of your better ones. You really are starting to mass produce these things, I hope your getting some oxygen between paint fumes. Wouldn't want to pop that last brain cell, you know.

As far as selling them, I've had a couple people ask me to paint bodies for them, and I was willing to do it at cost. I figured with the cost of paints (I use laquer, it's a little more expensive, $5 a can, yeah I can paint dozens of cars with a can, but if they request a specific color, I have to spend my $5 to buy that color.), the body, and shipping it to them, $10-$15 is barely covering my cost, and I'm not even including the couple hours I put into it or my skill (I's gots skillz), but people thought that was too much and passed. I took personal offense to that, because I'm not going to take a loss on this.

So anyways, I'm kinda ranting, but my point being, it's a hard to set a price point on custom painted bodies, where you can offer a good deal, but still get paid fairly for your time. I think it was just kids bugging me, so there might some serious customers out there, but I just thought I'd warn you of my experience.

You could always put a couple on ebay, to get an idea of what people are willing to pay.

For me, rather than sell custom painted bodies, I'm thinking about putting my flames on decal sheets, and selling those for $3 a piece.
...crazy man, crazy...dig it.
