you use different offset to fit the rims flush with the side of the car. 190mm bodies are designed to mainlly use 0mm offset rims. That is, when the rims go on the chassis they fill out the body.

Recently, HPI has stopped making 190mm bodies, since the trend toward deep-dish rims. Making 200mm bodies for electric cars means that the bodies are now 5mm wider at each wheel and encourages deep dish rims.

I have a narrow chassis, so I have to use 7mm offset to fill a 190mm body.

Hope that helped
