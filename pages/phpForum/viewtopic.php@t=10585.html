Post by Sporttracguy » Mon Oct 26, 2009 1:07 am

So I think my local hobby store hates me Here I am minding my own business, just looking for a clear body to paint and I run into this... a no name box with no labels or branding...

Its a 1/18 4 wheel drive CORR truck with factory brushless that seems to be a complete rip off of the xray m18. Which may be good for spare parts since this thing seems to be a bit of a fly by night brand and doesnt even offer a website for all of their extra parts that they advertise in their instruction manual.

So of course I had to buy it and try it out... and I must say, there better be a good source of replacement parts, this thing is INSANE! Not a ton of suspension travel and the electronics are bargain basement quality but the brushless is crazy fast and the car is a blast to drive.
Here it is next to my traxxas 1;16 slash.

And another scale shot :


and a shot next to my xray m18, its pretty close to an exact copy. You can see the scratches, I already took it out on a test drive.


This is entirely stock, and out of the box, it came with the battery, brushless motor, electronics, heatsink fan and everything.

another shot next to the m18

one more shot to drool over


Ive only had one day to play with it, I will try and have a follow up review later. So far so good,
