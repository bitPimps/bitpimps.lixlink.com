the track is mdf, which i paint with acrylic black with a big brush. and then give it a coat of matt varnish. ever speckled spray before? like holding down the button half way, thats how to use varnish without turning it into a slipppery nightmare. also seals it real good.
and sealing is good so that you can strategicly place synth oil and spread it out nice for a little drift. it does sink in eventually and leaves nice wheel marks around the place.

as for driving, i only use my three supers these days. they have great steering and power output and are real easy to strip down and service after each time my racin buddies have a go.

there are tricky spots that require a bit of planing (the left side of the second thumb) i.e. going wide. but overall a great track for testing out motor,gear and tyre set ups.
