I'm wondering how much of a difference there'd be between just NOT hooking up the LEDs, and direct wiring the LEDs leads to the Motor.

The SE manual says not hooking up the LEDs causes less load and therefore could make the car go faster.

That makes think that there'd be no extra juice by direct wiring.

I'm guessing the LEDs are hooked into the main circuit (let's say for argument sake that the circuit is running 100mA at 2.4v). Whenever hitting forward of reverse, the LEDs takes its' part and loads the main line (dropping either the mA or v of the main circuit).

Think of it like it's a water hose. the LED is like a small leak in the hose. Whether you plug the leak (same as not hooking up the LEDs) or plug a smaller hose to the leak and run it back to the main line (which would be what Hog suggested for wiring the LED leads back to the main circuit)...You end up with the same result.
