GTO w/ removable LiPo & ZZMT motor

  • Quote

Post by LBRC » Thu May 26, 2005 1:26 am

It’s always easier a second time, no Iwaver PCB yet, good news though the stock circuit board will handle a LiPo and ZZMT motor, at least for a little while. You can tell the CB is struggling with the current and it looks like a LiPo with the NX might just edge it out but it’s plenty fast with just the 14,500 rpm motor and blue axle.

The other good news is that with the LiPo this is the best steering SE I’ve seen yet, I have at least 6 detents to either side and the turning radius is the same for each direction.
