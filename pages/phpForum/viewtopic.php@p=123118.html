Tim Minor a.k.a. KingCobra passed away on May 26th, 2009.

Some of you may remember a post of mine a while back about him. In my opinion, the RC hobby has lost one of the greatest custom builders. I may even argue the best, in the fact of making his vehicles the most realistic while being completely and fully functional to drive. I've always found inspiration in his builds.

I didn't know him very well, except for meeting him face to face a few times, but we had communicated occasionally via PMs on scale4x4. He had stopped working a long while back, and went crazy in the number of builds he did and did what he could to enjoy himself til the end. In fact, one of his latest rides was published in RCDriver a couple months back.

Scale4x4 has a subforum dedicated to just his builds and threads. Check them out, you'll see what I mean about him being the best of the best.

http://www.scale4x4rc.org/forums/forumdisplay.php?f=119
