  • Quote

Post by hogjowlz » Sun Aug 03, 2003 5:30 pm

well the super bit what can i say. the countache body is by far the best one i have. it sits really nice is super detailed and is straight outa vice city.
the new chassis is very nice. not totally improved but it is lighter im pretty sure and the smoothed out underneath is nice. i havnt fudged with the front end. the wheels are the biggest improvment i have seen. good job tomy. the controller is a lot nicer. it feels less toy-like. the gears seem smoother than other tomy cars i have as well.

the range is the same as all the tomy cars. crap. i rerouted the antenna so it doesnt go across the board which helped a little. there are no big electrolyte caps on the pcb anymore meaning more room for a dual cell.


then the perfection motors: they live up to their hype. decent torque and mad top speed. the only single celled car ive ever flipped.
