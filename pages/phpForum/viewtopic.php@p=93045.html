So here we are...after such a long time I've pimped a bit. Working on this scale was a PITA and I was grimly reminded of the concentration and steady hands needed. But it was fun getting back to my roots.

I'm 22 today, this car was completed on the 1st.

Since my camera died, I had to use my bro's cell phone. So I guess you'll have to imagine what it really looks like. My GF's mom is on a cruise and has her camera so I guess you can expect hi-res pics on or around the 15th.

I did some slight bodywork on the front lip and that hood is painted carbon fibre. It looks great. That's an R33 skyline wing too . All rattle can as usual.

I'll be honest, I've come for ROTW and ROTM . It's my birth month and I want to represent . I've been seeing all these fly rides and it got me all riled up. Especially the DB5s. This is one of very few shells I have and am willing to mod.

Well guys, this is all you'll get for a while so tell me whatcha think
