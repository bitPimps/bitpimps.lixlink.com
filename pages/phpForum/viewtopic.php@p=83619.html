Here's my final entry in this years contest.







LXX Ferrari 512 BB with swollen fenders, black/pearl silver two tone covered in pearl clear, and an R34 wing. I thought about internal dual cell, but changed my mind due to the fact that I have very little time for the hobby. But I did go with 3.0 motor, orange gearset, and an aluminum heatsink. The pullback rims were the final touch. It took a few days getting the details perfect.
I was almost finished when disaster struck. After laying down the final coat of clear, some idiot at work knocked it over while trying to get something off the shelf it was sitting on. Needless to say, it gouged the paint in several areas. So I let it dry for a few days and did the necessary touch ups. Came out ok in the end........
