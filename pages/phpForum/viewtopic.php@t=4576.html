On a single cell fetted set up with kit gears, my motors don't get that hot. I noticed they'll get hot faster if there is a bad connection from the bottum of the motor to the tab on the bottum of the motor compartment (or on the end bell for that matter). This has happened in a few of my cars. The metal tab seems to give out after some wear. It just needs to be pushed up a little and cleaned with an eraser.

I don't know if this relates to your problem, but it might be worth a look. Even my dual cells with fets and kit racer gears don't destroy notors that quick. I use 3.8's and they usually last about a month or two, sometimes six. And then all they do is slow down a little. Some of the other motors die pretty fast though. Especially tomy motors.
