  • Quote

Post by Ecca » Fri Apr 27, 2007 9:53 pm

Real easy mod. I just cut some plastic off of my Zip Zap case and used a soldering iron to burn a nice slot for the plastic to slide in. Then I bent the plastic (heating it up with the soldering iron) to make it perform better. Haven't flipped my car once.




Any questions or comments, feel free to reply.
