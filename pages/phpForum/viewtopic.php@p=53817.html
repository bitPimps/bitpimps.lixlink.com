Here's a lil' sumpn from Team SuperFly. It's black and blue, and it hurts: The Bruise. I especially liked entry number 4 in the dictionary: 4. To hurt, especially psychologically. There's a little Dark Tari influence goin on, so I figured I'd name it. And you know SuperFly never goes anywhere without a fine lady!!!

This started out as a yellow/black pullback body.
Full body kit on a rarely-seen NSX. Shaved door handles and indicator lights. Tab-lowered front, slot-lowered rear
Perfect paint job; midnight blue metallic with gloss black roof and wing, gloss clear with dull clear over appropriate areas.
Super bit wheels from speed set.
Black louvered heat sink that fits perfectly in the cutout when dropped to lowered slot.
Spring suspension with almost full range of travel.
Just got my new fets in the mail, so those go in next week.
