RC4WD Product Issues

Post by sg219 » Tue Feb 06, 2007 4:23 pm

I bought a 10T/48P pinion from RC4WD and when I went to install it the set screw just spun. Almost no bite.

I emailed RC4WD and they sent me a new pinion.

I just finished trying to install the new one.

It did the SAME thing.

I intend on sending both of the pinions back for a refund.

So if you plan on buying a pinion from them, please think about it twice.

I've bought other products from them and have had little or no issues, so please don't take this as a knock on RC4WD, just the pinions I've tried.

Other RC4WD products I've bought with little or no issues...

-Shocks
-Spur gear (Would not fit the spur locker. Had to remove about >1mm of plastic from the pegs.)
-Beadlock wheels (Had an issue with the depth of the holes, I had to find thinner hexes for them to work. Stock TLT hexes are too thick.)
-Wideners (bought 2nd hand)
-6/32 rod ends
