iWaver 01... The best quality clone mini z to date. Period. I found myself battling people who were rabidly anti-clone(from dealing with super poor quality earlier clones) when I got mine, and had a really hard time understanding why people were so against this little car that I thought was such an awesome value... I have had mine for like 5 months now, and I still love it(and mines still stock... have you seen betty.ks?)... Even hardcore mini z fans are warming up to them... Definately worth carrying, and the GT40s and Carerras seem to be what everybody wants right now...

I don't see how you could go wrong with these. If you can keep them around or under $40 shipped(about what you'd pay Toyeast for one if that was all you ordered)... You're golden.
