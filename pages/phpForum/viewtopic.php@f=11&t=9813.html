I know I wasn't the only one to buy one. I got the Supra many years ago.

Factory dual cell, 2 speed, Incredibly SCALE Appearance, Plastic body, great weight distribution.

The tires are absolute crap! They will NOT hook on any surface known to man! These are also a VERY tiny tire, like 0.350 OD. The only other thing I've owned with similar size tires was a single channel IR racer that came either in a Pen or a weird remote with a cheap game built in, hold on I've gotta find one it just might work!

If you're in deep enough that you have to try colecting and modding everything possible, then you already know the answer. If not...
