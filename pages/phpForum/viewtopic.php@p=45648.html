Post by Gfunk » Sat May 29, 2004 5:16 pm

I absoutely LOVE these things for modding. Why may you ask? Many reasons. For one, they have two grea gearboxes right out of the box, both the power and steering gearboxes are pretty nice, as long as your gears aren't stripped. Also, as stated by Betty.K, they have dual H-bridges, VERY nice for anything you want with motorized steering. But the last thing alot of peopel DON"T realize, that beautiful little IC on there, when 2C or 6C is written on it, is capable of having a total of 4 channels, provided you have a controller with an IC to go along with it to add the extra channels.
