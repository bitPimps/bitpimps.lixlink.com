I had a lot of slot cars as a kid, and I always fantasized that I could drive off the track and keep goin'. So, when those NOS Bottle cars came out with their HO scale intact, I had to build me up a slot car that goes off the track.
T-Jets are my faves, so that's where I started. This was a total bitch. The body is screwed onto custom mounts behind the front wheel wells, and one screw in the back from underneath. The body is literally crammed on the chassis, and I broke the steering wires FIVE DAMN TIMES trying to get the shit to fit. It's not the best driving car, but it works pretty well, all things considered.
