I happened to pass by the Hot wheel display at the local Wal-Mart the other day and saw this Scion. I am not into Hot Wheels anymore but this one grabbed my eye, it had a plastic body. That got me to thinking and well I immediately took it home and ripped it apart…

The body would not fit a regular bit chassis and the Zip Zap chassis was to long? So I cut up a chassis and I’m in the process of lengthening the bit chassis to fit (might throw in an extra battery while I’m at it!

This is it so far I primed it and I’m in the process of coating it with cherry red hot rod paint! More pics when further along.

I did some more work on the Scion and got the paint job just about complete. I stretched the chassis and added some dub wheels. I installed aluminum pipes and if you are wondering about the size the Homies are smaller then the original more 1/64 scale there Micro Homies.

I still have a lot of work to do on the chassis I need to finish filling it in and I plan to add a 2nd cell. I also have to trim to front fenders walls as the wheels rub a bit and it does not turn as good as I would like it to. But being low the ground it does scoot real quick and with the extra cell I might not keep it on the ground.
