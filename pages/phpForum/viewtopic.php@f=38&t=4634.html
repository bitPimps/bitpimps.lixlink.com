I'm not sure if I posted this before (I've been out of the game for a while) but here is my latest creation. I started out with a 1/24th scale Tamiya Focus WRC model kit and immediately chopped the body in half. I then removed about a 1/4 inch strip and bonded the two halves back together. All the side windows had to be cut as well. I then cannibalized a set of XM wheels for the center hubs and adapted them to the Tamiya rims. I decided to paint it red to match my SVT focus. Lastly I made custom body mounts and drilled holes for the headlights and taillights. It came out pretty good and the body stays on no matter how hard I push the car.
