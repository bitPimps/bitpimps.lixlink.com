I had more people the first round after Drive line parts for the trucks than anything else. In the second round with a focus on Gearboxes I didn't have a single person contact me about Truck Gearboxes. More than enough cars to get a discount but no trucks.

I moved my dates many times before I finaly decided on a cut off. Even then you get the last minute people that contact you the day you submit the order asking if its too late and say "I'll paypal you right now".

If GPM does you the same way they do everyone else your order can take a month to process.

1) You submit the order.
2) They email you a week later that they got it and give you an invoice and ask for 50% before they tell you shipping cost or even it can be shipped.
3) You send them the 50% and pay the money transfer or Master Card fee
4) 2 weeks go by and you email them asking if they got the money. Yes they did but now your total is 5% higher than before because you want EMS shipping.
5) You send them the money again Paying the fee again.
6) 6+ weeks after the order was submited they let you know that 90% of the colors you want will take 2 months to do. But if you want to get Blue or Silver they have them ready next week.

I pay $15 everytime I have to send them money from my bank. More if I choose MasterCard.

Best deal realy if you want it is skip the GB and submit exactly what you want from them for yourself. And ask them for the total including the shipping via EMS the first time. For a full set of everything at GPM prices you are looking at arround $200 with shipping from GPM.
