that brushless esc would work, except it has no reverse like the mamba 25. For $9.99 tho..wow...just wow... who needs reverse?? hah
use now, buy mamba later.

Motor and deans are out of stock. good prices tho.

IMO better off getting the feigao 12x20mm motor. it's plenty of motor and
less current requirements. I havent kept up to date on the tiny lipos but
the 12x20 works great with a 2cell 250mah fullriver. if you can find the
300mah version then the 12x30 would give you some crazy tq for really
large custom pinions. motor requirements and lipo capabilities must
match up or it could cog/overdraw the lipo. I guess it kinda depends on
your chassis plate, if it can fit larger lipos, might be fun to try. A few
people on r7r have used the 12x30mm motors.
