I got tired of the limited selection of styles of rims that the ZZ-M's have. Sure most of the bodies came with something different, and there were 3 others in the "tire wheel kit" but overall, they're pretty much the same!

What do you think of these?


What it is:
ZZ-M rims that can use ANY hubcap intended for the ZZ or ZZ-SE.

What's needed to make it:
27/64" drillbit
1/4" drillbit
knife file or sandpaper

Gives you a few more custom rim options for your custom trucks.
