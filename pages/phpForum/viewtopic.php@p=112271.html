Post by Clint » Thu Aug 30, 2007 7:16 pm

Picked up an E-Sky Honey Bee King V2 (yellow ARF)



They are unbelievably cheap for a micro w/belt drive. ARF was 57$ from rc711.com.
RTF is about $115. If you do grab an RTF, a brushless upgrade is 100% necessary,
but you can grab an esky brushless motor and 25amp esc for ~50$.

It's a 400 size heli..basically a 450 compatible size bellhiller head and tail
assembly with the 300 size length tail boom/belt. Overall Body size is
comparable to a Blade CP, but the main blades are larger at 600mm
diameter(bcp is ~525mm) and the 450 size head makes for a taller
profile. Other than that, it still feels like a 300 size heli, and can be
upgraded/stretched with trex and other 450 parts easily.


Electronics are ordered(should be here this coming week)
I figured WTH..lets go with some luxury parts:

Just Go Fly 400DH 4000kv motor
Castle Creations Pheonix 25 amp esc
Castle Creations Berg 7P RX
Castle Creations 10amp switching BEC
(all three CC parts use the usb castle link )
ThunderPower Prolite 1350mah 3s lipo
3 E-flight s75 servos for collective
Futaba GY401 Heading Hold Gyro
Futaba Digital micro servo s3154 for tail

Teardown and reassembly will occur once more parts arrive, for now heres some pics as it is.
