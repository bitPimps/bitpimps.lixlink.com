Post by CaboWabo » Wed May 21, 2003 8:53 am

inside the chassis here's your checklist:
- make sure the battery inside the car has the (-) end taped. when you put the battery back in the chassis, make sure the "rubbing" hasn't worn a tiny hole in the tape (this happens a lot), this will short your shiat.

- make sure the battery inside the car has the (-) end that is taped, on the drivers side.

- make sure the wire leading from (-) on the battery in the car, goes to (+) on the battery outside the car.

- make sure the wire leading from (-) on the battery outside the car, goes to the (-) post inside the chassis, in between the (-) taped end of the battery inside the car and the (-) post that is part of the chassis.

- make sure the wires do not touch your PCB at all, otherwise you'll get a short.
