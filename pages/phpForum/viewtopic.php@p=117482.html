Post by nonickname » Wed Jan 28, 2009 11:30 pm

Unsure, but I just ripped apart an old heli, and it had a Propo PCB (which was tiny!!!, like 1/4 the size of a bit one. You could use that pcb and another for steering), a tiny tiny tiny little LiPo pack , a fullsize motor (with wires attached) and a tiny little motor which could be adapted for steering. I'd have a look. Also, on the TTTT I've begun to build (not much done yet), I'm using a bcg gearbox with a 1.0 for the center gearbox, then running a shaft (or if I'm lazy, a spring) to the rear part off an aurora AFX slot car (pinion and crown gear + axle).

In my opinion, hand build most the parts except for gears, electronics etc. Good donor gears are from zz's/bcg, clones, watches (if you want a really huge reduction) etc. Also, if you happen to have an old tape player it will probably have a mechanical song counter. The one I ripped out had a worm gear in it.

Good luck
