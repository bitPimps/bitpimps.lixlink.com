I know that you can get 60' - 100' range out of some nerf with work.
The mods they do can go from "pretty simple" to "very little of the original gun remains".
I know there are 3 kinds of nerf and they all mod differently: pneumatic, spring, electric.
Apparently there are some people who do Nerf Wars, and they each have different rules about what is allowed in their war.
