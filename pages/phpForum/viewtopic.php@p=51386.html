The Mini 96 costs $199 over at miniz-zracer.com. It comes with 72 straight tiles, 12 inside corners, & 12 outside corners. The tiles are 30cm square and you can configure it however you like as long as you have the tiles. It has a smooth side and a rougher "racing" surface on the other side.

You can also buy blank tiles to widen the lanes and off road tiles soon.
