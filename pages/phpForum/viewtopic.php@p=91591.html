Post by crazydave » Tue Apr 04, 2006 5:58 pm

You know, I got burned out on bits for a while there. Tried getting like Xmods and an Epoch and stuff, but they kinda sucked. I got my Mini LST, and I was very happy. Didn't think I'd play with anything smaller again.

Then this last weekend, I drove my Losi around, and it was fun, but I wanted more speed, but first I need to invest in decent charger, and right now I'm broke. So the Losi is alright and all, but this waiting game sucks, and now it's caked with dirt.


Last night I'm kicking back, and I look over at my bit collection. Its all covered in ashes and dust, and I had gotton lazy, and just started piling cars on top of cars. So I'm not sure why, but next thing I know I'm dusting them off, and arranging them, then I see cars I want to drive. I pick out 3 that were almost there, they just needed a little tweaking, and I was fed up with them when I last played with them.

After fiddling with the cars, I threw my 4'x4' track out on the end of the bed, like I used to, and for once all the cars performed perfectly. It was just so relaxing laying bed watching TV, making laps.

No larger scale hobby grade RC can replace that, and I've realized the fiddling and tweaking is part of the fun, it just gets out of hand sometimes with 50+ micro RCs to maintain. One thing thing bits have done for me that will never change, is scouring the toy store shelves looking for things that could become part of a RC.
...crazy man, crazy...dig it.
