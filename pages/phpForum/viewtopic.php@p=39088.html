Post by EdIsMe » Sat Feb 28, 2004 11:09 pm

I don't want a fast motor for the steering. Cell phone vibrators are low rpm, high torque motors. They're designed to spin an unballanced weight block creating vibrating force. Also, cell phone vibrators are designed to handle 3.5 - 4v which is perfect for a 4v Li-ion. The dual engines in the rear will provide sufficient torque even if there is a nominal deficiancy of torque per motor and for thus I want the highest rpm motors I can get. Also, having the dual engines soaks up any over-volted stress because the stress is split between two motors.
