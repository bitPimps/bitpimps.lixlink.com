i think by ground affects he means a body kit. for a body kit, i just cut out a strip of a credit card to my desired ride height, heat it up with a candle and bend it to the contours of the front end e.g where it wraps around to the fenders. cut it to size, glue it on, then use Bondo Auto Body Filler to fill in the cracks, sand smooth, and voila!

the u-v set up could be stripped down and a rubber band could be used to keep it all together. then depending on proficiency the led could be positioned (esp. in a clear super perfection body) so that it sheds light directly onto the flouro bars (what i did).

OR using fibre optics attached to the globe's front the light could be directed to any position. i.e. into the ends of flouro bars, individual fibres separated to point down in between the wheels like real cars do and maybe even two fibres mounted in the bonnet (you know the squirter jet lights).

you would have to paint any part of the globe black that wasnt being used for fibres with the last method to avoid unwanted light.
