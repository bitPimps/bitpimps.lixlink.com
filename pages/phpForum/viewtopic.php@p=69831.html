Believe it or not, the T actually handles the track layout better since it has a tighter turning radius, but since it's losing compression, it don't like to take a hard hit.

This track set up is left over from the "Losi Offroad Championships" and is ideal for a stadium trucks... The mosters are a bit large.


I like driving both trucks, but the T is a bit faster, and has a strong low end punch, but likes to flame out in a roll over This will kill a race instantly, so yeah, if you wanna act like a kid, then you can sit on the pit table while the Revo shows you how to act...
