Hello dudes! Here's K.I.T.T. for you. I hope you like what i have done initially. I used Sculpey clay for the hood and the rear bumper. Basically, the shell is a wannabe Bit Char-Gi that looks like a Skyline. I bought it at Toys "r" Us. From there i sculpted the excess clay after heating it up using hot water. Usual paint job (2-3 coats of lacquer paint). I've included his sidekick Michael so they can go around turbo-boosting. For reference, i placed the original merchandise from Universal Studios which is under repair right now. Enjoy!
