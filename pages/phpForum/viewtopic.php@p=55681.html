So I guess Hue don't use his Xmod now that he's playing with Mini-Zs. He offered it to me for a screaming deal I couldn't pass up.

So now I got's me another Xmod. Despite Hue's heavy use, the chassis was in better condition than mine, because he had only run it indoors, but the body had been beat on pretty good, mostly by Superfly's car.

So I wanted to clean it up, and make it like new, which was pretty easy, because Hue included most of the remains of the body set.

Here's what I started with


After switching back to mostly stock body pieces, I took the stickers off, masked it off and gave it a good sanding. I layed a light metallic grey. I dig this color because it turns almost silver when the light hits it.

I made a replacement lense for the headlight out of, what else?... Zip Zap packaging. It came out pretty good, except that because it's thinner plastic, it doesn't come flush with the hoodline, but otherwise it fills up the space pretty well, I'm happy with it.




I just wanted it to look like new for now, but I might lay some flames or a race scheme in a couple weeks when I get bored with it, and if I haven't scratched it up too bad.
...crazy man, crazy...dig it.
