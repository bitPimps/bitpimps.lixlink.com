A couple of threads have mentioned MS getting the boot from Target, and I wanted to voice a couple of more thoughts.

I have been watching this site for months and months...

http://tabletopracers.com/microsizers-pro-series.html

They had release dates for the new line of MS Pros for a few months. Changed it twice now(original release of Nov I think...). Every time I email them, they are a bit aloof of what is going on and I don't have enough guts to push for a straight answer.

Now Target is not stocking MS anymore. Last time I checked Toys R Us, they had one row of MS on a shelf, and many many clones strewn throughout different displays in the store.

Another thing that concerns me is that Tomy isn't putting the turbo buttons to use on their newer controllers. It seems that there is questionable judgement there also, or there is something going on business-wise that we don't know about.

I'm worried about the future of Tomy/MS... I just got into this hobby a few months ago, and I don't want to see the best stuff already out of production.
