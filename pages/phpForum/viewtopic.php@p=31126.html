aiight folks, I'll write ya'allz up a lil tut on how i did this...


first, u gotta print up what you want to be on your decal.... In black, as colored ink doesn't come out as wet as black ink(i've noticed)...
once you've done that, cut yourself a piece of decal sticker paper the size of your writing

take the sticker and gently place it onto the paper over the writing...make sure you don't push hard on the sticker or else you'll take the paper off with the sticker...


carefully pull your sticker off; it should look like this:
