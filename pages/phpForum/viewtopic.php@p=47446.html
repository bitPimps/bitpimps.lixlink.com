aaaaaaaaaaand we're back! there's a couple of things i really could have done better like the crooked front grill and those tail lights just aint quite right. but stuff it, i wanna trash it now! i threw a blue led under the hood to light up the chassis so it shines through the grills in a pimpin stylee! making plates was easy, print them up on marker paper (non bleeding). then i smeared some super glue on a tackle box (super glue won't stick to this stuff) and placed the paper face down in the glue. smear more on the back and allow to dry (about 1 - 2 mins). peel it off, cut em out and there ya have it, poor man's laminate!
i staged the crap outta these first pics by removing the shocks for that lowrider look and used some stealthy black wheels and nuts to finish it off.
