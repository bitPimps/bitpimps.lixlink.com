Well Yesterday was RC Country Hobby's Bi-Annual swap meet. I actually saw some commercials this year on TV. I ended up going around 9 am with nothing to sell, just looking to see what was there. Last time, I had a lot of things to sell and was there at around 5am. It was much more fun this way. Anyway, I skip ahead to the things I ended up getting.

I got a new (no fuel ran through yet), pre-assembled XRay NT18 with electronics pack and Hudy starter box for $130. I've always wanted one since I got into Nitro because I just don't care for 1/10 scale too much. I like my RC's small. Anyway, I've been working on it all weekend, tweaking the layout and the body, etc. I found out tonight that the Airtronics receiver that was initially installed was FM and all my radios are AM, so tomorrow I'm gonna go see if my S-Maxx has a micro receiver in it.

Also, I scored 3 zipzap bodies, all by choice: 1 Austin Martin Vanquish body, 1 Hummer SUT body, and 1 Mustang 67' fastback body (my dad has a '67 coupe). Each one for a buck a piece.

Lastly (I know this is gonna piss some of you off) I got two Combat Digi-Q Tanks for $23 each. They are pretty cool. My dad was in the military, so he was big on me finding the Panther and the Tiger. Pretty cool stuff overall. Here's the pic:


Let me know what you all think.

Edit: I just checked and the S-Maxx has a regular size receiver, and I don't want to wait to get one off ebay, but I also don't want to give RC Country the money with the jacked up price too (they are assholes to me 8/10 times I go in). Damn.

I'll tell you, I'm starting to understand the hobby store more, and one store pisses me off, because they are money grubbers, but on the other hand at the nearby Hobbytown I'm becoming friends with the manager, and they are good people there, but they are struggling to make rent. While the RC stuff is blowing off the shelves, they make hardly any profit off that stuff, they make their money on the toy stuff on the other side of the store. A lot of us remember that stuff from when we were kids, but since this area is largely Hispanic/immigrants, I guess it's just not part of their culture, and that stuff is not selling at all. So when you go to the hobby shop, bring your kid, let 'em pick out a science set, model rocket, or something. It'll help maintain the balance.

I know this sounds strange coming from me, I've made so many anti-LHS post in the past, but these guys have treated me right so I'm more willing to listen to their side. Also In an effort to try to increase profits, he wants to hook me up with some bodies to paint, and then put them in the front window for sale. I say if people are willing to pay that much, then lets go for it, I'd love to get more exposure for my paint jobs, and if I can help the shop stay in business through the summer months, so I know it'll be there next year, then that's just bonus.
