I think it's going to be based on your personal preference, ability, dedication, and pickiness.

basically, you've got 3 choices when painting:
- paint brush
- spray can
- air brush

more than likely, you'll use a combination of 2 of the 3.
use brushed paints for details.
use either the spray can or air brush to lay down the rest.

now, choosing between spray can or air brush...

I started with spray cans "rattle cans" as Ahh5hit calls em lol
certain kinds work great and produce most of the stuff you see in the pictures on this site. you've got alot of options there and if you're just starting out - I'd highly recommend you start with spray cans.

air brushes are what I consider to be the final step. its a pro's tool (most of them) and can produce professional and increadible results. however...
those results are from a shiat load of work and skill. things like shading are possible with an air brush and not with spray cans at this scale.
