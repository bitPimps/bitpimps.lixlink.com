hey there you there, it's me here now that my se's sorted out i decided to give it the monster treatment, just gotta sort out the shell and i'd like to run 3.6V through it eventually. i shaved some material off the knuckle mounts and got more travel outta the suspension and replaced the stock springs with some softer ones.



i also made a couple of cool looking skid pans out of the metal cover from a 1.4mb floppy. whatever you use make sure it isn't magnetically attractive or your steering will be useless. i used quality superglue to attach them, so far so good......
