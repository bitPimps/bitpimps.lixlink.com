Ok, i got 3 Super perfections...

On the 1st car, the steering coils in the front of the car, was not glued into place. And some of the wires attached to the coils were broken. Fixed it, and the car worked fine.

2nd car just needed another antenne...

3rd car's controller was screwed - someone had tried to take the buttons out or something like that.

So yes - the components are weak, in the super perfections!
