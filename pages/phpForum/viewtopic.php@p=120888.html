Post by frizzen » Mon Feb 16, 2009 4:41 am

Tatra time...

Redrustbucket posted about milling the drivecups in the ZZ-M knuckle, it was a must-do steering mod!!! Pull an E-clip, push the cup inboard, I used a round diamond bit in a drill press, cleanup with a file, installation is anti-dissasemblywise. Combine that with some liberal trimming of plastic, and it'll turn until the trailing edge almost hits the chassis!!!

So, because I'd just modded the knuckles, I had to know how it performs now. I finally gave in and yanked the electronics out of another ZZ-M, threw them in, and did a test-drive! It was a quick and dirty install, but the project moves under its own power now.

Turning is pretty good for a 4" wheelbase ZZ-M, it's within 2 feet and not the 40 acres I was fearing. Wheelbase is about TWICE the length of a stock ZZ, so I'm pretty happy with it. Doing the mod an a stock length one, you'd have to be careful not to run into yourself when you turn.

Green motor, green gears, 2 cell. Kinda slow and needs more torque to make up for things like its heavier body, that I was hauling a '59 'vette ZZ, if get crazy and put a pintle hitch on... Trying to decide if I need to torque test motors, play with pinions, or just volt up.

I'm starting to work on the interior layout.

I was thinking more about performance. Sure, it LOOKS like a truck, but it's gotta drive like a truck. A big, burly unstoppable millitary truck.

ZipZap Monster tech
- Green motor (8,000rpm), 7t pinion, Green gears (23.99:1) has a really good scale speed which I like; but lacks the obscene torque of this type truck. It struggles over terrain while carrying a ZipZap in the bed. Think fast trial truck, or around town driving speeds.
- Green motor, 6t pinion, green gears (27.98:1) doesn't loose much speed and is a little better torque, barely.
- Red motor (14,500rpm, second slowest, and highest torque of all ZZ-M motors), 7t pinion, Green gears (23.99:1) has a bit more speed than I'd really prefer, but simply hauls the mail with a ZZ in the bed, and can climb much better than I was expecting. Think more like highway or Dakar truck speeds.
- Red motor, 6t pinion, green gears (27.98:1), forget it. Since I'm not running a PCB cover like a stock ZZ-M the motor clip is only held at one end, and the torque keeps breaking gear-mesh.

So, red motor, green gears. Maybe I'll add more tire weight if it's still too fast after I finish putting lots more detail stuff on it.
