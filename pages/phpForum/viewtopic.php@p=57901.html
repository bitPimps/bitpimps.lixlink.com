That's it, I gotta say, you're now looking at a 100% xmod convert here....

Yesterday I finally popped a nelly 1.3 in to my xmod. Previous to this I was running my plasma/atomic can combo on stock fets and the 8t pinion. Top speed was good, torque was crap.

Once I put nelly in though, damn! What a difference. Now it only takes a minute of driving to unlock my ball diff. The take off is great now, and top speed has increased dramatically. ON stock fets the ball diff never loosened up. I had it setup pretty tight too.

What is interesting is that the XMOD on the tammy speed checker clocked in at 24kph with the above setup on stock fets. Once I put nelly in it hit 27kph. This doesn't sound like much, but day-am! Put the bugger on the ground and you can clearly see the increase in performance.

It just goes to show you how the XMOD suffers greatly from the in-efficiencies of it's AWD drivetrain. So basically whenever you can get any extra torque you should try, it makes the biggest difference!

I'll post some pics of the setup when I get the chance. The install wasn't too bad actually, considering the turkey the xmod is when building/stripping.....
