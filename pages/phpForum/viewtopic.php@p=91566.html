it is messed up, but not how you're thinking. It's not a normal fade, It's a kind of speckle fade. But in laying the paint, some of the red got too far forward and I had to alcohol it off. If you look at the A-arms you'll see where I was trying to erase the red and failed. The MAXXA stickers are also covering such mistakes. If you look closely at the fade area tho it looks more like... confetti blowing away? The top didn't fade as planned either, hence the big HPI sticker

I'm still VERY pleased with the results tho. Every time I look at it I the hear popping sounds of anti-lag . Even if I only glimpse it
