Hey dudes chill out a bit. I'm just a little guy trying to keep the track business going.

I just got back from the NY toy fair (like 3 hours ago - so a bit lagged out).

Here's the deal.

As far as I know, Ken has sent 3 samples to me (2 went to yakboy) and a bunch to Sven at bitracer (he lives in USA and runs an estore in Germany for them bitracer dudes).

Ken was not at the NY toy show, he is in asia sorting out mass production for the USA and re-enginnering for Europe. The model I and sven have are first shot samples from low production run - they are not legal to use in Europe

However, walking arround the NY Javits centre, guess what I saw - some metal tech racers. It was quite funny really because I clocked them and asked about them, the nice lady rep said that she would like to show me them running but couldn't get them to work - being a bit of a smart arse I spent a couple of minutes, sorted out the problem (dodgy connection in the controller battery pack, I had same problem with first sample Ken sent me) and prompltly started screaming it arround the convention centre.

The company showing them was a smallish die cast distributor company called DMV Ltd from 2409 beacon hills, rochester hills Michigan 48309 . tel 248 854 4026 (no web site on business card). The MD shares a business partner with Ken. This tells me that there is not any major distribution yet in the USA and I GUESS that they will not get major distribution in 2005 - but I could be well out of line in saying that

The reason that you are probably not getting replies from Ken is that he has been hit with loads of emails since the news broke about them on the community. Don't get me wrong, Ken is a great guy but has other priorities at the moment (sorting out production and new body shells ) - I was just lucky in starting a dialogue early on.

So cut the xfile conspiracy shit
