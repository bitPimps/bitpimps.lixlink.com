Q: What paint should i use
A: For an airbrush I'd go for Badger's Freak Flex http://www.megahobby.com/index.asp?Page ... rodID=7806 I just tried it and it was smooth, plus it's water based for easy clean up. For rattle can Krylon Fusion (any hardware store) actually works pretty well, and it's tough once it's cured.


Q:Who can do the FET mod for me (ill pay)
A: You Can! check the tutorials...I don't think anyone here is doing this anymore, but with a little patience and practice (on Walgreens $5.99 or 3 for $12 bits) I'll bet you can do it...ok, I've never tried, but what the heck, I say go for it.

Q:Where can i get a FAST motor
A: Out of an old pager. This site has a ton of useful stuff: http://www.didel.com/Frames.html?MainFrame=News.html Also you might seach "pager" in the search box and see if you can find a few examples from past builds.

Q:did anyone do 4wd(i read the fourm for a bit)
A: Oh yeah, and how! Check this out:http://bitpimps.lixlink.com/pages/phpFo ... 60&start=0

Q:Where can i get grippy tires
A: Diecast make great doners, so do slot cars in the HO scale...I don't think you can get this brand anymore but the swap is the same for just about any brand.http://bitpimps.lixlink.com/articles/20 ... wheel-mod/

Q:where can I get gears
A: Beats me...you could cut your own if you have a watchmakers hand mill, but that might be beyond the average persons level of commitment. I have plans to build one if anyone wants a copy let me know.


And don't worry about questions, it's good to see some renewed interest in micros.

Post by sidewinder » Sat Dec 20, 2008 3:52 pm

Oh, sooner or latter you're gonna break something...you should see my "dead pile." But, I suppose the place to start would be wheels, just take your time and follow the tutorial, you should be fine...or if you want really safe then you can't beat a custom paint job. Buy some cheap matchbox type cars from a dollar store and practice. The bonus is you might even get some good cheap wheels and tires that you can use when you're ready.

Oh, and all water based paint washes off while it's drying, you just need to give it time to set up. Unless your car is older than about a 1989 model it has water based paint...stupid EPA, they had real problems with it at first...look at a late 80's Chevy...especially the Beretta...you'll see the paint on the hoods and roofs peeled off. But, they have since perfected it and now all automotive paint (for commercial use anyway) is water based. I've never tried Duplicolor on a bit, but I have used it on race cars for quick touch-ups and it works fine.
