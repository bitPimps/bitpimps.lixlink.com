That's a solid plan. I started noticing things when I switched over to lithium power. First was the increase in speed. Then I started noticing the problems that plague Xmods. First off the suspension and drivetrain are not designed for significant power increases. This is what prompted me to buy alloy parts and High grade bearings in the first place. With all the mods and money I have spent, I built one hell of an Xmod that is almost perfectly balanced.
