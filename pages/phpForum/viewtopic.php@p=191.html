R-Spec motor

  • Quote

Post by payaso » Tue Feb 04, 2003 7:37 pm

Tinyrc recently hooked me up with a r-spec motor to test out, and I must say that this little gem is packing a great deal of power. It's got the speed of a 3.8 with a good amount of torque to boot. The only motor I have that can keep up with it is the 3.8 from bitchar-gmotors. Both are fantastic motors for high end speed, but the r-spec seems to have a little more off the line torque than the 3.8..

I suggest either one of these beauties if your all about the speed, and they're both available at tinys shop. http://tinyrc.com/shop/products.php?pid=2

Please post your opinions and thoughts in this thread.
