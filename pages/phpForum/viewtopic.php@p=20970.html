Post by ph2t » Fri Aug 08, 2003 5:39 pm

1) My soldering iron has the tip filed into a smaller cylinder, this helps.

3) I use a solder sucker (rubber bulb with teflon tip) to get all the solder 2way to leave the pad nice 'n clean.

3) I also use contact glue to hold the components in place before soldering them.


ph2t's major advice time

Don't overheat the solder pads on the @#$%% pcb. They will lift off the pcb very easily and then you're buggered 'cause it's damn near impossible to follow the fine track back and get enough surface area to be able to solder onto it.

I've found it's easier to cut the legs off the old transistors and remove the black body. Then reheat the solder pads and remove the legs. This way you heat up the pads for a less amount of time and have a less of a chance of lifting them off the pcb.
