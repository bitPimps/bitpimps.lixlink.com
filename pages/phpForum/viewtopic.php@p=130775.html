  • Quote

Post by HirotoR34 » Fri Sep 20, 2013 8:24 pm

Been a while since I've come around here, but life sometimes takes you in a weird direction.But I'm still into anything r/c which now includes helicopters. It started when my dad bought me a blade scout for Christmas. I had so much fun with it, I was hooked. Stepped up to an mcx2 and had some more fun..but nothing could prepare me for the lunacy of collective pitch birds. Did a little research online, found some info on how to fly one. Next step was to buy one, so after a trip to the local hobby shop, I came home with the blade mcpx v2. Charged up a couple batteries and was prepared to fly off into glory. Um, it was a cruel spectacle. I couldn't even hover the damn thing for more than ten seconds before crashing over and over again. So after many more flights and tons of money into replacement parts I eventually got better at it. I can't do the hardcore 3d flying like the pros, but can do flips, rolls, pyros, inverted flight, pretty much take off and land in the same spot. I really enjoy fast forward flying and going into banked turns, low altitude flight. I can even keep them in stable hovers. At some point I decided that between fixing them if I had more, I would always have at least one to fly. So it basically snowballed from there
