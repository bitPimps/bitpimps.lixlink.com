Yes, they're rare. No, they ain't worth shit...Well, except if you plan to do some mods on 'em.

Back when they first came out, I bought like 5 of the sets. I figured it shouldn't be too hard to sell them off since they seem cool and were only available in Japan.

Nobody and I mean NOBODY was/is interested in them. I still have 4. I sold one on no-reserve ebay and got like less than $20 for it, so I'm keeping the rest and I'll pass them on to my goddamned grandkids before I take another loss like that on it.

Hack away when you get yours man, ain't worth it to keep in the box as investment.
