I've been working on this on and off for a while now, and lately I've been a little busy with other things, so I haven't really had time to mess with it. I have to finish the ground effects, and figure out how I'm doing the front end, and then it should be ready for paint. It started out as a Mitsubishi(obviously), and so far it has been convertiblized, wing holes filled, hood ducts filled, hood and trunk shaved, door handles shaved,exhaust eliminated so I can do some tubing, and the grill and foglights filled so I can do an intercooler and different fogs. I have almost fit the convertible boot from a die-cast MR2, but that still needs a little tweaking for clearance. Anyway....







I don't know what kind of wheels it will be rollin' on yet, but it's either gonna be pullback or alloy. As for paint, it's gonna be a simple one color affair, because I really don't want to mess up. I will decide on a color when it's ready for a color.
