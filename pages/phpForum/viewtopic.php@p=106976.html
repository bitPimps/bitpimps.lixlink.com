I wish I had bought one sooner this little thing is one Bad “A” Mo Fo!

Of course when I got my Micro-T the first thing I had to do is get rid of the body that was on it I am not a fan of the stadium truck so I had to find a donor body and I had to have a VW Bug!

I found my the perfect donor body in the 1/32?? Herbie VW set it has the white VW and the black one. I chose to use the black one because I liked the vents on the side. I proceeded to chop the VW up and Dremel the heck out of it!

It did not take me long to figure out how to mount it I wanted to use the same type of mounting so I just extended the rods with tube aluminum and drilled holes in it for the cotter pins. The aluminum was attached to the chassis with the help of some small screws.
