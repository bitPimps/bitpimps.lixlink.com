This is one of those nasty motorworks trucks.

Before shots;




I had actually already started the putty work. It came with a gay ass tail foil. At first I wanted to redesign it, but then I decided I wanted a more normal look.

After shots;





As far as mods, I gave it a very small gauge wire for an antenna. I found something smaller than the smallest gauge of guitar string. It came from a wire cutting device that they use for scale train environment building. This is a 49mhz, and with that wire, I haven't found a place in my apartment where it wont recieve the signal. The wheels are modifier wheels. The back tires have about three coats of black silicon. internal dual cell. Fits easy in this motor work's chassis. I didn't change the gearing. I think it's 12:1 (stock). I wanted more torque to simulate a real truck. And I didn't fet mod it. When you dual cell this particular type of motorworks car, it runs like it's already been fett modded. I don't know enough about transistors to figure out if it really has fets in it already, but if I had to put money down, I'd say it does. It's almost the exact same speed as another car that I have which is fetted and dual celled w/ 12:1 gears.

Got any ideas for a name? I'm not sure if it is a particular make. I think it was just a generic truck.
