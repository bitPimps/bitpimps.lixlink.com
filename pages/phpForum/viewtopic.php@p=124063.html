new micro heli i done bought

  • Quote

Post by betty.k » Fri Aug 21, 2009 8:03 am

was at the local track/toy shop tonight and i spotted a cool looking 4ch counter rotating heli that i couldn't resist!



dunno what scale you call it, the blades are 7.5" in diameter. brand name is "nine eagles" so i'm guessing it's some chinese company we'll never see again!

in the air it's pretty damn stable, the controls do what they're told. i guess for the most part it's no different from other micro helis of this class but there are a few things about it that impressed me.

the radio is 2.4ghz for starters, i haven't pushed it too far but it hasn't skipped a beat. there's a neato lcd display that shows battery charge, trim points and a numerical throttle display (but who looks at the tx while flying?!)


the big worry about most tx's is whether it's mode 1 or mode 2. but not with this little bugger! simply loosen 2 screws, flip the aerial the other way and turn the tx upside down! fucken genius!!
the removed cover is where you poke the battery for charging.


another thing i thought was cool was the intergrated servos. they're basically a linear type deal, a worm gear is used for movement with (i assume) a linear pot for reference.



and the crowning glory........



good ol' glow in the dark bits!


but you'd be a darn fool kid if you flew a heli in the dark!

okay after flying this thing almost all day, i've found out a couple things about this.

One, one of the motors doesn't quite spin up as quick as the other, so that's likely bad lubrication inside the motor, leading to higher energy consumption and therefore shorter flight times. they also get HOT.

two, the flybar linkage is quite tight, so a sanding the ball a little frees it up nicely.
