Post by sidewinder » Wed Feb 25, 2009 2:52 am

Looks like a really good start. One thing that will help when its time to paint is sandable primer. it will help get any small nicks and scratches that you might not notice before painting. I'm not the one to give much paint advice, but I do know that the more prep time you put in the better it will come out. Also, be sure to spray a bunch of really thin layers, the first should be almost transparent, and be sure to wait between coats, most paints will recomend the proper length of time between coats. If you have trouble shaping anything some automotive filler putty will work great, or you can get green putty at almost any hobby shop. I know I'm in the minority here but I like Krylon paint if you are using a rattle can, and House of Kolor paint if you use an airbrush, but Testors will work fine too.

Anyways, good start and keep us updated.


edit: And, if Crazy Dave corrects me on any of that I'd go with his opinion since he's the pro here.
