Post by payaso » Sun Jun 24, 2007 9:20 pm

Lookin good so far man.

When jumping, try to let off the throttle while in the air.

See, these trucks are heavy enough, and have enough rotational mass to steer/control them in the air.

Keeping the gas on while in the air, will raise the front end up, and if you peg it with enough air, it'll back flip.

Hitting the brakes while in the air will bring the front end back down, and with enough air, will make you front flip.

For a nice, and level landing with the front end up. Just tap the brakes.

For a level landing with the nose down, blip the throttle, and it'll correct it.

ALWAYS let off the gas/brake before landing, as this will put a hefty load on your diffs. Yep, it'll destroy em.
