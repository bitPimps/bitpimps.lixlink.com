Post by Son_Gokou » Sat May 08, 2004 6:06 pm

I've heard that, for those who don't have a pinion puller, a good way to get the pinion off is as follows:

Obtain a steel, STRONG AND STURDY butter knife which you don't plan on using anymore...(well, hopefully not for buttering.) Cut a little notch in it, big enough to fit around the leg that holds the pinion, but make sure it's small enough to hold the pinion....This way, the pinion gear should be on top of the notch and the rest of the motor beneath it. Support it on some high, strong surfaces next to each other. Take a hammer and whack the nub that sticks out (pervs! ) of the brass gear until it sinks into it...Then take a screwdriver, or something thin and strong and push the armature leg all the way out of the pinion gear, it may also help to heat it up to allow the arm to slip out easily...


Just a quick tip....
