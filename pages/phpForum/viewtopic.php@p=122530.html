Well, it's going for $70, I pocket $50. $25 for the body, 2 sheets of Parma masking film at $5 a pop, a pack Exacto blades, $3, then there's the 8 colors of paint, and the last of my $10 can of Spaz Stix, I'm barely breaking even. I get nothing for my 25 hours of work.

I made the mistake of letting him set the price, and yeah I could've demanded more, but not only is that not my a style, but I was so shocked, I didn't know what to say, I just wanted to get the hell out of there and hide. Then I come home and see the guys on the RC Driver forum hated it. I'm pretty profoundly depressed right now. Shit like this makes me feel doomed to a life of making pizzas for shit for money.

I can't help but to think this is karma smacking me down for going off on Shaun yesterday. I didn't mean to, I feel bad. He just annoys me. I can't help it, but I know that's not it, because life has a habit of smacking me down every time I think I'm good at something.

At any rate, I'm not painting any more bodies unless all materials are supplied, and they kiss my ass and tell me how cool I am.

EDIT: Well one dude said he'd by it, and this guy Evil3 who is a really good painter, I've seen his work in Parma ads, gave me some really good constructive criticism, but said it was a great paint job. So that makes me feel better. He was saying it should go in the range of $55 plus the cost of the body which is right where I was shooting for. I thought I would get more since I supplied the body. But it was a freebie body, so I'm going to take the crack dealer approach, and just let them know, I let them slide for the first one, since I had the extra body, but they need to provide the body next time, and I still need to make sure I get $45-50 in my pocket. I think that sounds fair. I need the practice, but I aint painting for free!
