Okay, I really don't want to lead anyone astray here, I am NOT necessarily endorsing these cars. It certainly is a Shen Hurricane. I reckon I got this one in 2005 for AU$10 posted (at the time maybe US$4???). They are dual cell, already have headlights, magnet steering, a pretty nice bullbar and from memory tracked pretty okay (adjustable steering). They aren't all fairy farts and rainbows but, I'm pretty sure mine is over(under???)geared to shit, and the rear gear box is a unit making it REALLY tough to change the motor or gearing... This one was on the way to enter the end of year competition, but with the rear gear-pod and oddball chassis, I just couldn't find a good place to cut, no matter how hard I tried!

Honestly the HIGHLIGHT of this little truck is the easy-swappable wheels IMO.
If you want to wait like 2 days, I will charge up a set of AA's and do a full review with video... But only if people are interested.
US$15 for two is a pretty good deal as long as you realise that they are still pretty gutless. I would grab that, but only if I saw it in a shop and could buy them with change in my wallet...
