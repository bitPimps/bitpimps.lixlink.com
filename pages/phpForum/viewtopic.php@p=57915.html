Sorry to reply so late in the game, but I found out that 3xAAA (4.5v) overloaded my nimh batteries in three cars, luckily very little leaked out and the cars still run just as good as they should. Yeah, 3v is much better for the job, as I found when I rewired the terminals of the outboard charger to accept two batteries like it should. Now all I gots to do is get a 3v DC adapter between 700 and 1000ma, and I should be set.

I fixed my 49mhz controller myself, although I suck balls when it comes to soldering. I could use a new charging plate though.

Also, my 45mhz chassis rear axle mounts were filled with superglue and resurfaced, so it takes a 12:1 gear without locking up. (The car is single speed, Red normal TOMY suspension bar, 2.8 orange turbo ZZ motor- which I didn't know until recently that the cap's metal shroud came off, 200mah 1.3v battery, LXX Perfection LoPro rims and tires from the Phantom Series, good for torque runs).
