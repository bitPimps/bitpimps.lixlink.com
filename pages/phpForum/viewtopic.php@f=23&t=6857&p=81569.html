I had thought about getting some but never did. Looks real good Pimp
To tell the truth the chrome tape is Hell to work with on something that small! I have used it on models and it works real good with flat surfaces the best but the fenders were curved so its like trying to cover a basketball with paper and it come out smooth. I actually removed the first attempt and this is the second.

The flash on the camera shows every line and imperfection in the tape.

I used model material called Bare-Metal Ultra Bright chrome it is actually metal tape that is extreamly thin.
