Motor Home RV Bit Custom

  • Quote

Post by steelwoolghandi » Sat Apr 09, 2005 11:31 pm

I had this idea while looking at my RC Simi Truck and I thought what a cool chasse for a Motor Home so I said what the heck and drew one out!

I had plenty of polystyrene so I drew the RV then transferred it over to the polystyrene. Then I measured everything and cut out the roof that piece made the front back and roof all out of one piece no cutting.
I cut out the windows and door and added trim using Evergreen styrene then I used Tamiya putty and finished out the bad spots. I have it almost ready to be primed. After primer I will fix any of the bad spots and then add the rest of the detail.

I even plan to have an awning that rolls out from the side mirrors, windows and even air-conditioning units at the top. And it will not be just for show but a working RC!
