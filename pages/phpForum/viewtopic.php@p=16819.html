Everybody tell little ol' me what you really think.
















I made this for the contest at RCmod.com and entered it today. A lot of work went into it.
Mods.
Stretched bB Body to ZZ Length (two cuts)
Headlights (Green) and Taillights (Red) Black electrical tape prevents glow of lights from anywhere except where I wanted them.
Front Air Dam
Side Skirts
Testers Dark Blue Acrylic Paint.
Yellow Stripes are Dry Erase from Pine Car decal set.
Toyota decals from 24th scale slot car set for GTP.
Tailpipes custom made from aluminum tubing.
Krylon Clear Gloss Acrylic covers whole thing.
Window tinting by Sharpie.

It took me two weeks to get it to where I wanted it for the contest.
