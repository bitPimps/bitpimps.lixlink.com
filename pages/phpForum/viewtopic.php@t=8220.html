Thought I would post my ride in here too.

I'll Just post the pics. Someone asked what Kind of body I used. And this thing really was built custom. I got the Fenders off of a 1/43 scale cheapy hot rod (obviously I had to chop and resize them to make them fit. Then I got the Trunk part from a 1993 red Macdonald's cadillac hotwheels car. The side doors are plastic with putty over top sanded down. Roll bars are putty too. There are two small metal parts. The rim around the window is metal and so is the rim around the grill. The wheels are from a D-rod. And use the method on described on this forum. For the front wheels you may notice that they're not drilled through. Big secret there is that I put the pin in the other wayand glued the inside of the wheel making sure the wheels still spin. And the Interior actually has real leather and real carpet. AS for extending the Chassis I got that Idea from Sat4life I believe. I just made a clean cut down the chassis right after the charging terminals and glued the PCB end to end with the Chassis Parts. Then glued plastic for the sides puttied over and sanded it smooth. as for the matching homie, I actually had the car painted purple then went out and hit the candy machines and bought a homie. Turns out he was going to be the Purple Tennis shoepimp. I was like holy shit is that lucky.







Yes I did talk a lot about this ride. So I had to try to walk the walk. This being my first Mod and my only Bit. I was scared shitless to try anything. In the end this forum and everyone who is part of it gave me the confidence to do all the mods that I did. I tried to pull of a mod that would be at least somewhat comparable to your guys first mods.

Anyhow this post has been Way to long. Once again I would just like to say Thanks to everyone for being such a big help
Bart

P.S Ill try to get pics of the Chassis. I didnt think of getting one at the time, now i gotta get a hold of a Digi cam again.. but I will try to get a pic of it
