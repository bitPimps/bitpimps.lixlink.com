Do the NSSM (No Screw Shock Mod) for overlands, it's basically removal of the pins in the shock, and cutting the top shock body back, hot glueing the springs to the top and bottom parts of the shock, and viola, you have a very soft suspension set-up, not limiting flex, and you will also get some droop, keeping the COG lower.
