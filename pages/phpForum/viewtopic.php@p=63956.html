Post by stampede_dude » Wed Jan 26, 2005 8:16 pm


A truck i snagged off ebay cheap. originally had a 13 turn motor with stock gearing and clod tires with all stock parts except the esc and the motor.(how stupid was this dude?)i got it off ebay cheap, sold off the clod wheels and adapters. bought some 5x11 carriers and bearings and topped it all off with some off road gearing, about 12/87.i bought a new servo also(traxxas stock on the pede sucks) a futaba s3003 or something like that, not the best but it gets the job done for only $10 from tower. i bought some crap packs and then before Christmas i got the masher 2ks and stock rims in place of the crappy original mashers i originally had on it. slapped a gp3300 in it and it wouldnt stay on the ground. knowing a brushed motor wouldnt last long i traded him my combo for his jr sc-1 esc and a 17t amber plus 30 bucks from him . recently reprgrammed everything and i did a little trading and bought a new hitec agressor reciver and transmitter off of ebay before then. spent the dough i saved for a mini t. somewhere in the mix i scored the gpm aluminum adjustable shocks and put an aluminum ideler in the tranny and a slipper cluth and some revo body pins. and made a custom esc mount. enjoy...
