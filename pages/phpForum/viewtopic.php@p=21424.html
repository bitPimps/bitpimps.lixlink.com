Post by crazydave » Sun Aug 10, 2003 9:33 am

I used pens for everything on my alloys.

I cut down some pen tips to make bushings to adapt the new wheels to my axles.




And then my 10mm rims are way too wide to allow the car to turn, so I made some lo-pro tires out of pen grips. I used a thinner section with lines on it for the front, and the a thicker slick piece for the rears.

If anyone wants to try this, I used a Pentel G2 mechanical pencil, because it was about 50 cents cheaper than the pens.

CaboWabo wrote:nice dave!
great idea on using the pen tips!
how'd that work out? just glue the axle into the pen tip?
Yep, that's what I did. It works out great, but it was kinda a pain in the ass finding tips of the right size. I found those fancy advertising pens for prescription drugs, that nurses always have, are the perfect size, but they don't want to give those up so you gotta steal them. One of the tips I used was from a fancy Paper-Mate, so if you can find refills w/ a plastic barrel for Paper-Mate pens, those should work. Probably not the most convenient way to go about it, but it's what I had around, and they're the perfect size.

Oh yeah, and I'm using the narrow part that fits into the barrel for the bushings.
