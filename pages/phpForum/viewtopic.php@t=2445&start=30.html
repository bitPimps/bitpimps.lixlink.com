Ok, I know I'm dragging up an old thread but I've really gotten into my daul cell monster bit fet modded beast and now I'm looking for the fastest motor possible.

Some observations:

The EM3.8 is the best motor in this beast. At 50% speed the car runs quite fast and is comparable to a single cell 3.0-3.5. At 100% speed (boost) this car just flies! I cannot say it enough how fast it goes.

I tried the perfection 3.8 in this same car and I found the performance comparable. I couldn't really tell if it was a bit slower or not but I *think* the perfection didn't run as fast in 50% speed mode as the EM38 vanquish motor did. Still in 100% boost the machine flys again!

I then tried the perf 4.2 King in this beast and I noticed that the top speed was crap! This is in 50% speed mode. The top speed in this mode was less than 3/4 the speed of the 3.8's (EM and perf) in 50% mode. When I went to 100% mode id did pick up speed but not to the same level as the 3.8's.

So my question is this: should I buy the bitchar-gmotor from tinyrc? Is this motor the fastest motor out there?
