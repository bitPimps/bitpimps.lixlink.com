Post by crazydave » Mon Jun 08, 2009 5:35 pm

Ass of these motor have huge variances, so any one of them could happen to be faster than the others, but in my experience, the Perfection motors have the most torque, and are the most reliable. The BCG 3.8 has the most potential to be the fastest, but is weak on the torque, this motor really works best with FETs, I've also wore out 2 out of 2 of these motors very quickly, I'm not sure why. The R-Spec is very similar, but with slightly more torque and slightly less speed, and while it is rated as a 2.4 it may be a tad faster than the Perfection motors.
