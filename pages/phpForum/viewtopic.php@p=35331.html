Hi!

@Hue35 - I work in Sales and studied Economy and Marketing. I'm just using pictures to make up for black holes in my english

@TheEvo - Camera is Olympus 5050Z

Taking good pictures is difficult in our mini hobby. Closeups, difficult light conditions makes cars on photos worse than in reality.

As crazydave said: "it's all about the lighting", so first advice; use one type of light (same or close temperature). I took Ferrari pictures wit 3 types of lihgt and couldn't set white balance properly. This picture was taken using Logitech Quick Cam and table lamp - colors are more natural.



After taking a photo you can improve it on the computer. For example clean up the noise

As as example i used Hiroto34 image, i hope it wont be angry at me

For cleaning pictures i use NeatImage, free version which you can download here: http://www.neatimage.com/download.html
