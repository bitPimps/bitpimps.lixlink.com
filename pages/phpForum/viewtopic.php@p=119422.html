Alright, what's frizzen going on about now?

The Matchbox Power Scouts are a pretty viable option for ZZ-M bodies. Plastic, decent detailing, pretty good scaling. They're about 1/8" wider, and about 1/4" longer than a zz-m body. Wheelbase is a bit longer than I'd like, but it's not too bad.

Around $7, they have a motorized rigid 4x4 chassis, working winch is a joke, tires would be zz-m height without the lugs, fake beadlock rims, haven't torn one apart yet to view guts... But honestly, it's the bodies.
