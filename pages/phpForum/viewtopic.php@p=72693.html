I bought one of those wacky Stunt Cars a while back and proceeded to take it apart as soon as I got it home. Well the body has been sitting on my worktable for months I got to looking at it the other day and said “Self can you make anything out of it?”

“Self I think I can make something” so I proceeded to start chopping it up! I then found an old PT cruiser body and removed the fenders. I then glued the fenders on the body and removed the front window. I am planning on making an old 40’s car hauler out of it. I have already got a hauler bed for it and a Simi Truck chasse. I will post updates, as I go along will see if the Turd can be the Word ?

Ok been working on the turd and I am getting close to the end. Here is the completed sanded body (well completed to a point). I had to do a lot of filling and sanding to get the finish I wanted, I used Car paint to finish it off. I wheels on the chassis are muscle machine wheels off a Hauler truck and the back flat bed is from a HO scale flatbed truck I added some styrene to widen it to fit.

You will notice the front droops that is because the body is just sitting on the chassis I do not have the brackets done yet also the bed is just sitting on the chassis no brackets on that ether. All in all it is going better then expected I am working on the front window that will hinge and open forward like many of the old trucks had before air-conditioning.

I don’t have an idea for the front headlights yet so any suggestions would be welcome!
