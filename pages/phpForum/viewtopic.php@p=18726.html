Post by ph2t » Mon Sep 08, 2003 8:24 pm

Game Over!

I've officialy stopped development of this one 'cause of a few reasons:

1) The problems holding production weren't solved, bugger * tricky*. bugger *too hard*, bugger *too slack*.
2) The whole extra pcb thing is good in principle, but ultimatly takes too much room.
3) When the mosfets are put in you get a 3V output from the RX chip vs. the 0.7V output with the original BJT transistors. This voltage increase is due to the fundamental differnce between BJT's and FET's. 3V is still enough to properly turn on the FET's, I trying some different model FET's now that work well at 3V.

I still want to tweak/experiement with this mod and I will, just not in it's current from.

Keep your eyes peeled....
