Finks...you are right, those have been out for quite some time now. I bought three from toyeast. Quality control seems to be pretty good with them, the detailing is nice, light up headlights, and exchangeable roofs.

The controllers can be set to full speed or two speed (with use of a booster button). They are also set up to run slower when turning. I assume this is to provide control during cornering. You may or may not like that.

They seem to work well, although one of mine didn't seem to want to run straight. I pulled the body off and it ran straight without the body. The bodies are hard as hell to get off and I cracked the shell near the bumber trying to get it off.

The packaging is pretty nice too, if you are in to collecting like me. They are supposedly limited edition and I know Toyeast is already out of a lot of them, so if you want one, you may want to get it soon. They are cool enough to get one or two.. something different. I'm considering modding oput the one I cracked.
