Wall-e Action figure to R/C...

  • Quote

Post by Sporttracguy » Sun Aug 09, 2009 10:39 pm

So a while back I picked up one of these Wall-e action figures with two sets of interchangeable rubber treads. It sat on my shelf for some time and then I got motivated to turn it into an r/c toy. I used the gears and electronics from one of those fly wheels rc toys

This thing has tons of torque and is a tad top heavy but works great. I took a short video so you get the idea. I still need to attach the back panel and make the battery permanent. Probably a little over powered, its currently running on a dual lipo cell.
Next on the list is to make the eyes glow blue...



Left the Coke can in there for scale.

This video is still loading so hopefully it works.
