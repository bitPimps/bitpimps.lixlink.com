OK so some of you may remember the green Stang I made for SG for his Rippers green contest. It was an extreme quicky job that took only a few hours and was all hand painted.



Well I decided that this was going to either rot in my tool box or I could send it to SG as he would probably appreciate it more. Course I am a cheap bastard and sent it in a manilla envelope from work for free figuring what the hell, this should be just fine. NOT, this is how he got it.



So I felt kind of bad, so I just made him a new green ride. It is Auto Modellista inspired in it's paint and has rolled down windows and a sunroof just for kicks. Hand painted (cuz lord knows I wouldn't spend money on a rattle can of green paint) but it turned out supprisenly well.





So I will be mailing out this shell in the next couple of days. I was thinking on the way down to work today about going full on Auto Modellista and putting a circle logo on the hood w/ "SG" in it. Trust me, it isn't a flawless shell, but I am tempted to make another for my collection. Fun quick job.

:edit: Looking at the pics you can't really see it, but trust me, the windows on the sides are taken out to look like they are rolled down as well as the sun roof.

Thanks afrin. As for how I did the paint, the trick is the right sized brush and doing your strokes VERY fast and accurate. if you go back to fix somthing after 1 min it will just fuck it up. There is also 2 layers which was important. Thin coats as we all know is the secret to a great looking paint job.

The Windows were all very easily done w/ a dremmel and then touch up w/ an exacto knife.
