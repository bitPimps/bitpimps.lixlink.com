49mhz MicroSizer Pro Booster Chassis
Custom Dual Antenna's
Custom Rims
Custom Decals
Perfection Gold Finned Heatsink
Tomy 3.0 Lucifer Motor
Stock Orange Gearing


I used the Tamiya Green that Hiroto sent me and some Testors Green Flake on the Windows.

I originally didn't set out to paint the windows, but a blooper on my part forced me too.

I also had plans for a stock sun roof, but the same blooper caused me to remove it. I rubbed all the stock paint off the top of the body with nail polish remover to get a clear roof.

The over all blooper was that I put the coats on a little too think and when I removed the masking, it got all messed up.

For the antenna's I used a Xmod antenna and cut it in half and the bent it into shape and soldered it onto the PCB. (It was a pain in the ass to solder these onto it. They just wouldn't stay in place.)

The rims were painted with the body color and Testors gloss black.
