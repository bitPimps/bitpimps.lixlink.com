Received my MRC this weekend (shipped USPS and arrived Sunday, figure that one!). Thanks Smoke&Mirrors, so far this thing is tons of fun.

So far I've crawled it around the living room and over my son's big mess of toys. Attempts were made to crawl the dog, but he wasn't having it.

Now that I have one, what I love most about Crawlers is it's you against whatever obstacle you setup (or go to), no need to have other people to race against (which is a real boon for me, since all my buds are married with children and busy as hell all the damned time!) to have a ton of fun. AND, you don't need a lot of room and it actually works great in a messy-assed house crawling over all the junk laying around (such as at my home!).

Now I'm already looking to upgrade...The gearing that is. Can anyone suggest what I can get to actually SLOW this thing down even more? I want this thing to seriously CRAAAAAWL, not to mention whenever my 3 1/2 year old son sees me driving it he immediately wants it and does nothing more than either gun it FWD or REV...So the slow-down would help out there too.
