Post by FeRReT of WaR » Mon Jun 30, 2003 4:21 pm

ok ill make a quick review of it.
well for starters the asking price is $18. a bit high but its worth it.
ok the body is nicely painted with nos logos and other brands, one thing that i dislike about the paint job is here and there, there are little bad spots.
onto the motor. its pretty fast faster than my MS pro. i think that is because its a dual cell. the battery life is about 8 minutes its not accurate because i used crappy charger batteries because it takes x6 AAA batteries. 3 for controller 3 for charger. it has a turbo button but the bad thing is that the turbo does not work in reverse.
ok now onto the drive line. the tires are kinda hard so that makes the traction suffer. well it does nice on velvet pool table . i have no idea on the gearing i dont want to open the gear box up. the whole motor and gears are on a little box, held together with 4 screws. the motor is soldered in with two wires .the steering is ok it works just fine. it does not have the steering radious of a microsizer, but it can do the job. i needs one thing modded to it, weight added to the back so it does not jump around when you throttle it up.
all in all its a great buy. you can do so many body mods to it since its true 1/64 scale. not much room for anything else.
i give it a 3/5
quickoverview,
good: fast, dual cell, true 1/64, nice paintjob, good run time.
bad: soldered in motor, not so good handleing, $17.
