Whazz up Pimps!

Just got mine in the mail today!
Purple '70 Cuda, 49 MHz

Pros:
- beautiful Muscle Machine styling
- awesome detail (exhust & susp. springs on the chassis bottom!)
- great looking controller (color coordinated with the car!)
- removable charger/battery case (just like the NASCAR Motorworks)
- nice long retractable antenna (14" compared to 9.5" on a Super Bit)

Cons:
- mine has very bad range (not quite 2 feet! )
- hard rubber tires (lots of wheel spin on hard surfaces)
- big turn radius (hard tires don't help here)

Other than the range problem, these things are NICE! Hopefully they're not all like mine (2ft range!). Maybe mine slipped through quailty control....

OK, I had to open the car up. Looking for range!!!

Well, I couldn't tell if there were any crip caps but.....

What I found:
- 110mAH batt
- a second motor is used for steering! That's right no magnet! It sits right down the middle of the chassis, next to the batt. It's hard wired. I think there's possiblities for proportional steering.... anyone up to it?
- The drive motor is under a plastic shield. I had a hard time trying to remove it. I left it alone since I didn't want to screw it up by breaking something (it's my only one!)! I'm going to assume it's also hardwired.

Sorry I don't have a digital cam... Otherwise you'd be looking at pics instead of reading about it!

Opened up the controller too. Nothing special here.
I found out why I didnt' have any range.... broken antenna wire on the inside! WOO HOO! Easy fix!
