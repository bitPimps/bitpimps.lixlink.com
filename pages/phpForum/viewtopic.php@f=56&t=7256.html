I'm digging my Xmod Evo truck. After getting bearings, stage 2 motor, and the off road kit, I've been having some fun, and getting it scuffed up. So now that the new has worn off, it time to make it mine.

Anyone that has one of these might notice my influence from the BFG ad in the little Truckin' mag that comes with it. Funny thing is when I went to buy paints, I forgot it was red, and wondered why I needed another shade of blue, so I skipped it, and used the Metal Flake Blue that I had. I got a Light Champagne Metallic for the bottom. I'm really happy with the way it looks, but when I went back and looked at the ad again, I saw it was actually red. That's ok, I like my blue better anyways.

Wanted to take pics out in the dirt and sunlight, but I screwed around too much after work today, and ran out of sunlight. I'll do outdoor pics tomorrow, but I couldn't wait to show it off, so here's a couple crappy snapshots.
