As long as they are the 1/43rd scale. The only thing to look out for is the wheel sizes. The larger wheels or wheels without the low profile tires will require fender trimming. You can swap the tires around too on like wheel sizes to get the low profile tires on whatever rims you want. The only ones not compatable with each other are the larger hub truck wheels to the smaller hub car wheels.
