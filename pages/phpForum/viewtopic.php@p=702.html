The base is made of black foam board....the greens are felt paper, the rails are a mix of foam board for the straights and plastic corrugated board for the corners. The design is printed out on paper... Whats nice about foam board is that its lightweight...i designed the track so i can fold it like a binder for easy transport... The dimensions i havnt measured it yet...but its roughly 8ft long and 4 feet wide. I'm still putting up some fences on the straightaway and some grandstands too...i guess im about 60% done...
