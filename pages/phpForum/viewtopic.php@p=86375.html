What track are you guys running your bits on? I'd love to see some pics if you could.

I think the best thing about bits is having a racing partner. My brother and my neighbor had races almost every other day, and each day it got more and more competitive. We tried so many different types of races.

1 race we used to love was called "Winner Overlap". To win you have to knock everyone out, and by doing so you would have to take the lead and then overlap someone. Once that person was overlapped it was "good bye charlie". This is crazy with three people because who ever is in last you see them struggle to catch up and let their nerves take over, while the guy in first is focused on catching that ass.


Another game we used to play was a bit different. We used to have team racing.

For example since I loved my Murcielago soo much I was always
"TEAM LAMBO" and my brother and neighbor loved the F50 and F40 bodies, so yes they were "RED DEVIL FERRARI's"

It was always me and my little cousin against neighbor and brother. It is a one on one race to see who completes the most laps around the track. 1 Partner races, the other SNIPES!!! We used to include those "BDAMAN" marble shootin toys. We put in the less damaging balls and the bodies never took a beating. Your partner would place a Bdaman on a selected turn, and the other teams sniper somewhere else. So while you race you have obstacles. This was by far the most skilled racing we ever did. And this type of racing also caused the most fist fights ahha...

Moral of my long ass story, cherish your racing partners forever. You can modd all you want, put loads of speed in them, but if you cant use and enjoy them in a race against other people....IT IS NEVER THE SAME!!!

DT and CD I think it is truly pimp how you get your kids involved in the racing!!!
