Shen Qi Wei, Finally a review

  • Quote

Post by Finks » Wed Aug 24, 2005 4:19 pm

OK so I beat out DT on a brand spankin new Shen Qi Wei
(naw, just kiddin, I bid first and he being the true pimp he is, let me have it, mad props bro ) So it came today, and all I can say is, Not too shabby. "Not too shabby" you say???? Not OFMG these clones are FtW!!@!!!11! or Good Lawd the best clone EVER!!!!!!!!!! Well, no, not because they couldn't be, but I haven't owned an LXX (coming in the mail) and it's my understanding those are the reigning king of clones.

Now don't get me wrong, this thing is nice, hell, it's VERY nice, but it ends there. As far as clones go it's the total package without becoming revolutionary. Here, lets just get down to it.

Obviously the first thing you notice is the crazy ball shell this thing comes in. It is by no means ground breaking, but it is cool that it stores the car on the charger. Also to note, little lights are located on the side of it's clear shell every time you hit a button. But the nicest thing about the controller is that the car snaps into it so solidly. That's a big thing that erks me w/ clone controllers, a lot of the time the damn car just doesn't pop in right or is a pain in the ass to keep the tabs constantly connected while it charges.

As for the cons, well there aren't any. At fist I thought it odd to use with the lid up, but hell we do that w/ Zip zaps. This thing doesn't make you look manly, even tho it uses playing cards suits as the directionals on the controller. But wtf, we are in a hobby that's target audience is 10-15 year olds.... OK here's a pic



Now for the reason these things have gotten so much attention, the shells. Well again, they are ... Nice, very nice. I got a Ferrari (I think... correct me if I am wrong and as to what model it is) and lets just say, all that can be easily fixed is absolutely heinous, and all that a modder really wants in a shell is all there (with the exception of the opaque windows)

Pros, The detail and precision is spot on. I have yet to see better. IT sits perfectly on the chassis and all the small details are all there. These have SO Much modding potential. Also, I don't think I have ever seen a shell like the one I got before, could it be unique?

The cons, yeah, #1 the ghey ass stickers, all clones have em, and they all need to be removed ASAP. #2 who the fuck would paint a car like this with such a disgusting color scheme. Re fuckin tarted, but that's all right, this car wasn't ment to be displayed on my shelf without a custom paint job anyhow. More pics.


Yes it's that gay Note: this is the cover of one of my kids coloring books, hehe, dirty dirty gnomes


Ahhh that's a bit better


The Ass shot

OK now for what I honestly know very little about, the chassis. This car runs great. Has a very nice stock speed that would own on a custom track. Not too fast, not too slow. it can almost pop a wheelie when slammed into reverse then going forward. It tracks perfectly straight and the tires are made of a nice composite (note the word nice is used a lot in this review) The run time is almost 2 min on the dot and yeah, that's the end of all I know. Thought I would be helpful to those that actually know about this shit and snap a pic of the PCB for you to put your $.02 on.


All I can tell you here is that.. ummm it's green



So, in conclusion, this is a very nice clone, if you can find it at a decent clone price, buy it, for sure, you will not be disappointed. Most importantly tho, as far as I can tell in the two hours I have owned it, you are getting bit char-g quality out of a clone. Other than the opaque windows they didn't skimp on craftsmanship. Also if you have read this far.... Wow, congrats
