Post by sidewinder » Mon Jul 09, 2007 5:34 pm

Looks good, try taking your exacto knife and trimming the decals just before they get to the end of the body otherwise the edges get pulled up. Also if they cross and indents (like door/hood lines trim it a little on each side so there is no air gap at the line to pull up the stickers. Finaly one thing most people forget is no matter how clean the car looks before applying the decal wash it with a grease stripping detergent (like dawn dish soap) and rinse at least twice...let air dry and try not to touch where the sticker will go or finger grease will make the bond weak. Or you could really dive in and paint the stripes...see the tutorials for flames etc. some of these guys really know their shit (and how to paint too).
