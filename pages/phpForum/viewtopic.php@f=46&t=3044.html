Post by hogjowlz » Mon Jan 26, 2004 2:35 pm





first of all they call them superslicks but they come with treaded tires?

not too much info out there on them. well after taking a little break from rc stuff i broke down and got one of these.

looks = well its a little bigger and a lot better detail on the body. it is more to scale than bits or zipzaps and sits pretty low already. paint is nice stock(good sprakly metallic).

electronics = dual cell + room for another 2 or 3 cells easily. non propo but the steering is motorized rack and pinion and it works very well. runtime is very very very good.

motor = torque/ stock the speed is ok. torque is way beyond any bit motor.

mechanics = must be made by same manufacturer as zipzaps. very similar quality

controller = kinda strange but it works. separate charger(3aaa) plugs into the bottom of the controller(runs on 9v). range is better than any microsizer.


overall = im happy with it. plenty of room for electronic tinkering and mechanically its sound but should be easy to improve. driving is fun out of the box with plenty of power.
