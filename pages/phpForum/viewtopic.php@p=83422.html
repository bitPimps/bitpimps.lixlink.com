CD, no they don't come with off road kit (about $15 to get one).
It's pretty good in stock form. They come in two length options H2 shorter, F-150 longer.
MA, I'm yet to try to fit car body on it (it looks so good and detailed i wouldn't wanna change anything)
Now, in comparison to OL.... OL can suck dick.
$60 vs. $150. 4WD vs. 2WD. hot motor like SO3 vs. X-Speed. You decide.
I spend $120 and got : the truck, bearings, 4wd, lift kit and lots of fun.
I used to have OL, cool little bugger but no comparison to this one. On OL
I broke motor pod mounts front and rear, disintegrated bunch of gears including alloy( all on first month) . It's electronics are in my drifter Xmod.
Xmods evo truck does have it's weak points like front suspention gets funky with lift kit(stiffer springs fixed that), rear pivoting point gets stuck if ran in the sand( some plastic cover fixed that too).
Xmods propo controls are far from best but there is always iwaver to help out. Once alloy parts are available for evo truck OL is toast.
It is fresh still, so i'm yet to see the damage i can inflict on it but so far so good. I'll get pics of the chassis in a day or two.
