Post by crazydave » Sat Jun 21, 2003 5:43 pm

Thanks for the compliments guys.

I got to tell ya how this video came about.

See I was looking at that Celica body, and theorizing that the little exhaust pipe is what was keeping it from riding out wheelies. So I cut it off, and Bam! Instant wheelies. So I had to grab the camera, but then I thought just doing wheeies back and forth doesn't make for a very interesting video, so I grabbed some cardboard and taped it up. I had taken a bunch of shots, that I thought was cooler than the ones that made it in the vid, but when I went edit it all together I found I was saving wrong and lost everything. So now it's late and I have to go to bed, so I just went and snapped off some shots real fast and took what I got. I just thought it was funny that I'm sitting staring at my car winding down for the night, I get a little idea, and I'm up half the night editing video.

I also got to tell ya, I've had this camera all along, but it's just a $200 digicam, and the quicktime videos is just a bonus feature. So I figured it wasn't very good as a video camera, and was waiting to see if I could borrow my MIL's digital video camera, but I've since found out they paid like $4000 for it, and no ones allowed to touch it. So with that idea out, I was forced to use my digicam, and I actually think that I get pretty good results considering what I'm using, and it's natural room lighting. If I could get a hold of a photo flood, I think I might actually get really nice results.
