dual battery charging on normal controller

  • Quote

Post by hogjowlz » Tue Feb 25, 2003 12:14 pm

i have a thread about this on bit science on tinyrc but ill put this crap here anyhow.

we got a prototype going with jumpers instead of a switch. its fairly simple to do compared to the normal dual battery mod except you run a few extra wires. so it can be run in either series or parallel. essentially you have two leads for each config. so a total of four wires going to a row of 4 jumpers. you put one jumper in the middle two pins for series. you put two jumpers for parallel. in parallel you can charge off of a 2aa controller. it just takes a while and then you take the one jumper out and put the other into series and you are off. also with no jumpers the batteries wont discharge so you will always have a fully charged ride.[/img]
