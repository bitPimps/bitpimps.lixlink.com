Post by spooky » Mon Sep 18, 2006 1:37 am

I have the Xterra and H1 MZGT. These are 1/50 scale, a little bigger than a ZZSE. Actually for trucks they are about the correct scale size compared to a ZZ car.

These are ZZSE 3rd generation and quite a bit faster than my ZZSEs but the ZZSEs were all bought used so they may be first and second gen. My MZGTs have pretty poor reception but the controller is a lot nicer the the ZZSE.

A cat 5 might fix it or some better batteries. AAAs made a huge difference to my SSMT143 stock electronics when I did that swap.

Due to their size these will fit an Iwaver PCB nicely and they may also fit the ZZMT with articulation mod better than a stock lid.

These are a lot smaller than a SS or Epoch.
