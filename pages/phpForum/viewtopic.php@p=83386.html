Now presenting, from "The Dirty Bird Workshop", Hachi-Ryoku's very own formula race car. We call this one "Pimp in a hurry"!



1. Lipoly batt (kokam 140 mah)
2. fetts
3. SS blue motor (30,000rpm)
4. K.I.T. gears; 3:1 teal (high speed)
5. back wheels are slot car solongies (foam tires with black silicon/extremely sticky)
6. front wheels are pull-back wheels
7. brass antenna (just felt like it)
8. streched chassis
9. body is a HO gauge shell (very light)
10. custom paint job
11. custom decals
12. on/off switch
13. mini plug/connection system for easier/more reliable charging
14. 35mhz booster PCB

I think that about covers it. The whole car is extremely light. Definately the fastest car I've made.

I am going to do my best to get video footage of this thing. I have to. To not show you all first hand how fast this thing is would be a crime. I can't even attempt to run it around my kitchen boosted. So far I haven't had a place to get enough space for it. That's ok though. I'm gonna take it to the clinic I work at and try my digital camera out. It may not work out well, but it's worth a shot. The large patient waiting area after hours is prime racing terrain.

Will enter in the comp as soon as I get video.
