Post by richh » Wed Oct 13, 2004 9:30 pm

ok, i've had a little more time to play with the truck. some more comments.

- the 21K motor is definitely lacking in the torque dept. does not like climbing obstacles with it unless you get a running start.
- you can charge the truck w/o having to put the controllers antenna down, why you can't do this with a zzse still boggles my mind
- there's a little spring washer that goes on between the wheel and the nut; this things a royal pain in the ass to get on.
- ground clearance is pretty poor right by the wheels (cause of the gear on one side in back and the steering blocks/knuckles in the front)
- the steering motor needs an upgrade in the torque; when you hold the truck up in the air the wheels turn a lot more than when on the ground.
- pops a nice wheelie if you do the reverse to forward thing on carpet and fresh charge

to answer some of your questions...
-steering is not propo, turn radius is probably between a foot and a half an 2 feet though i'm guessing with a stronger steering motor it would be less (see above comment)
-tires are hollow, a little on the stiff side. decent grip though (just look at the pics and how much crap sticks to them)
- yea, the the flame sticker on the hood is a big ?; the sides are printed or painted, why couldn't they do the hood?

i hope you aren't getting the wrong impression, this thing is fun but it ain't no mega articulating, boulder climbing, 1/64th txt or tmaxx. think more along the lines of a 4wd zz with lots o' torque, big wheels to go with it, and a decent looking body. still worth the $25 in my opinion.
