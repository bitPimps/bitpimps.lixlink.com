Post by CaboWabo » Wed Jul 16, 2003 8:34 am

I've been looking all over these boats for a good place to get in the boat.
You can tell the boats were molded in 2 pieces, a top and a bottom. The bottom includes the majority of the boat. It seems as if they heated the plastics and fused them together. There are no tell tale creases or cut lines from where the plastic would have met (telling us its probably been heated and fused).
The spots where the two plastic pieces have been joined are not going to be easily cut. I'm looking for alternative areas to cut and get in to the boat.
There are several things we have to think about here, mainly to do with putting the boat back together. Boyancy (sp?), just being slightly off will render the boat almost useless if not totally useless, this is proven by the lean in the boat I have now, the side it leans on turns fine, the opposite side doesn't. The boats already site very low in the water at the end of the boat. When cutting the boat and re-fusing it back together, if we take off just a little too much height, our boats going to fill with water and flip or sink. And what about fusing the boats back together? we need something that isn't heavy and that goes on evenly so we don't create a lean or have the boat sitting too low in the water.

I'm still searching - I will rip open one of these boats - though I honestly don't see a whole lot of good potential here.
