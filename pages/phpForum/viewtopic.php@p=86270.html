lol maxx.

since the car is rwd and you can't really get awd yet, ryan, i suggest you start practicing.

put electrical tape on the rear wheels -- trust me, it's grippier than the drift kit plastic and you'll have an easier time with it.

lock the diff, somehow, as long as it's locked solidly and won't come loose under power.

countersteer REALLY early. as soon as you know the rear end is out, let up the throttle halfway or more, and countersteer all the way.

took me a year to learn how to rwd drift, so i'm hoping there's someone out there with more skill who can perfect the art and teach me back.
