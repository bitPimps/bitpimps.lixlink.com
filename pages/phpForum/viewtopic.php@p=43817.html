well from memory a good dual cell with fine tuning was doing about 2300rpm(at the wheel).

a way to estimate this number is to take the motor speed(we will say 30,000rpm) and divide that by the gear ratio say 9.86 and subtract say 15% to account for slop in the gears.

so in theory a 3.0 motor should spin the wheels about 2500 times a minute.

but keep in mind a real dyno also measures torque.

the true benchmark for measuring power output would be the 1/4 mile.
