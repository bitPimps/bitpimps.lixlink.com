The Shaq 'Vette has the typical RS "custom manufactured in china for radio shack" on the box, and the only marking on the bottom of the chassis is "china". I really don't think it is anyhing near an Epoch, quality-wise. Don't get me wrong, It's not a terrible car, but it is definately no Epoch.

I bought mine as a way to hopefully put off wanting an Epoch a little longer, and it's shortcomings have made me regret that choice. If I had it to do over again, I'd pass on it and wait until I had enough for the Epoch.

Like I said, it's not totally bad, but the steering likes to wait a second to do what you tell it to do, and that is hard for me to adapt to. It is hella quick, though.
