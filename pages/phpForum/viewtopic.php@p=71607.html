Post by steelwoolghandi » Fri May 27, 2005 12:37 pm

I was at Wal-mart last night and saw these on the rack and was amazed they were not di-cast they were actually all plastic. I found a 54 Chevy and a Cadillac Escalade. I took pictures of the Escalade still in the package but was in a hurry to get the Chevy out and start chopping!

The Sets are Lowrider Magazine sets and come in two pieces to allow you to put a third piece in a middle to make some sort of limo type out of the car. I was just excited that the bodies were plastic and in a scale I could work with.

I took the Chevy out first thing and started a Chopping!!! I married it up to a Muscle Machines SSTuner body as it has a longer wheel base so less cutting I still had to remove a little over 1/16 of an inch of plastic and glue the two pieces together to fit the Chassis in I then removed the front window (it’s the only window removable).

I plan to remove all the body lines take off the bumpers (save them aside), putty and repaint. I think it’s going to be a lot of sanding and putty to get the right look that I want but should look hot when done!

Here are some pic’s of the package and the Chevy I also have pic’s of the wheels not sure which ones I will use or if I will just stick to the stock wheels on the Chassis
