Post by crazydave » Tue Dec 23, 2003 8:03 am

The Super Bits are improved. The new chassis handles better. The cars come in A,B or C frequencies, and the controller can be switched to control any of the 3 frequencies. The new PCB has more power, and the bodies, well you just have to look at them.

The range is not great on them, but no worse than the original Bit Char-Gs. The difference being, when it loses signal it remembers the last command. Which is particularly annoying when racing on a track. It really sucks when you're making good laps, then all of sudden you car starts beating itself into the wall repeatedly.

Another gripe people have, that doesn't bother me so much, but I gotta admit, it is annoying, is that the controller has 2 buttons that do nothing (yet).
