They're not narrow, they're about 15 cm wide, sometimes more\less like in the 3rd piece with the bridge.
you can drive on it quite good with every motor you want, I usually use 3.0 LXXs 1-2 speed and sometimes 2.2to 4.2.

OK it's not easy to race on it, the plastic tube stops the car if you go for it!
I made it like it is because of the space in my room, 90\100 cm are not many but I prefer the track to be a Bit more difficult like in the "real" world!!

If you measure a U turn it's about 30cm or less.
