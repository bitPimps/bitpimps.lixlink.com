Post by sg219 » Sat Jan 14, 2006 4:03 am

I ordered a Mini-LST from Ultimate Hobbies during their New Years Day Only sale.

$169 shipped. I got it on Jan. 11th.

This truck kicks ASS!!!

This is my first NEW RTR above 1:28th scale.

The Paint job on the body is a killer metallic green!!! (I had to ask for the green one.)

Here are some stock pics.

















The batteries ready for abuse!!!!

The blue Losi Sport is the stock LST battery.



I ran it stock for a whole 1 pack.

Time for some action.

I installed the Mamba 6800 and Mamba 25 ESC!!



I had to put some velcro on some spots to reorganize everything.

I also had to cut off part of the battery hold down end, just one side, so that the Kokams and other batts can fit in good and tight. Plus there is velcro on all the batteries and the bottom of the battery area to hold in place.





New sticker layout.











I also put on some Dynamite GREEN metallic body clips. I had to trim the front one about 3/4" shorter to fit properly.








Very fun truck. I'll need new tires very shortly.

I also need to change the pinion again. I first put on a 10T Mini-T pinion on the Mamba with the stock spur, but thats geared WAY too low. I'm going to put the stock pinion on it instead.

The Mamba 6800 was a direct drop in. Easy to instll and set up. Just gotta screw it in and put on a pinion, then secure the switch somewhere.

I bought it for $169.

About $50-75 USD in Team Losi Plastic.

The reality is that I beat the shit out of this truck!!

I'm running a finely tuned Mamba 6800 (until the motor shaft snapped, now a 4200, which sucks) and when I go out, I usually run several packs thru it, I make an afternoon of it. 3 Losi 1100 nimh, 2 Kokam 2s 1500 lipo, 1 Thunder Power 900 lipo, and to top it off, 1 Electifly 3s 1250 lipo.

I sometimes skip the nimhs. The 3 cell lipo really gives this trucks sturctual properties HELL!!!


I usually run in gravel, and/or asphalt reclaim piles. These surfaces are really abusive.


So, unless your putting your truck thru offroad hell, I think you'll be allright with just having an extra arm or two around.
