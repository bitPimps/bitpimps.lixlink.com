Post by HACHI-RYOKU » Wed Aug 25, 2004 8:51 am

It might be the cannonball one's that I'm talking about. When I say bullet style, I'm mean they come with the car attached to the controller and a plastic dome over it.

As for the black motor, they seem to be a very fast clone stock motor. Of course they're not the fastest motors by far. Not all of the cars have them. And some people said they've bought many, but none had them. That's probably a distribution issue. Either way, I wouldn't buy one just to get a black motor. It's just nice when you find one.

As for the bodies, they seem a little diferent than the norm. There is a Mercedes CLK and another that looks similar. There's also one that looks like a Vitz or Netz car. They also have a black RX-7 with a ton of stickers on it. There's some others I can't think of off the top of my head, but the good thing is, they come with a bunch of bodies that aren't available from tomy. One of the stickers said _____lion, or lion_____. I can't remember because I took all those nasty stickers off and threw them away.

Sorry I can't be more specific. I buy a ton of clones and I don't always remember the names of the companies. I pick them up to get a good idea of variations, mod potential, and cheap test cars. A lot of them just get torn up and used for spare parts.

Hey, if you're looking for good cars with frequencies other than the standard US freqs, then I might suggest "Hobby Link Japan". If you do a search under company at there menu drop down thingy, and look under Tomy, you'll find a good selection of bit stuff. They have alot of stuff on sale. Specifically, they have extra chassis that come with a clear Skyline in their "accessories" section on sale for about $6. TWO CATCHES TO THIS DEAL- 1. they have expensive shipping costs and 2. you'll need a multi-freq controller or a 2-speed/booster controller made for cars that are two speed. The controller will of course need to be the same freq as the car. Don't go get an American 49mhz 2-speed controller and expect it to run the 45mhz 2-speed car. I use to see the multi freq controllers for sale here and there on the internet. I picked up a few and use them frequently. I like them. But, if all else fails, then just take a look around any ways. Like I said, I found alot of stuff on sale there, and I'll order from them in bulk to reduce shipping costs. I recently picked up the Skyline history set that came with the three Skyline bodies for $10. This set was about $50 when it first came out a long time ago. Don't confuse this set with the new superbit Skyline set.
