viperdout wrote:That's bullshit!!! No RS sactioned parts?!!! They have to reconize that people don't always use rs parts. I should've just typed this whole thing caps. I wonder if having an RS 2 aaa holder counts as an rs part...
Actually I don't think it's bullshit at all. Tamiya, and Kyosho are well known for sponsering racing series where you can only use their parts. So this is not just a RS thing.

Also it keeps things fair. Not everyone knows of NML's motors, for example, and among those of that do, not all of us are willing to spend 50 bucks on a motor.

And my final argument, having too much speed and power does not win the race, driving skills do. I tell this story all the time, and I'll keep telling it for as long as I have to, but when I raced 1/10th I thought I needed everything the pros were using, if I was going to race, but when I got out to the track, I quickly discovered I was making faster laps with my weaker batteries, and slower motor. On top of that, my buddy had the graphite, anodized aluminum, titanium everything, nearly 2000 bones invested, and when we'd sometimes switch cars during practice, my car, a slightly hopped up sport kit, would out handle his car every time, because I had mine dialed in, and that just pissed my buddy off.

So my point in brief, stop whining that RS is putting on an inexpensive racing series, for us broke dick mofos. If you want to go spend unlimited money on a car that's gonna be faster than you can handle, then get a real hobby level RC, a Mini-Z, or a Mini RS4, or something. That kind of racing has been available to us all along. I'm happy RS is providing me with a cheap alternative.
