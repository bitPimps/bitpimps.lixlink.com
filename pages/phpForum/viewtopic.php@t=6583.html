Post by Nasphere » Thu Aug 18, 2005 7:05 am

as you saw, a hand-grenade controller, press a button, lid opens. the tank has REAL treads, geared really low, it would climb over anything but the ground clearance is a little low. the turret rotates, but its tied into the left tread, when it moves, the turret moves left right left, just back n forth a little...

THE SIZE: Bit size!!!! with about 1/8th inch added to either side... small, loud, and fun!!!

ive been asking if anyone has seen or played with the hovercrafts... supposedly ph2t ordered one, no one else knows they exist! dang they are fun if you tune em right. i took the stock battery pack out, squeezed a cell phone battery in, lasts about an hour instead of 10-15 minutes. the main issues with em are they dont last long (run time), they're a little too heavy (they drag a touch in the rear), the charger eats batteries because it has a relay, the fans are off balance and are noisy... i put a cell phone battery in, built a standalone charger, lightened the back a little by taking water-proofing out, and i balanced all the fans... simple, easy fixes.
