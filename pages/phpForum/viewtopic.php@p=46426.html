Super Street Acura body set for the SE

  • Quote

Post by crazydave » Sun Jun 06, 2004 8:18 pm

Found this at the Shack troday.


I kinda like the RSX, so I had to get it. It includes 2 bodies, 2 sets of tires, 2 sets of new hubcaps, 2 sets of spare wings in alternate colors, and 3 sheets of decals.

1 set of decals is Super Street and Zip Zap logos, but the others are kinda cool, with graphics, stripes, Acura logos, numbers, and CF hoods.


The stickers are pretty nice, and fairly thin, but could be a lot more flexible. The CF hoods are cut no where near close to fitting the shape of the hood.


The bodies have separte front and rear ends to hide the slots, and look fairly long, and more true to scale than other Zip Zap bodies, when they're off the chassis.



You might notice a slight lip along the bottom edge of the side, like it's body kitted, and the windows are blacked out. What that is for, is what appears to be an underglow light, but I haven't tested it yet.


Unfortunately, the bodies sit like monster trucks because of that little underglow thingy.


The front tabs on these chassis are already lowered for my muscle car bodies, so you can imagine how bad it would be. On teh pluse side, since it has sparate pieces for the front and rear ends, it should be easy to cut new slots, but the underglow thingies might have to go.

Bottom line, pretty disappointing for $12.99. I mean 3 sheets of almost decent decals in place of 3 gear sets, and 2 fast motors. That don't calculate. I'm wondering if I was mischarged.
