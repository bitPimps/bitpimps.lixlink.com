Four days ago this body had no left front fender from the front mount to the top of the fender...it took a fair amount of work ans some spare styrene plastic to reconstruct the front end (which also has a full front overlay)...from there it was a challenge to find a fitting paint theme...well the VDS does kinda look like a space ship, or more like the space shuttle. The PHOENIX name refers to the Phoenix ledgend of being born again from the ashes...fitting for this body I think.

Yeah it's a little rough, paint ain't perfect, but it is a beater body that was brought back from the dead and will only go back out on the track....where the wolves wait.
