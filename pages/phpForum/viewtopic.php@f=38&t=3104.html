Post by crazydave » Mon Feb 02, 2004 7:15 pm

TMP wrote:dave....your god man. that is incredible. how exactly do you prep the xmod bodies and what paint did you use exactly. Im definatly gonna try that on my second body. (free from radio shack)
I use Duplicolor automotive laquers. When I had laid graphics on it before, I was just doing it on a small section, but I could tell paint didn't want to stick to these bodies, so before I painted this one, I sanded it really good with 350 grit sand paper. I had just masked off the windows, so when I put clear coat on them it made them look melted. I had to wet sand the windows afters every coat with 1500 grit to smooth them out. By the third coat the paint was laying nice.
