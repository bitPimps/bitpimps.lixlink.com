You know I am ginnin ear to ear w/ those compliments pimps. Hue as for your question.
http://bitpimps.lixlink.com/pages/phpFo ... php?t=3061
Very easy to do and the rubbers fit nearly perfectly (may want to do CD's wire insert mod, we'll see)

As for this puppy, you pop off the hubs from the MT of your liking, take a dremmel or an alternative way to sand fast and shave em down to the same thickness as a Zip hub. This is very easy due to the softness of the plasic they use. I dremmeled out the center but you can drill to make space for the Zips pins. Pop em on and if you drilled the correct size hole, don't even need to glue em on. If I was to repete this process after learning what I did I could do it again in 15 min total.
