Dark Tari had sent me a bunch of bodies. One of them was masked and painted, but not finished. I'm not sure why, he'll have to explain that, but I assume because it wasn't laying very good on the doors. I kinda liked the color, so I wanted to see it clear coated.

I added the grey trim along the bottom. Blacked the windows, and rubbed out the sides so they were smooth. His masking was realling good, only 2 little runs to clean up on the tricky headlight. So I fixed that, clear coated, detailed it, found a wing that fit, and here we go...

Well, what happened, It was one of my 1st paint jobs, this thing has sat for months. I bought a Candy Apple paint and when I did all the masking, priming, and painting, I expected some sort of metallic finish but this is what I got. I was unaware that I would either have to shoot it with a metallic silver or gold, then a light coat of the candy apple, or hit it with a metallic clear coat. So I left it in the Body Bag and gave it up cause I just knew you would create something from it and I was right on the money! That looks damn Pimpin Cavey!
