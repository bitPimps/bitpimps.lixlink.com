Awsome man! i cant wait to see what you do with it. For the roll cage inside i had to remove all of it from the bed forward, otherwise the cells wont fit up in the cab correctly. I used the rear screw to hold the bed in place and the pcb is glued to the underside of the bed rool cage(so yes, everything is hanging down but its no taller than the thickness of the bed so its not a big deal) you may be able to see how its secured in one of the pictures, ill try and get some better ones for you.

For the motor, i tried a zzmt motor and it was just too tall, it got in the way of the rear suspension arm. the Superslick motor works but i had to cut the pinion shaft and the pinion shorter so it would fit the width of the body.

For the rear axle i actualy got rid of the stock baja axle and used a zipzap axle instead. I removed the stock hubs from the zz one and mounted the baja wheels to that. Hope that makes sense. For the other giear i just cut a small slot in the suspension arm on both sides and used a piece of metal(a broken drill bit) as the shaft and glued that down where it needed to be. (you could also use the stock shaft from the microsizer you are taking parts from.)

Hope that helps, let me know if you need any more help.
