Post by Sdog » Tue Oct 19, 2004 9:27 am

Dude....make sure you have the throttle and steering trims on the controller set properly. The problem you have with the car going in reverse when the trigger is in the neutral position most likely means you need to set the throttle trim. The turning problem doesn't sound like a steerng trim problem, but try anyway. These things sometimes need a little tweaking out of the box.

Make sure you don't have any hair and crap stuck in the wheels....sometimes its hard to see. Just make sure your wheels spin freely. If all else fails....take it back.

I'm not sure you are going to find too many GTO's though. As far as I can tell, they were all pretty much gone weeks ago in my area. I was lucky enough to grab a Red Chevelle and Orange GTO (the two colors I didn't have) right after the $14.99 sale started. They are still in the box making nice desk decorations until a later date

Oh....and I agree with Payaso. Go with the SE if you can get another one. When you get a good one they are great little 1/64 scale cars. The last few i have purchased GTO, chevelle, and torino have been great.
