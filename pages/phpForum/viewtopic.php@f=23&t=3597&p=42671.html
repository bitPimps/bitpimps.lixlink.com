ok heres my dual cell story , i had been dual celling clones , but ruining there rear ends due to the power and i was sick of it so i decided to dual cell a zz. i didnt want the batt hanging out the back , so i fliped the pcb( solderd the wiers the the other side ) made a dual cell pack , and a custom carger . then the fun began , no body would fit with out mods . so i used a yellow rx7(only body i felt like hacking up) cut a whole in the windshild so th pcb componets wold fit , shaved the light mouts after removing the lights and put on a few decals . after all that it ran like a dream , faster than any of my dual cell clones ,and more stable. any way what do you guys thing ? this is my first non clone dual cell, and im happy with it , but id like to add a 2 speed pcb .....
