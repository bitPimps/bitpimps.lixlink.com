My Grandma is awesome: 20yo RC

  • Quote

Post by crazydave » Thu Jun 25, 2009 10:21 pm

Well the sad news is my Grandfather is not doing too well, and has been moved to a home. After visiting him we stopped by to see how Grandma was doing, and she just wanted to talk about moving, and dividing up her stuff. She's an avid pack rat/collector, and has been gathering stuff since the 40s. I really didn't feel comfortable talking about the subject, so just to kind of move away from it I said, "I don't know, you just have a bunch of Grandma stuff. I don't want to take your stuff, but if you got some RCs, or guitars then we'll talk." I said this assuming she had neither around, but this statement instead sent her running to the storage shed.

She brought back a 20 year old Radio Shack RC, that is nearly identical to one I had when I was a teen. I was my uncle's, and his girlfriend had bought it for him as kind of a joke birthday gift. I think it only ever got run that day, and was put away after the party, as there is still a chunk of icing left from the birthday cake on the tire.

My friend called right when I was pulling it out of the box, so I was a little distracted. When I told him about it and he asked if it was cool, I told him, "Not really, 20 years ago it was a crappy Radio Shack car, but now that I'm almost 40 and I haven't seen one in 20 years, it's way cool!" My favorite part is the old 80s F-150 body. These were my favorite years for those.
