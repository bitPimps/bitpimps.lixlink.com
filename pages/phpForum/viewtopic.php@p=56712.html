I have a Epoch (nice handling, not great for bashing) Stock form is pretty slow as well. I have a Mini-T (thanx Payaso its been a blessing and a curse, especially after looking at my banking statements, haha) as you can see in the "Oh look what I gone...." section SG was talking about ..http://bitpimps.lixlink.com/pages/phpFo ... c&start=75 The truck is great for bashing especially with a Mamba brushless motor in it... only 2wd tho... You might wanna Associated RCT10 instead 4WD Shaft driven. you definitely don't want the mini quake or the Spider etc. I also got an Xmod (had to do it after I saw the 'Stang) Very fun car. easy to get easy to upgrade (lithium-ions, Nelly, carbon driveshaft, AWD, etc.) .... Ok enough of the rant....

My Choices.
Mini-T - $119 - Great for bashing and racing (Brushed or Brushless), you can take it anywhere, on any surface (even in the snow ). You can change everything on it highly upgradeable and customizable, Hop-ups been around for a while so wide selection of quality parts.

Xmod - $50 - Great for racing and bashing can't take it anywhere only smooth surfaces. parts are easy to come by but hopups are mostly being made by GPM (Shotty quality parts) Still a fun car to drive if you wanna race on a track.
