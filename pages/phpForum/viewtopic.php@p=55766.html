Post by z-beam » Mon Sep 20, 2004 8:01 am

cheers pimps, i pulled out the skillz for this job. and im glad i pulled it off.

the supra body has always screamed for some major modifications, when i first got one i was temted to do a matmobile styled supra but after contemplating the shell over a couple of weekends drinking a few brews with mates i dicided a sharkmobile would turn out nicer.

it did have more fin details before i painted it but it looked cheesy as.

some of the things i did to streamlined it where the removal of a lot of the chunky bodykit on the front end, smoothing out the roof (it has thos stoopid ridges that lead into the hole it the rear window), extending the bottom of the doors and getting rid of the sqareness above the front wheel arches (but i kept the square back edge of the arch itself).

nice to hear how much you liked it fellas. i hope to produce some more nice works soon.
