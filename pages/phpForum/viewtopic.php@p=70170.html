Never gave this any thought cuz I find spinners cheese-mo, but here is an idea.

To make the spinners just use zip zap plastic and cut to form (use the thin shit)

The issue is how to get it on the axels. Simple, for the front wheels just take out the front axels, thread them through the spinner and wheel but don't fully insert them so they arn't as tight as stock. This will create wobble, but like I said, spinners are gay.

For the rear cut down a needle w/ a flat head heat the tip and insert into the plastic with the spinner already threaded.
