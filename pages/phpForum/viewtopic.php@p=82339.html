Story behind this:

I ripped the shit out of my old track because I got so bored of the testing, so finally I built me a new test track. Here I wanted to start him off on a good start with bits, so I gave him the Ferrari F50 Super Bit Char - G set. He LOVED it. He then helped me build the rest of the track and we started fooling around when all of a sudden. My little brother pulls out the digi cam and we had ourselves some bitpimps vid material.

You know it's great to see how kids just love to play and I felt good getting this kid started with micro rc's. It's kinda shit racing by yourself, then hitting the boards and seeing all these great micro's but not being able to race against em. Even though he's a lil kid, I give him a few days of practice, a slight passion and inspiration and next thing you know I might even have more kids wanting to play on the shitty test track I built while my other one is hittin construction again. Already I had some of this kid's friends asking me to make some orders. I've ordered some lambo sets for them, can't really find any thing good.

I want to show em some Pullbacks and what you can do with the wheels, but I can't seem to find any.....That's a whole different story.
