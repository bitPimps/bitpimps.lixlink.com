There is an RS down by the job in the Underground that I stop twice a week
to see what's new. This time the manager talked to me. He was surprised
at my knowledge of 1/64 scale cars.

As we talked further we went over to the monitor and came to Da Hood
where I took him thru my Gallery. This cat freak'd man. He couldn't
believe that there was so much interest out here for the 1/64 cars.

He was talkin about make'n a display to show pics of my cars ?????
