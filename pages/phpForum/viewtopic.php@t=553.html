nox -

You asked for it, so here you go, the best description of the tuner kit is "Binggity-Bling-Bling -Bling"! Just like dave said for only $13 you get all this goodness, what a deal!



The bodies are great as far the detail goes and durability... i'm not too crazy about the Mits., but then again I bought the kit for all the other goodness! The Evo sits nice on the chassis and the lime-gold finish is the bomb son! The 3.4 purple monster is awesome for the single celled hoopties, way to much for dual's... unless you enjoy flipping your ride all over the place lol .
The 2.8 orange motor is a nice addition to have for the dual celled whips, it adds what the 2.1 was missing. I'm using it right now with a 12:1 gear ratio and am having a blast in my Yenko, later on i'm gonna swap back to the 2.1 and use the 9.86:1 ratio and see the results of that combo. I've got a feeling that my dual will be even more controllable at that point, will let you know. The tires I never really was a fan of, I tend to us Bit/Ms tires because they have better surface coverage than the ZZ. And as far as the remaining parts, the gear-sets, hub-caps and spoilers... well they do their jobs well. The only thing missing from this kit is the stabilus set, and a extra set of front knuckles and hubs (still searching for them ) that would've been the cherry!
