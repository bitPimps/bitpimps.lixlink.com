the main switch on the back puts the fan on high/low... low, you will probably never use. theres a big O chunk of foam inside the skirt that i took out, it was there to keep it afloat when the fans on low and your in water...

this thing can hover on sand!!! but it works better on fairly smooth concrete er anything else smooth. ph2t will definately like it... its fun! but one thing i think is a must: a standalone charger. the charger in the controller has 4 AAA batteries with a relay to switch on charging...

When you get it up to about half speed (which is fast) and hit the dif thrust, it spins so fast, and before you hit anything, you flip off the lift, and it stops.. freakin cool...

Im bringing my craft back cause i dont think i conditioned the batteries right, so they wont hold a charge anymore (oops)... oh well, its first day, so they'll take it back.
