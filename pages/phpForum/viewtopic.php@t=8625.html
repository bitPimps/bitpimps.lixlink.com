Most of the time shipping fees don't make me irate. You have to figure the cost of shipping plus packaging material (Boxes, Peanuts, Bubble wrap, etc.). Part of my job is to handle the shipping for the company I work for. Most of the items we ship for consumers the S&H charge is $10.00.

Don't get me started on insurance costs (I believe UPS charges $.35 for each hundred over the first $100.00). So when people are saying they are shipping UPS and the item is under $100.00 don't believe them when they want to hit you with insurance cost too.
