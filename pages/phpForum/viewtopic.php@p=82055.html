Ok, i just need to vent on account of my own stupidity. Ive had an epoch for some time now and i love it, it was a little slow so i added a cell and i lasted about 3 months on a 4 cell car, then i wanted more speed and torque so i ordered an external turbo.

The turbo came today and ive been installing it, un installing it, re installing it...ect.... because i couldnt get the damn thing to work. Then i thought i fried something in the process.. so im freaking out because i just spent the last hour breaking my epoch

After that hour i took the turbo off to see if that was the problem and then my worst fear, the car still didnt work So i start fooling around with it trying to see what i broke all while the crystal is sitting all alone on my desk away from my car.

Turns out the entire time i had forgotten to plug the crystal back into the car

I plug it in and everything works great. So i just now finished installing the turbo and its time for a test drive.

Sorry guys, just had to rant.


EDIT: OK, i just took her out for a spin and WOW! I'm impressed with how much better she runs. Now for a kyosho power boat motor and ill be good to go.
