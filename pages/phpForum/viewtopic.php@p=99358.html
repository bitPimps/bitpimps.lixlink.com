Post by Clint » Wed Aug 30, 2006 7:07 pm

Cool, I see you moved the mt bevel gear to the lower section of the blue
transfer gears. If that works well enough, you can replace the blue
transfer gears with the black zz motor pinions and reduce the diff package size.

Fyi, the pressure of the zzmt bevel gear on the transfer gears determines the diff's action... from full open slip -> to no slip(locked). So you can adjust as needed.
