Yep, i have that exact model. I picked it up on ebay for fairly cheap but i think i got lucky, they seem to be pricy now. The body has great details and their "I;52" scale rating really isnt true,, its damn near close to the exact length of a zipzap. It comes stock with two cells.

I opened it up, charged it up and "poped" open the novelty remote with the "D" pad style driving controller. Its extreamly hard to drive,, to be honest you almost need to use both hands and then things get crampt and so on lol. Out of the box its a little slow, the motor is hard wired in and it has a single LED headlight that turns on when you go forward. overall stock for the price you are generaly better off with a mircosizer or zipzap.

On the other hand i think the body has awsome detail and i would love to get my hands on the subaru wrx version but i cant see spending that much money again.

here are some shots of it in depth



thats it next to a microsizer body, so its a little larger but since zipzaps are larger they are about the same size.. I just didnt have a zz body handy










WOW! thats alot of pictures.. Anyway, i have since gutted the whole thing and modified it to hold three cells and a microsizer pro pcb with a red Superslick motor and a solid antenna. Its now way too fast for its own good and im on my roof every other turn lol.

Anyways, there ya go man
