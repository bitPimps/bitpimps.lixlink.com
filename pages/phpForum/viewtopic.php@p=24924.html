Post by MaxximumRC » Fri Sep 05, 2003 8:29 pm

My cheap tank just came in from ebay.
Here are some pics with my web cam.



As you can see the controller is smal, but in even in my fairly large hands it feels pretty good. The range on this thing, well, sucs. It also stuters when it starts getting far away. It is alredy duel celled, but it uses to cells about 1/3 of the size of normal bit cells. It goes on pretty steep slopes probably around or steeper that 45. It is powered by two green end ball motors. It can go from carpet to hardwood and yo can't notice a differece. Al in all even with the range problems, for 8 dollars shipped from Honk Kong I think this si pretty good.


EDIT: oh yeah one more thing the controller uses button cells, but they some with it, and it has an external charger.

And another thing is that it will not interfere with other bits even though it is 49 mhz.
