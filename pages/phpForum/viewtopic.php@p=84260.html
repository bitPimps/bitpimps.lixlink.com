I've been flying this into stuff just for fun, with the cage you can park it on the ceiling, bounce it off lamps, hug walls... Can't figure out HOW the twits at Radio Hack are killing them. Anyway, unless you're just unsafe at any speed, or don't have much business playing with a $60 toy, or a RadioHack employee, go for it. Seems like a pretty good way to get addicted to RC Helicopters. Definately not as controllable as something with a cyclic control, or a non-fixed collective, but for what it is, it's pretty decent and more durable than a more sophisticated craft.

My only complaint is I'm stuck with a 27mhz, I live like 100 yards from a very highly traveled interstate. The damn CB's the truckers use are f*cking my signal like a $5 Lot Lizard. I'm flying along straight and level, trucker keys mike and all the sudden SB's throttle dies, or goes full power into the ceiling, or anti-torque rotor fires up, or all of the above.

What channel of 27mhz are we using? Any idea how hard a freq change will be? I assume it's just a fairly simple crystal swap in TX and RX? I'm thinking either go to the high end of 27mhz, or I may end up converting over and using to one of the Amature Radio freqs that's allowed for "telecommand of model craft" since I've got my LICENSE now (I'm KC9ICR incase there are any other Hams here).

** EDIT Opened the TX, found a 13.5975 mhz crystal Opened RX, found a 26.740 mhz crystal.
