45tr0 got most of it covered, except that there's a few flavors of regular bits. the OG ones, the Microsizer Pro (or bit w/ boost), and the Microsizer Sport. The Pro has a turbo button, which allows the car to go full speed. When you don't push the button, the car goes half speed. There's a mod to reverse that function.

The labo Murciealago is one of the nicest bit shells there is, imo. The countach is nice, but it's hard to lower it as much as it needs. The Diablo is nice like the Murci, but I prefer the Murci. You could try to find a lambo set and get all three bodies for not much more money.
