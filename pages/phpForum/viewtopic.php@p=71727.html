AE85 Levin with a little attitude

  • Quote

Post by sat4life27 » Sun May 29, 2005 11:30 pm

What started out as a little white Levin with nothing to do. I first wanted to see how low it could drop it. I managed to get it pretty damn low with only making the wheelwells bigger.



Then I decided to try to make my first bodykit. I cut up the stock side skirts of a civic xmod to make the side skirts for the Levin. Used plastic from a package to make the front and rear bumpers. The spoiler is from a Import Racer AE86.



Now a little paint was what she needed to finish the deal. I used the same Royal Blue paint (I hate to waste things) and here she is....



I will touch it up and detail it tomorrow.
More to come..... a DC Mod is going to make her fly!!!
