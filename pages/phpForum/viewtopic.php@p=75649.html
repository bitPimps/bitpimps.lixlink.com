Post by Mazda787b » Sat Jul 23, 2005 11:24 pm

Well, I don't seem to post here as much as I should, so you guys are getting the first look into my next project.

I have been browsing a local Racer's forum called R/C Pimp for some time now, trying to find the right truck. I finally found her last week, it was just what I wanted.

The Truck was a Losi XXX-T Matt Francis Edition. Some of the original MF parts were replaced for looks. All the things that make the truck what it is, like the Graphite, Aluminium, ETC was all as it was originally. I only paid $50 for her as a roller. The only non-cosmetic things that were changed on the truck were the shock bodies. They were threadded, but the local guys prefer the clamps. He also included a Trinity Kinwald 3degree aluminium rear arm mount.

I picked her up Tuesday, and began work that night. Thanks to some 3M Adhesive Remover, all the decals were removed from the chassis. I cleaned alot of the dirt off of her, and the following pics illustrate how she was before the latest trip to the local hobby shop.






I went Friday to another hobby shop, and I picked up a set of RPM Ballcups, RPM Gear Cover, Losi Rear Bumper, Losi Front Kick-up, and Losi Body Mounts. A new Antenna tube, as well as a JR Z-590M Servo, and RS310 Rx (not pictured). The next 3 pics are of the truck as it sits today.







Notice the Trinity Arm mount, and that I did not finish putting on the last turnbuckle!

I am waiting for my LRP Quantum Reverse to be replaced/repaired, and it is all downhill from there. Threadded MF2 Shocks will be here soon, as well as some used tires mounted on Yellow dish rims. I will have an extra set of tires for the track only, as I don't want to ruin a set of $40 tires on the concrete. I am not counting the bashing tires as an upgrade for the car, but my racing tire choice will be as follows: Proline R3 Evil twins on Losi Dish wheels will be out back, and either Proline Blades in M3 or The Edge in R3 compound will be mounted on Losi front wheels.

All that is left will be batteries, motor and a body. I will be running batteries from another local matcher, Twisted cells. You cannot go wrong with matched GP3300s! Depending on what kind of deal I can get, I may buy a new Novak Equalizing tray and a set of their matched cells that comes with it in a combo. To let my Pirhana Digital charge at a rate of 5A, I am working on modding a PSU power supply into one suitable for my racing needs. It will have a 12V line for my charger, a 3v line for motor break-in, and a 5v line for running a lathe or similar. Body will be a Proline Crowd pleazer. I am not sure on the motor, but I will most likely have two. One will be for the tight, indoor carpet track I will be racing on this fall. That will most likely be a P2k Pro. For the larger outdoor and indoor tracks, I will be using a Kinetix modified Trinity Monster Stock (when they get back in stock) or a Epic Binary 2.

If you look closely in the pics, you can spot out some of my other projects.
There's no replacement for displacment.
