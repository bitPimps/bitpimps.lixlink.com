Well, we broke down and bought an RCP track... two actually.
Here's some shots of our layout, which uses 2 large L tracks.
It took a bit of getting used to, but just a bit... the surface is
fucking incredible to drive on. We're using the slick side and
there's too much grip (nice problem to have for a change).
The ONLY thing that's a little hard to get used to so far is the
grippy side rails. It really just means we gotta get our drivin'
skills together, but we may figure out a material that will allow
the cars to slide off the sides a bit more... only a minor problem
actually and we already are getting used to it... we both agree
it was money WAY well spent.





Here's the cars we drove today... MR-01s that are basically
stock other than bearings, diffs and x-speed motors... gettin'
our Initial D style on...
