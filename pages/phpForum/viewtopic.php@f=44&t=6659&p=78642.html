I got my kyosho oil dampers, GPM servo gears, and 1 degree knuckles today. Damn it took forever to get those servo gears in, and I still have some sort of binding problem. If I tighten the servo case down, it binds hard. Loose for now, but I have to fix this before I race on the 7th.

Anyway, I put a gpm rear mount in place of my kyosho mount, and put the kyosho mount on the gpm mount (ala ph2t) and mounted the oil dampers to the kyosho mount (middle hole). You want to talk about ugly? jeez. gunmetal and blue do not go well together on my blue and yellow truck. I guess it will have to do for now. Waiting on my nelly deluxe and 3cell 700mah lipo to get here, and then I'm going to be seriously booking. It handles like a dream now, but I've yet to take it outside. Just cutting donuts on the hardwood ATM.

I've got some binding in drivetrain too. I thought it was the stock diff gear, but I replaced it with a gpm delrin gear and still have a little clicking... especially when going from reverse to forward. I've taken everything apart, and it all seems to be ok. I'm running pretty new bearings, so I doubt it is that. Plus when I spin the wheels in reverse with my hand, it seems to get caught on something. It doesn't seem to matter if I use the stock 12 tooth pinion, or the overland 12 tooth SP pinion. The only thing that seems to make sence is the rod going through the titanium GPM gear seems to be too short, and doesn't seem to go into both bearings very far. it ends up being all the way in one, and perhaps not in the other at all. I can't tell with it closed.

*sigh*

Pictures of my new paintjob on a madforce white body, and my slick chassis with custom alloy upper suspension arms coming soon. Hopefully, I will figure this out. It's really bugging me because all of the gears look fine. no missing or worn teeth. drat.

-JNY
