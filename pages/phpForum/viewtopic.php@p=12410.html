These Thunder Chargers are da bomb, but I just can't quite take a liking to the bodies. They're a tad longer than bits, so only my Cannonball clone bodies fit. That leaves me the option of my hummer, which I already have plans for, and the Celica, that was my first micro RC and has been through every experiment imaginable.

I didn't think it was worth saving, but I gave it a go. Sanded most of the guck off. Repainted it with the original paint scheme I did 6 months ago. Gave it a new Zip Zap wing, and since I had already hacked out the back window for a dual cell, I went ahead and made it a wheelie machine

When it came together, I think this body is actually ready for one more tour of duty.







I made a video clip, let me know how this works out, it's my first time posting a video.

Sorry for the Quicktime format, if anyone knows how to convert it to Windows media let me know. This ones been optimized, I have a larger higher quality one if this ones too hard to see.
