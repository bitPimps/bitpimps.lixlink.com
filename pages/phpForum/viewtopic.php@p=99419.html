Yes, I received my PiccoZ. So I doubt there's any shortages (status on Radioshacks site says "ships in 1 o2 days' when I ordered mine a couple of days ago. I'm sure there was some mis-communication at that store in NY.

Now, as far as what LiPo to use in the ZZ, I suggest going by SIZE and not capacity!!! I had gotten a sweet deal on a set of 5, 160maH LiPo cells. The cells are 20x30mm and they're just too big for Bits or ZZ/ZZSE, and I have my doubts about getting a decent fit even in the ZZMT.

But Max is right, someone found a dealer that is selling the batts that are in the PiccoZ helicopters. It's a tiny 50maH Lipo and is a mere 20x13.5mm in dimensions. That's only a little wider than the diameter of the stock battery!

I ordered a few of the 50maH to see how well they'll work with my Bits/ZZs, I'm hoping for good results!
