Post by HACHI-RYOKU » Tue Nov 23, 2004 2:49 pm

Yeah, you'll regret not spending a bit of money on a digital camera. That's just one of the things in life that you have to put a little money into. Otherwise, you'll get sick of it and then go buy a good one later and end up paying again.

Also, some tips; optical zoom is normally better than digital zoom. Get a camera with optical zoom. Digital zoom sounds nice, but takes horrible pictures. Also, don't settle for anything less than 3 megapixels. 1.2 megapixels is cheap, but a 35mm disposable camera takes better pictures. I personally don't settle for anything less than 5 megapixels. Limited video ability is also a very cool thing.

For bits, the thing to look for is digi cams that can take "portrait" pictures. Most cams do, but keep an eye on that. You don't want to be stuck with one that doesn't, because you may not be able to focus on a bit without it.
