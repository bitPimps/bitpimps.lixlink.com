Post by sg219 » Tue Aug 22, 2006 7:22 pm

Since building this truck, it has undergone some minor tweaks and adjustments and has performed VERY well!!

Like all RC's, something always goes bad or breaks.

I cooked the original Dynamite Tazer ESC, and froze up a servo.

I installed and/or changed....

-Plastidipped my battery packs to protect them from corrosion.

-Unfroze the front servo, its a Hitec. I opened it up, broke it down and sprayed it with some silicone spray to free things up and to protect it from H2O. Works great now. The rear anceint Futaba servo hasn't given me a bit of trouble.

-Installed a Mtroniks WATERPROOF ESC!!

-Sealed up the axles a bit more with a couple of drops of silicone caulking on the holes leftover from the stock ballstud mounts.

-Sprayed the bearing with some silicone spray to repel a bit of H2O.

-Painted a new body!!
