here it is again, i did not like the proportions of the last attempt so i scaled out another bus using the wheels as a sizing point, this is what i did , i had a picture of a vw bus and i measured out the height of the wheel and then i used that height as a measuring point to see how many wheels hi the bus was and found out that mine was not tall enought , i had the bus 2 and a half wheels hi in the front and it needed to be 3 wheels high and the same for the rear , i had 1 and a half wheels high and needed two wheels high, so i changed it again, i think that this is really takeing shape now, i like the height and will go with it, i wanted it to be as close to an original and to scale as posible... she's looking good now i think...
