OK Dudes,

video added to the web site. Its 11mb, so broad band only I suspect. Got both the new Hitari's racing on a simple oval. Had to make it simple cause the turning circle of the hitari's is larger than any other car we have tried.

Apart from that, the cars rock. Both steered straight out the box, controllers work through brick walls, and you get all the upgrades you need. In terms of speed we paced them against a standard zipzap with a 4.2 pro motor, they beat it with 2.15 motors

I would recomend these to anyone
