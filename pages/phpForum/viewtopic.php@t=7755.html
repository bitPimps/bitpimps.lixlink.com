MotorGong wrote:Hand Airbrushed? How much does he charge, or normally charge to do something like that?

Yeah, he hand cut all the masks. I haven't received the body yet, or paid for it yet either. He hasn't given me a price yet, but it's very reasonable for what others are getting.

He's a member here, but hangs out at banned rc.
betty.k wrote:sorry to hijack ya thread, but what did you remove to shave weight?

Quite a few things. Not sure I can remember all, but the main ones are as follows.

1 steering servo. The Revo comes stock with dual weak steering servos. One good one is more than plenty.

All the reverse gears got tossed, and I run a foward only conversion (FOC) That drops a shit load of gears. Crazy all the shit it takes to make it go backwards...lol Less rotational mass at that, and improves acceleration so it's a triple bonus.

The micro servo for reverse got tossed off as well as the linkage.

OPTI Drive got tossed.

Wheels and tires were changed out for GS SUTCE racing wheels with Bowties, and Trinity 3 stage foams.. Super light, and alot less rotational mass.


I'll be chopping down the receiver box next, and a few other things. I'll post some pics of it.
