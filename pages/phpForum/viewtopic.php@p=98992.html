Agreed on the Bensu... I also had one labeled "Thor" that was just as shitty, and I believe it was just a re-labeled Bensu... the "Cannonball" Bensus weren't too too bad, but parts weren't compatible with other bits/clones... what really made those ones worthwhile, though, was the cool little stand alone charger...

Also, there are clones by a company called Sen Fa that 219 and myself bought a bunch of on eBay that were pretty shoddy...

But the all time worst, IMO, were the Motorworks trucks with the die-cast bodies that came in a set with the micro boats... I had high hopes for this thing, it was dual celled out of the box, and with the heavy body removed it was an absolute rocket... a rocket that never went straight, no matter how I configured the steering magnets, tweaked the centering spring, whatever else I tried, it had a mean left hook that I could not fix...

I still have the remains of it in a baggie in my bit box, someday I'd like to try to stuff the pcb and cells into a ZZ chassis or something...
