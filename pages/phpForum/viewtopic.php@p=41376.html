Here's a sneak peak of my prostocker project.

Bird wrote:I have never seen a Camaro do an ollie before. Awesome car. Great vid. Damn.
LOL, yeah, no shit, huh? It didn't occur to me that is like an ollie. Funny.
CaboWabo wrote:I loved seeing the thing tip up on a side and drive on 2 wheels.
I'll tell ya, I loved that too when I was editing, but the more I watch, the more I realize the power loss. I need get the rear end exactly precise if this things gonna stay on the ground. Hog's JB Weld just might be coming into play here.

sg219 wrote:What about a small amount of weight in the front plus the wheelie bar to keep it level on the drag strip. Like a truck pull kind of thing maybe.
Well, if you watch the drags, they always pull the front end up off the line too. The idea is to center the weight around the rear axle, to lay the horse power. You don't need the front wheels off the line if you're pointed in the right direction, and the rear end hooks evenly. (I still need to work on that last part.) If I work some kind of shock absorbtion into the wheelie bar, it should take up some of that impact, and bring the front end down without lifting the rear, in theory, we'll see.
