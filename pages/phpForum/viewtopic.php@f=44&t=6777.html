Here is the Lithium Protector I used. This unit can supply up to 20ma to a 2.4-3.6v LED.

I did resize the pictures. They were originally 2048 width. 1024 width is unreasonable?

If you use a single 20mah light, you can see them very well in daylight. I was thinking about mounting a single red LED inside the front gearbox. You can use 2-10ma LEDs to the headlights, which looks great at night and is visible at close range in daylight.

I'm still waiting on the nelly deluxe to make it here, then I will be able to let you guys know how it performs. It was already insane with a 2 cell lipo and spider FET board. I'm planning to put a switch in for a "turbo".

The idea being I can cut the voltage going to the motor in half by putting a large resistor in parrallel with the motor circuit to half the voltage and up the resistance. Hopefully this will cut down battery use and make the thing have a serious runtime. It should be able to pull off 30-45 minute runtimes with a hot motor. When I'm not showing off, it is more fun to play with it for more than 30 seconds at a time before the motor, ESC, and battery overheat from typical pimpsmurf abuse.
