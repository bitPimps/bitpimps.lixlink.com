I have to put a good word in on these little bad-asses too. I picked up two.

I put one in a monster bit that betty-k modded with his suspension kit. I dropped two 1/3aaa's in to give it just a liittle more power. Now this car rocks. It's got great handling and is fast as hell.

I have to give the biff a 10 out of 10 for performance. It is faster than a regular fet mod. It's perfect on betty-k's monster.

I have to put in a side note though. So far I've tested this in a single cell, duall cell, quad cell and a car with a lil-poly batt. The quad cell and the li-poly cell seemed to make it hot, so I stopped using it in those two because I didn't want to fry the the biff. Transistors normally run warm, but this was borderline fry with just a few seconds of pushing the go button.
