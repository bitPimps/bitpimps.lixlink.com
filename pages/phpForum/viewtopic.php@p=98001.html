Hi Pimps, Thanks all for the props! Initially i just wanted to do one...but then I felt pressured in my previous thread. So I had to work double time...I just felt that i needed to make up for the past years. Im glad you pimps like it.

honda_s2000: Thanks im glad you noticed:)

DT: Thanks mang! couldnt have done it without you reminding me of the good 'ol days

Masta Cabo: Thanks! how do I upload to my album?

SuperFly/Hue35: Thanks i really appreciate your comment. I guess we all have a look thats distinct. I was kinda worried about the GT, i thought maybe someone thought of it already:)

CrazyDave: The color of my bug reminded me or your cali bug way back.

Sky: thanks bro, it used to be a jota.

Bird: I like little details, it adds a little surprise to those looking.

The porsche to me had a perfect body that i didnt want to mess with, so i just added that spoiler, and a pearl blue coat of paint, lowered it a bit and liked the way the stock bronze rims contrasted with the color of the body paint.

The Bug at first concept was supposed to have a ragtop just like hiroto34s mini, which was very inspiring...and then i saw this bug in a magazine with a tiltup moonroof! So i thought that i just had to do this with my bug. I had to make 3 color changes with this car, the first was supposed to be a two-tone..but the color wasnt what i had in mind...then i got me a purple sray can...but it looked too much like grimace...so i said fuck it...and got my silver leaf can and sprayed away...and topped it with clear blue to get that mettalic look. The porsche wing just fitted in nicely...so i decided to keep it that way. Then i went in the interior and chopped up a hero-clix WW2 soldier and made him into a rally driver.(ill take a pic of him later) And installed a roll cage, and a beetle dash and racing seats.

The Ford GT was supposed to be my comeback piece, and took me a long time to make...along with research and some prodding from my officemate pubejoolz. (BTW, hasselhoff is in the crowd..lol) It is a lambo Jota with lots of sanding and puttywork. Thank goodness for decal sheets! I couldnt have pulled this one off without the decals. I used here a superbit chassis with pullback tires coz i wanted it to look really wide and sporty. Then i got the eingine from a bitracer chassis to complete the look. And lots of clearcoat and polishing! lol

Im glad, these 3 projects are off my chest...and now im off to the next projects!
