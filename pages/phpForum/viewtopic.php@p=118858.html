Post by ibjamn » Sat Sep 13, 2008 8:53 pm

If you want NIMHs, you basically will be looking for two (or three) 1.2v 1/3AAA cells. Anything more than 120mah will do. Put together, There is some in your local Batteries Plus, at least I remember there was.

Another option is the replacement rechargeables you can buy for cordless phones, and hack them as you please. If you see one pack that is 3.6v that just means it is three 1.2v cells(If you didn't know already) but they will ne higher capacity, and the stock charger may have to be modded, or just do multiple/longer charges for higher capacities.

Edit: Also, if you want to hack a Micro-T battery pack, it is four 1.2v 1/3AAA cells wired in series and are 150mah.
Just a thought.
