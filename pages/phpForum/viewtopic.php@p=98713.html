Rheinholdt wrote:Oh, Yeah! Bilbo. I've been scoping those PiccoZ helis too. My Shack still had some @$30 last time I looked. I didn't get a chance to try it since a guy and his kid were playing with the demo but it looked like the main rotor doesn't have a speed control? To "land" it appeared that you have to cut the power and "slam" the ground.
No, the RS Pico has a speed control for the main rotor, and it is entirely possible to do a soft landing. Both controls (up/down (main rotor), rotation (tail rotor) are proportional, so you can fly it with a fair amount of finesse. The biggest "problem" to be aware of is you have to charge it with the controller (like a ZZ or bit), and with a fresh set of good quality 6xAA alkaline cells, you will only get about 6-7 charges before you don't get a good enough charge to fly properly. This will manifest itself as the inability to get it trimmed so it doesn't spin counter-clockwise, as there is enough power to the main rotor to lift, but not enough to the tail rotor to stabilize. Unfortunately, this happens around the time you've crashed enough to think you've broken it, but it just sucks the batteries FAST.
