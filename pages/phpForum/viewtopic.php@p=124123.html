Thanks guys! It's coming along.
It's supposed to be a Tatra 815, with an excessive tundra lift as probably it's going to be snow-camo and I wanted to keep the bed usable.

Detail is getting there, still need a couple more Bic pens (fuel tanks), need to build the framerail toolboxes, bumpers, lights... Need to decide on air intake and exhaust setup, come up with a material for the bed canvas, finalize the wiring, music wire antenna, throw in some bed floor detail...

Sorry about the file fuzz on the interior, and the dash being crooked, I was taking pics while trying to troubleshoot my brotherinlaws computer.

So far, I've learned a LOT about making styrene bodies! If you want to try, don't be scared, it's not *that* bad!
After the Tatra is done, I think a Tundra Buggy for the same chassis will be next, or a Samurai for a stock ZZ-M.
