i got tired of the typical mad force offerings from kyosho so when the unpainted bodies came out it was tops on the list

i already had a few sets of gunmetal wheels and parts so i went with a complimentary color selection and decided the classis muscle car look would be a good effort atleast. i used tamiya camel yellow and metallic black. they were both easy to use and came out very well. the pactra indy silver for highlight parts as a bit trickier to work with but pactra has some very cool colors i still have to go in and darken the grill.... give it some shadow/weathering.

anyway, here are the pictures.
