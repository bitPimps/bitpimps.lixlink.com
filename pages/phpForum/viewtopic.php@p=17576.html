I picked one up today at walmart. $17.+ beans.





Note, the steering adjustment is backwards compaired to other bits.
and the 3 screws to take the body off. (swearword)



In the bottom right corner of the box is a button.
When pushed, pops open the "slide out" charger.




Note the two connector pointers on the bottom of the slide out charger.
It (the charger, containing the two AA batteries) when slide back in the box. IS the power source for the Transmitter.





The connecter to keep the car connected to the charger is cumbersome to work the car on.





The Motor is the exact same as the Radio Shacks NOS/NX Motor.
