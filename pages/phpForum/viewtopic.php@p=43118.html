Zip Zap Trans Am Body Kit

Post by Sdog » Tue Apr 27, 2004 8:12 pm

I just happened to stop by a local Radio Shack today and came across a new "Past and Present" Body Kit. The Kit contains a Black "77 Trans Am and a Yellow '02 Collecters Edition Trans Am. These Are not SE bodies and do not have lights.

The detailing on the bodies is very nice. The sides of the '77 body are a bit tight on my SE chassis, but fits with just a little coaxing. The black wheel set for the '02 looks pretty authentic (right style and color), but the wheelset for the '77 are not the gold honeycomb style wheels that where on these cars. Instead you get a set of gold rally style (I think that is what they are called) wheels, which look good nonetheless.

I can't find these on the Radio Shack website, but they are out. Hey CJ, you are usually the pimp with lowdown on the Shak stuff ahead of time....you on vacation or something?
