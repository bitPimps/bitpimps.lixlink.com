Post by steelwoolghandi » Fri Jan 06, 2006 7:08 pm

I found this today at Wal-Mart it’s by Jada Toys the same people that make Dub City. It’s a 1969 Chevy Chevelle SS and called a Bigtime Muscle.

I did not see an antenna sticking out when I bought it but when I took it apart it was wrapped around the inside of the body.

The Steering is a steering motor, the controller sucks but I plan to change that and replace the PCB board. The size is a little larger then an Epoch so about 1/32 scale.

All around a nice little RC for the price $9.95 it runs real fast but the tires are slick so it gets vary little traction on my wood floor.

Thought I would post this in case ya’ll haven’t seen them yet.
