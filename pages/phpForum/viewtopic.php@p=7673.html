Post by crazydave » Sat May 17, 2003 9:31 am

Franken-zap lives again. Back from the dead, it's a more subtle, refined car now.

I ended up using the PCB from the LXX booster. The steering coil wires had fried, and were a dull grey color, so I replaced the steering coils. The LXX had some kick ass steering coils in it, so my steering is better than ever. While I was at the front end, I replaced the clear plastic bulk head thing with a black Microsizer one, because the clear plastic cracks too easy. I had to shave it down here and ther to get it to fit. I also covered the holes from where I moved the steering coils back.

I liked how kwikbb kept his dual cell looking stock, so I decided turn Franken-Zap into a dual cell sleeper. I painted the PCB board cover to help hide, "my little secret". I think this is going to be temporary. Anyone want to take bets on it making through the weekend before I tri-cell it again?


See, innocent enough. Just a painted PCB cover that's all...


...or is it?


Right now it's wearing my new favorite body, but I'm going to buy a new ZZ to put this body on, because I want to see the hubcaps with it.
...crazy man, crazy...dig it.
