Sure, no sweats.

The SRM mod is based on the theory that if you boost power to the TX you _should_ be able to get greater range on the pissweak MS controllers. The thread for this mod is at tinyrc.com and also at ausmicro.com.

The multi freq mod is still in theory, getting data stage. I want to be able to make my TX's on their own freqs so not to conflict with other TX's when racing. There is a thread at ausmicro.com about it but this mod is sorta on hiatus until i can get my hands on a CRO. There is two possible ways I _think_ it can be done. 1) modify the RX circuit to decrease it's bandwdith (currenty at 8MHz, this can be seen on the TX freq's avail today @ 27Mhz, 35Mhz, 40Mhz, 45Mhz, 49Mhz, and 57Mhz) and make it more "selective" on what signals it recieves. Then moving the TX osc freq to somthing in between the freqs I just listed. 2) modify the osc on the TX2C and RX2C chipsets (the 220K resistor between pins 11&12 on the TX and pins 4&5 on the RX) so to send the L/R/Fwd/Rev commands on a different modulated freq. It's all still in brainfark stage at the 'mo and I don't know if it will even work.....
