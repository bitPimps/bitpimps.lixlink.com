Post by CaboWabo » Wed Nov 17, 2004 2:00 pm

aight, here's my picks....

1st Place: (overall nicest car)
http://www.rcinfo.dk/forum/index.php?ac ... si&img=510
This entry just screams Fast n Furious. Not that FnF is the look people should be going for, but we all know how popular that look is, and this entry pulls it off with class - it's not over the top, too gaudy or flashy - it's just right.
The colors work brilliantly with each other. The blue-ish headlights, amber blinkers, chrome, green, and black - just beautiful.
This reminds me of something we'd see here at bitPimps, just in a larger scale. Though the tribal work maybe decals, it still looks fantastic.
And who doesn't love that color of green? lol

2nd Place: (best detail)
http://www.rcinfo.dk/forum/index.php?ac ... si&img=617
I chose this purely because of the painted graphic on the side of the door - very cool. Looks like it might have been a stencil, not sure - but doesn't look like a decal just slapped on.
The base color had to of been hard to work with to find a complimentary color.
The CF hood looks like one of the better jobs out of the entries as well.
I also like how the tail lights faintly shine through.
Looks like lots of attention was paid when making this one.

3rd Place: (best idea)
http://www.rcinfo.dk/forum/index.php?ac ... si&img=610
I don't know if all hoods on these things open up or not, and typically I could care less if they do - however I do like this one. There's actually "stuff" under the hood so that makes it more practical to have an opening hood. But that's not what did it for this entry. It's the name "Pauly" on the rear that matches the gold flames with no lights, and shines gold/amber with lights. Brilliant idea. The body some what desires more color, but hey - somebodies gotta own a black/silver/gold one.
The disc brakes behind the rims are a very cool touch.

4th Place: (craziest car)
http://www.rcinfo.dk/forum/index.php?ac ... si&img=609
Talk about originality. There's not a single other entry there that went this far creatively and still looks good. Probably not very practical, but hey who cares - it looks great. The chop job and addition of a trailer are just two of the things that set this one apart from the other entries. A little too much going on with the body, but again - the wooden trimmings make up for that. I'm impressed with the originality and how well it was pulled off.


That was tougher than I thought, you'd think I'd know by now.
Pass along a word of congratulations for me to all those who entered. There were some really slick rides in there and I enjoyed looking at them. They did a great job.
