Scale does not matter as far as friction is concerned. It would definately matter, BUT getting ceramics for the sake of it isn't going to matter. For instance, atomicmods.com has a complete ceramic set for $30ish. They absolutely can not be Abec 5 Ceramics (120K RPM) because those are $20 for JUST the two tranny bearings. I can't even find high grade ceramics for the wheels yet, but boca is pretty good about staying on top of the RC world and maybe they will put out something. VBX has always seemed overpriced to me, but thier high-grade bearings are top notch. Atomic mods hasn't released info on thier bearings other than the sizes. I wonder what composition, Abec/ISO tolerances, seals, etc. One would assume they are metal, single shielded, with chrome grooves and abec 1. The VBX bearings I was looking at were Abec 5, and they had bearings for the tranny that were over $40 ea I believe. Of course they were hybrid ceramic (not full ceramic which I can't find at all) with stainless races and shields. REALLY nice Abec 5s. They even have ceramics with rubber shields (pretty awsome for cleaning) but rubber seals have lower rpms.

I don't really care at this point. I think I'm just going to order some atomic mods. I'm getting too old to be the best on the block.

Yeah, I used Abec 9 ceramics in a longboard of mine, and even experienced street skateboarders had issues just keeping balance while standing still. The friction coefficient is insane.

They typically last 5x as long as normal bearings, which is a long long long time if you take care of them.
