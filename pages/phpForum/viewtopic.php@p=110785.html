I'm glad you like them, that was my one disappointment. I used blue flip flop on the front end, but you can barely see it. I don't know if I didn't lay it heavy enough, or if its just like that. Live and ya learn. Stuff like this just makes me better.

Thanks for noticing, its been very difficult doing what I want to do in the confines of Micro-T bodies. I kinda wish I put some drop shadow under the stripes, but wasn't sure I could pull off that much shadowing in such a small area. I like this though, because with the rectangle shape of the body, it almost looks like a flag.
