ok my coworker had to get a toner cartridge today so i went along and low and behold they had chevelles and torinos. so i got the chevy

ive only ran it for about 2minutes but heres my take on them:

looks:
the body is superb. absolutly the best body on any bit or zz to date. the only se ive seen where the rear sits higher than the front!! the bumpers are separate pieces of plastic. any chevy or oldschool muscle fan will love this body.

electronics:

twitch is still there. the motor has a different looking contact on the back. bigger so im sure its the same or better than the old style.

speed/torque:

not sure yet but possibly improved. then again probably not if they didnt bother to fix the twitch.


so for those of you who just want the muscle bodies id wait a few weeks to see if they offer a body kit. im sure they will. but now i have two working ses so thats kinda cool i guess. time to bust out the soldering iron to fix this twitch.
