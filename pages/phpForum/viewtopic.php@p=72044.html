I get my EL wire from a friend who hooks me up with end pieces off great big rolls, and the inverters are usually parts from the same process. The wire is 0.9mm it has the smallest exciter wires you’ve ever seen, since I got a little out of practice it only took me 6 tries to solder them to the inverter wires without breaking or shorting. There are lots of people selling EL wire and inverters over the internet these days, even 1mm and smaller.

Since the car was made completely out of spare and left over parts I had no out of pocket expenses for this one at all. If you had a couple of broken SE’s laying around it wouldn’t cost much to make except for the circuit board, and they use to be pretty cheap. The SS bodies are sold separately, a Epoch or Iwaver CB, parts from one or two broken SE’s, extra hubs, extenders, and tires from a wheel kit, battery, and the lights. Except for the lights widening the rear axle is the only hard or tricky part.


Still working on the ZZMT tut,I need to take circuit board installation photo’s, got carried away on the last one and forgot, then after seeing Sat’s SE I just had to use my last IW02 CB on one instead of the tut, but I will finish just as soon as I get another CB.
