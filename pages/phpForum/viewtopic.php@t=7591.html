I was just thinking this baby is screaming for IRS. Ill have someting like that done on her just as soon as i get her in the mail. Im thinking some overland shocks, IRS, Brass U joints, the whole thing....cant wait


EDIT:

My buggy just came in the mail today, i got a good 7-10 seconds of play time out of it before i started ripping out the guts

People werent kidding when they said it was slow stock. It comes with two cells and a very tiny bit motor is used to power it. (thing wouldnt even run on carpet stock)

Now, onto the good stuff. Everything is cleaned out and its ready for an upgrade. Im thinking dual blue superslick motors with an SE board and SE steering. In the rear ill have a full IRS using some brass U joints. THe front sort of has a suspension and it works for the time being but that will also need to be upgraded. All in all the performace isnt up to snuff but the platform is great and im loving the body. Im thinking ill stick this sucker on the vacuform, make a mold and start making some lexan versions of this body for other projects
