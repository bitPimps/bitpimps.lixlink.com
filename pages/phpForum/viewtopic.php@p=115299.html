Post by redrustbucket » Wed Jan 16, 2008 5:56 pm

I charge 3x w/ about a minute to cool in between and run it off the ground till the wheels stop turning before recharging and the run times still suck charging off the controller.

I made up a charger with a 6v 800mah wall charger from Radio Shack for $20. It's pretty easy to wire up and way better. Just cut off the lead and solder it into where the batteries connect or get another plug to cut then you can disconnect it and use the controller with batteries when you travel.

Now I add a zzse bat. wired in parallel. It's more compact than dual mt batts. and the same voltage.

If you don't want to mod your controller, do what Hue said.
