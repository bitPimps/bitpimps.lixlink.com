Post by canabits » Sun Jan 08, 2006 1:49 pm

Radical Decals are waterslide decals. They are printed on solid sheets and need to be trimmed before application.

Some diecut HO sized peel & stick decals can be found at http://www.slotprospeedway.com/peelstickdiecut.html

Really great quality for a decent price and great guys to deal with.
As an added bonus the vinyl they use is very thin, minimizing the 'peel & stick' look once applied (more so if you add a clear coat over them.
