bloody hell!! i can't even remember what this thread was all about!!
he he, well i fergit who asked what but i mainly wanted the pb's for looks! but i find they are super grippy which i like. it flips a little with turns but if you think about the scale of things i'd like to see an f1 car turn that tight at that speed and stay upright! i'm using a booster pcb and that helps to control things much better. i took it for a run outside on the concrete and the lack of suspension prevents it from really staying on the ground but it was turning at full speed most of the time without flipping. after i fuck with the center of gravity it should handle much better. i'm thinking of moving the battery forward and cutting out some chassis so it sits lower, similar to hachi ryoku's mods http://community.webshots.com/album/135656737qEtQbh
i'm thinking foamies all round will help this baby out on concrete.
as for the front wheels, whoever suggested the wd40 tubing was right on the money! i like to widen the front wheels anyway and they rub if you don't put a spacer in. for the rear i just ground down some plastic from the inside of the left wheel so the tyre goes over the smooth part of the gear. still a ways to go as you can imagine but as it is this thing's a heap of trouble juz waitin'to happen!!
