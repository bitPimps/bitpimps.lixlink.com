I was at TRU today and came across this. It is a 1:32 scale Celica. There was also a EVO and Supra there. The detail on the body is pretty good and it is very light plastic. So I decided to see if I could get a xmod chassis to fit.

On the lowest setting the chassis will fit and I chopped down a CF driveshaft to fit. I also think a IW02 cb and so Li-ions will go into her.
