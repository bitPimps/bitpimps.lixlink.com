The BB/CF shaft mod is a real bitch to do. Only do it if you have some
real issues with the stock bushings. ie too much friction(shit runtime) or
bad vibrations. You have to be super careful drilling out the main support,
there's only a few thousands of an inch(no joke) of plastic left around the
bearings when your done, and even so, it needs to be stretched to press
the bearing in.
