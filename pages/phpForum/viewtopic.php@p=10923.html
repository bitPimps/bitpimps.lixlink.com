Well, I was kind of boored today, and remembered how my clone from the MRCC e-store was fairly torqy, so I got out my old lego toys and scrounged around and found some of those off-road type wheels. Then, I went downstairs and glued them on. After a few different glueing's to the front wheels and just 1 to each of the back wheels, I got them on. A VERY important part is that you must not have the little metal axles on the front wheels glued to the wheels or else it wont go because the wheels will just barely turn. Also, if you want a body, you will have to raise it alot, but I was too lazy to try to put on a body for these pics.
