Post by hue35 » Wed Sep 24, 2003 11:03 am

You could charge it at least four times from the controller. The best rule of thumb I've heard so far is charge it until it gets warm to the touch. If you can't feel a little heat on the bottom of the chassis, put it on for another charge. Superfly went Double Live Gonzo with this method once and the car got HOT... it had to have been real close to melting the plastic. Anyway, that didn't kill the battery, and the car still works fine.
