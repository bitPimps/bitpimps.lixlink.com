I just recently spent some time under a hot soldering iron working with one of these boards. My controller blinks red too when it is on constantly. I'm pretty sure it means low batteries since I've had mine in there for a while. Replacing those will at least give you the greatest potential for good reception. If you brave you can try soldering on a better antenna. I tried steel wire for guitars, some special size, and it's worked pretty well on other cars I've used it on, but on the X-Trek it doesn't seem to work well. I'm going to try finding some thin copper wire at my local shop or the next time I run to my pop's house. I'll let you know if that works.

Battery life is good and once you adjust the trim I find these to be pretty fun. I think I lucked out with the Ferrari I got because it has decent reception and pretty equal throw for left and right so it turns alright.
