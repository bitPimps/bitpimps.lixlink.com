After spending some time with it, I realized, the motor still has good pinch, it just felt odd when I was starting it, because the pull start was slipping. I also discovered the ear is cracked off the steering servo in such a way that it took the whole side of the case with it. I can replace both of those, but I'm running out of broken CEN car to rob from.

I went to the LHS for my birthday the other day, and decided to just grab whatever they had on hand. They actually had the '69 Camaro that I wanted, but it cost $7 more, and the wheels and tires already put me over budget, so I had to settle for a Skyline. I also got some HPI chrome mesh wheels, and V-groove tires.

When I was mounting the body I screwed up royally, don't know what I did, but I managed to get the mounting holes about a half inch off. So since I have two sets of holes, I wasn't feeling this body any more. I was just going to do a single color, when I decided, what the heck, let's experiment. I streaked in four shades of blue (if you count aqua as a shade of blue), grey, and backed it all with silver. The sunlight makes certain colors jump out. These pictures look good, but I should take some under different lighting, so you can see how it changes.

Anyways, it actually runs great. I wish I had a road coarse to race it on. Sedans are a little boring to me, if I had a road coarse to navigate, it would be much more interesting.. I can drift with the tires I used to have on it, but yesterday I was just blasting speed runs up and down my parking lot.

That's what I was saying when I posted the pics, different lighting make some colors jump out. First pics were done at sunrise, with forced flash. Second pic was taken at dusk, before a rain storm, with no flash. In the first pics you can see the Maui Blue, it almost glows, and the Bright Blue across the nose is always visible. In the after pic, the Ultramarine Blue pops out. I actually buried some spotted flames in that shade, and they should be obvious in that pic, but now they're buried under nitro crud, and the glare on the hood. When you look at it under incandescent lighting, the Aqua is predominate, with the darker blues apparent. It really is a cool paint job. Y'all should fly on over here and check it out in person.
