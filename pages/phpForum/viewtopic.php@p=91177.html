I really hate to tell anybody what to do with their rig, especially when it's more awesome and trail worthy than any of mine, but I noticed in your layout that the top green Monster Idler gear is setup as a straight transfer rather than a reduction. So, despite having 5 gears, you're running a 4 gear ratio. Depending on pinions, that setup is around 71.92:1 or 83.95:1, with rearranging those gears on it you could drop it to like 288:1 or 366:1.



If you really want to make that thing move slower than your grandma, you could rearrange your gears like found here http://www.gcrossett.com/zipzaps/modifi ... /index.htm
