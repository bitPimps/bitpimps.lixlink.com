For those that dont know, it is 40 mhz B.

It has a much larger range than my 27mhz Compact Char g does which has the crip cap removed. It reaches about 5-6metres.

The handling seems to be better than my Compact, however, havn't tried it with anything in it but the standard 1.6

The lower chassis look great, compared to the old one.
