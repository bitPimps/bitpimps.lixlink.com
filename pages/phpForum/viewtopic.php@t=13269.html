Post by betty.k » Fri Jan 03, 2014 8:48 am

1. double cell will increase the steering power,beyond that you could wind your own steering coils.(fatter wire) the q steer has the steering coils (electro magnets) laid side by side. the microsizers have the steering coils on the opposing sides of the magnet. much better imho.

2. stronger magnet, look for a rare earth magnet to replace the stock one.

3. try one of the pcb's that has a motor for steering, (tank steering) you can use an exsisting tx laid out for a car so long as it's the same frequency. you may need to play with the polarity of the motors. you'll need to marry this front end to the back end of your choosing.
