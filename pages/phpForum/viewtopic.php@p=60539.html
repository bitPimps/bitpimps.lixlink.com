Post by DarkTari » Sun Nov 28, 2004 6:57 pm

Yeah It was one of the last "untouched" rides in his garage. I'm workin
on his 2nd Supra. off and on, (that rear spoiler is a pain) so I figured
let me start on another one to take the pressure off. Red & Black are his
favorite colors (Bulls) so I decided to try the Anodized paint that Hog used
on his Monster Truck. Did a silver undercoat then laid the anodize and it
came out Candy Apple Red. The wheels did look anodized when dry so
it all went pretty well. There were lil issues aroung the top but hey !
Very Pimp, what Ya running under all that candy apple bling?
Under the body is just a stock Rally Racer chassis which he has run the
shit out of, til the rear axle breaks loose. I replaced with another clone
rear, some new fronts (the old were scarred bad) and knuckles to tighten
the steering. He put a KIT 2.6 in it just before the Pimp'n so it runs
strong & fast and with the body drop'd, it handles like a slot (with clean
tyres). Yeah he's real happy with it now, it's always been one one his
favorites.
