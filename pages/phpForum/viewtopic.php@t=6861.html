steelwoolghandi wrote:I like the color of the mustang is that a Testers color?
it was an experiment, first i layed down a layer of white, then used the
dupont green/purple metallic flake to layer on some color. The flakes built up in the body crevices creating an outline of the body lines in grey.
The smooth areas are tinted purple or green depending on the light.
lol, it does look like pottery glaze.

The putty is just some generic automotive grade epoxy putty. It is my most used car so it takes a beating, the putty is tough though, havent had
it crack off yet. the paint on the door handles is showing some wear from rollovers.

To charge the 3 and 4 cell cars, I modified the radio shack dodge viper charger stand to use a 6v dc wall plug and added a charger pot from the zz controller.
