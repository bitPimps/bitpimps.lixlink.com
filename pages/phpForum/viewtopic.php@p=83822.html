Ok this isn’t Max but I though someone might like to see it anyway.

These are prototype coreless motors (built to order from the factory just for the Epoch), and a nice little aluminum ball bearing motor mount to hold them, Max’s mount looks similar.

I’m letting the cat out of the bag here but in the near future several versions of these little coreless brutes might just show up for a “Shotgun” motor mount. That’s right you just flick open the back, shove the motors in the little round holes, and pull the trigger to blow the competition away. Sort of like the rest of our micro sized toys, only faster and made out of a high quality ultra strong and light composite material with ball bearings.


A motor change takes only seconds in the pits, hm wonder why I mentioned pit stops.
