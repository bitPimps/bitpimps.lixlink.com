Post by extrememicros » Tue Nov 04, 2003 8:20 am

At this time, we have decided to make the site strictly wholesale only site.

The change will take place on November 11th. Until then, all orders for retail will be honored and filled. After that, the site will sell motors only, and to resellers.

Our reseller policy will be fairly lax. IE, we won't have a huge application, and all that junk. Just need to prove you sell goods, or want to begin to sell goods.

Minimums will most likely be 100 motors at a time and we are strongly considering flat pricing regardless of RPM count.

The other items on the site will no longer be offered. We will probably try to move the remaining tubes out in a different manner, and since batteries are fairly easy to come by, we will no longer stock those either.

I have checked a few US sellers of motors for wholesale pricing, even directly with a couple manufacturers, and our TBA pricing will be far more competitive and hope that our potential customers can see that as well.

We do want to take the time to thank each and every customer who has supported us over the last six months or so. We appreciate all of your support since our inception.

Feel free to contact us for further details.

Thanks again
Todd Cuzzort
