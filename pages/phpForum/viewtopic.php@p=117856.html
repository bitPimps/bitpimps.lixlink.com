I bought myself a bunch of stuff back in May for my birthday. All that stuff was in the hopes of making a nice RC shell (to use this time )
Yes, it's july and it still isn't finished, blame the educational system.

Here's the progress of my little project.

Edge Slide Racing Wheels Chrome,7mm & 10mm offset From Genkidoridori.com
HPI GT wing set (85612), and
HPI tuner kit (85613)



The chrome on the intercooler was peeling with the tape, so I had to do a little rescue. It looks a little rough around the edges, but alot better than it was

Before:



After :



The shell arrived a long time after the other stuff, it's an HPI Nissan 350Z



After LOTS of drawing, erasing, re-thinking, tossing and turning, I finally came up with a paintjob. It will be Green wit 3D ghost crystals.You'll notice some bands behind the door, those will also be ghosted in different greens. But, I need a totally different green all together. One that will fill the background of the crystals. I'm thinking either mint-green, or using a light coat of smoke to make a slightly darker green.



Just a shot of the whells I'll be using



Last night I did the CF hood, roof and boot. It was a painful learning experience and well worth it, have a look:







I still have to buy the Green and other colours to finish the paintjob. Also I'll be looking into getting some decals made.

Took me about 2 months to get this far cuz of university finals, hopefully it'll be finished by the end of the month.

Lemmie know what you think of it so far

Maxx
