Post by frizzen » Wed Sep 19, 2007 7:58 pm

Yeah... I let the magic smoke out of an Mtroniks Viper RV15 today.

I charged up one of my old 1500 mah NiCad sport pack, dropped it into the truck, the servo centered and the motor started making some noise like I needed to reset the neutral point, within a couple seconds smoke was pouring out of my esc, I yanked the deans plug as fast as I could, but it's definately FUBAR. The Positive input wire desoldered itself and completely ejected itself from the esc. With what happened, I'm thinking it managed a deadshort somewhere inside the potted electronics. I'll grab a pic when I find the camera.

The mtroniks Limited Warranty looks like it probably will NOT be willing to help me since I swapped from a Tamiya connector to a Deans Ultra, yes I had the polarity right on all parts concerned.
"Mtroniks Ltd. guarantee this product to be free from factory defects for 24 months from purchase date, verified by reciepts. This does not cover... ...alteration to original connectors, switches, or wires (apart from the fitting of an in-line fuse)..."

So, should I even bother trying to contact mtronics about a possible warranty claim, or am I just fucked and out $40 + shipping?
