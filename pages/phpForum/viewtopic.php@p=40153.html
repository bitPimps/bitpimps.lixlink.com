Post by ph2t » Sat Mar 13, 2004 5:18 pm

Got my Madforce earlier this week and I gotta say that it's a shitload of fun! This thing can go over anything. I've run it inside my house (floorboards) and outside on concrete and grass no problems. In the dirt is leaves the coolest tracks and can donut with the best of them.

So far the mods I have done are a 2x2 stack of IRF7389 fets (Thanks Aaron!). Also I've got a 35 turn motor from www.minizworkshop.com that works very weel (faster than the Kyosho X-Speed and an insane amount of torque). I've got the 12 tooth pinion installed and the top speed is insane.

I'm also running with 750mAh NiMH's that reallly pack a punch. So far I've gotten it to wheelie for a total of 6m's, lol....

I'm very familiar with that from racing 1/10th scale off road. You can actually control how level it is while it's in the air with your throttle. Nail the throttle, in the air, and the nose will lift. Slam on the brakes. The nose will drop. Really it's the same thing a car does on the ground if you think about it. Ideally, for the safty and long term durability of your car you would be wanting to land flat.
