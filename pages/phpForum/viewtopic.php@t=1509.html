Well I went out and bought me one. It's crap.
The only thing bad is how they put the knuckles together. They did the reverse pin method to mount the wheels on the knuckles. I used to swap out my wheels on bits this way so that I wouldn't need to drill into the rim. the problem is that if the knuckle hole is too tight the wheel will catch and not spin free enough. That is the problem with this car. It doesn't wanna go straight. I tried swapping out wheels with a bit, knuckles and all, but unfortunatly the knuckles on this car are shorter. What I'm gonna have to do is totaly remove the wheels, drill through the rim, and put them back together the right way. I really don't want to do this so I'm going back to wallmart and returning my shit. I'll wait to see what they come out with next.
On a side note, they had the chevy 150s pulling boats up in that piece. They are nkoks but branded with the motorworks name. I guess an exchange might be in order. They also had single packs of the nkok escalades and boats.
