Post by Sdog » Tue Oct 26, 2004 9:33 am

the MT trucks really are a lot of fun. I have been pretty surprised at what they can run over considering the suspension istn't anymore then an og front suspension set up. I also initially thought it wouldn't have enough ground clearance due to the steering knuckles, but none of this seems to stop it at all. They have tourque out the ass.

They also seem pretty well built and just about any shell looks cool on them.

The only problem I have run in to so far is the drive train seems to get squeaky after I run it a bit, especially in reverse....doesn't hurt the performance at all....just squeaky as shit.

I definitely recommend picking at least one up...get the motor upgrade too. It will climb just about anything with the stock motor, but its fun to launch it off of small ramps with the orange setup.
