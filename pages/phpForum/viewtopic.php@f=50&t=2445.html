  • Quote

Post by payaso » Wed Oct 29, 2003 12:30 pm

I use to be on this motor kick, and have bought almost all of them, but kinda stopped when the "Perfection" series hit the market.. I figured around 50 motors was enough.

Ever since perfection hit, that's kinda the bulk of the motor talk lately.. I've been hearing all these great reviews on how good their motors are, and how fast they are, so I couldn't resist anymore.

I now have three of the 4.2s (2 pros, and a King)

All I can see that they are good for is torque. The speed on them is shit compared to a 3.8 from bitchar-Gmotors. (Kyles)

Back in the day, people were always asking him to make a faster motor than the 3.8 he made, but he always claimed to making as fast as he could already.. 3.8 is pretty much the limit.

His parents own a factory that makes motors for pagers, so he knows his shit.

Now after testing out some higher RPM 4.2 motors against his 3.8s, I see that he was not being lazy or BSing. His 3.8s out run any motor I have in flat out top end speed.


With all that said, I feel like I've been lied to by perfection. I feel like they are counting on people (kids) with out alot of loot to buy their product first cause the higher RPM rating. And I guess if all you had to compare it to was the stock 1.0/2.2 that your car came with, then yeah, it's a fast little gem...


Namuna has tested the 3.8 from Kyle, and it tested true to it's stated RPM, so how could a 42,000 motor be twice as slow as a 38,000 RPM motor?


I think "Perfection" would call their motors 7.0s if that's what people wanted to hear.

Does anybody else have these motors to test? (3.8 from Kyle/4.2 Perfection)


Sorry for the rant, but I was so hyped up on getting a motor that was gonna be a butt load faster than my fastest one, so when I got it, and felt the dissapoinment in it, I had to share it with you guys.. I feel like perfection are liars.

They should just put on the site, Well, it's really a 2.2 with a different color endbell on it, but we can put stickers on the box that say whatever you want..



End rant.

Post by SuperFly » Wed Oct 29, 2003 1:01 pm

I agree that the rated RPMs are not that meaningful in determining a motor's true "performance." I find also that different motors behave differently under certain circumstances, like with dual cells or mosfets. It kind of reminds me of wattage output ratings on stereo or guitar amps. I've had 25 watt amps blow away 200 watt amps.

For me, one characteristic that that almost trumps overall speed is ability to coast. That quality helps me turn the fastest lap times and have the driving feel that most closely resembles real driving. One of my favorite motors for this reason is TinyRC's Fomula 34 motor. I have one of Kyle's pink motors (I think that was a 3.5) and it was my fastest motor by quite a bit. The biggest problem for me with his motors was longevity.
