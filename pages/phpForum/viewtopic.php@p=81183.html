nope, sad to say i cant take credit for that awsome RV bit that SWG did.

On an update on the baja bit, ive been testing out the suspension and the off road ability of this bit and i must say that im impressed but it has left me wanting more. The suspension is perfect but with the dual cells i dont feel im getting the raw power that this bit really deserves. Normaly i skip the small bit motors and go straight to the zzmt or the superslick motors but since this bit was so small i figured a zz motor would work just fine,, and it does but since when are any of us ready to settle for something with less power than we know we can have? Im in the process of trying to cram in a zzmt motor, the trick is getting that massive motor in there and not having it get in the way of my rear articulation. Ill make it work, its just going to take a little more time.

Took her in the sand and i shoot a bit of a rooster tail but im looking for atleast 6 inches of throw from the back of this baby before im happy.

EDIT: Turns out the zzmt motor is just too damn tall and gets in the way of the articulation at every angle. Its looking like a superslick (1:43 scale) motor is going in its place. I found a purple (2700rpm) motor and im working on getting that thing in there. Its a little long but i think i can make it work
