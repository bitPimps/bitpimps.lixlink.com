Post by gnubee » Thu Nov 04, 2004 11:06 pm

i run lipos in my mini-t mamba. lipos are not for kids to play with. if you over discharge they die. if you overcharge they bust open and start on fire when the insides get oxygen. and water speeds the fire. we are not talking about a blue puff of warm air like when you spray butane on your pants and light it on fire (dont do that either). we are talking about one of the hottest flames you can make with readily available end consumer ingredients. and it shoots out in a jet from any puncture in the casing. no spark is needed to ignite.
danger!
they must be handled with care and a disregard for what may happen to your spending money. if you are ok with your heavily modified toy turning into a pile of melted black plastic with wires sticking out of it and you are an adult willing to take risks they are the best battery in terms of size, voltage, weight, and capacity but there is no cheap and safe way to use them.
