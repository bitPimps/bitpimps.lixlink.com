Post by Smoke&Mirrors » Sun Jun 08, 2008 3:36 am

You know would be a cool idea on top of that, if we are going to go there, just combine two crawlers, 2 pcbs, 2 steering motors/housings, 2 motor housings. Put one steering housing in front, a middle section for the pcb's and motors, and then another steering housing to act as the rear. Then just reverse the two steering wires on the pcb board of the rear section and flip the motor that controls the rear end opposite of the front motor and that way you can control the front and back independently with one modded controller. If you know what I mean. Or you could just use one motor, but still flip the steering wires on the back end so that what looks like left to you will turn the wheels left. It's a cool idea and if you hydro the back as well, it'll be like "the stick" and a very capable crawler.

[YouTube]http://youtube.com/watch?v=FPrJWc4J7i0[/YouTube]

As I recall reading, he had each wheel wired up to a separate servo and maybe even motor so that he could control each one independently.
