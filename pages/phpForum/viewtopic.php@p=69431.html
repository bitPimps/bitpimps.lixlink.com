Some of you might remember my F-150 I did up Dub City style. This is that car. I didn't like the last paint job I did on the F-150, and been too lazy to paint it again, so I've been looking for a bodies to fit on the chassis. The problem is the Ford's hood line allowed me to tab lower a bit too low, and nothing else fit. When I thought, "Hey, why not another truck."

These SE truck bodies are a bit too wide for the chassis, but I just thought it looked pretty badass with the wheels all sucked into the wheel wells, and the body practically laying on the ground. So I had to show it off.





I think I may just leave it with this body. Wasn't it Sessiz that was saying that he wanted to see this body with a blue and white flame job? Well you may just get your wish.
