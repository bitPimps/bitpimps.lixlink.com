Post by crazydave » Fri Jan 16, 2004 6:25 pm

TIN wrote:I painted the body with krylon, and every coat would react somewhat like water and oil. The paint would seperate and pool all over the body.
That's exactly the problem I have with cheap non-transparent clone bodies, using various types of laquers.

Best you can do is like Hue says, sand it really good and primer it. The primer will stick but it still doesn't like to sit very level. I always have to give it a sanding after the first couple coats, whether if I use primer or laquer, and then everything goes smoothly from there.

Really I just try really hard to avoid wanting to paint bodies like that.
