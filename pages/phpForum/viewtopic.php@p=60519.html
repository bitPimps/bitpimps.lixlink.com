for the moment, yeah. stay nimh's.

li-ions have the issue where (under high current load) they heat up and their internal resistance increases, this limits their current output, or to say "punch", lol...

Post by ph2t » Fri Dec 03, 2004 10:14 pm

hey arch, yes you can use the plasma ammy in the squat bb can BUT. and it's a big BUT I'm sorry. The plasma commutator is extra fatty and will have issues with the brushes from a closed can type motor housing (squat, 3racing, yeah racing cans, etc....). This is due to the fact that the brushes aren't desigened for a fat comm. Open cans' like the ATOMIC linked at the begining of the post are designed for the fatter comms.

I would suggest you put the Ultra Dash armatures in the squat cans. I say this because the Ultra Dash has the same amount of turns as the plasma dash. But the ultra has the stock sized commutator. This means no issues with fitting the squat can's brushes.

Make sense? I hope it does......

Going form the video's I've seen of your RCP track, neo's might be the best setup for you. Nice hard arse torque to deliver you around all those tight corners you have . If you were racing at a bigger track , I'd use the magnets that came with the plasma, excellent ferrite magnets.
