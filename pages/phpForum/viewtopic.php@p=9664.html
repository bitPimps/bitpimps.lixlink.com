Yes, it is that kit, and I love the motors. The 2.8 has good top speed and tons of torque. Pushed to it's limit using the green 9.86:1 gears, it just starts to flip. Back off in time, and you'll be driving on 2 wheels. It would flip, but it was difficult to get it to.

The 3.4 smokes. Easily the best motor I've owned. It's very fast, probably true to the 3.4 rating, but still probably as fast as my pink endbell 3.8 was, and puts Perfection 3.8s to shame. Gobs and gobs of torque. It reached top speed nearly instantly. It's a little harder to flip a ZZ than a bit, and this thing was not only flipping, but doing barrel rolls back onto it wheels. One time I got it to roll about a foot. Flipped twice, and away we went.

The motors are worth the price of admission, and all the other goodies are just icing, but I love icing.
