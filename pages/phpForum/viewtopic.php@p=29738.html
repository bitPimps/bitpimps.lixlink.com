Post by Namuna » Sat Oct 18, 2003 10:27 pm

- Nominal is 4.8v to 6v (depending on NIMH or Alkalines)...I can't say if the XMods PCBs are the same as ZZs as far as how it passes power to the motor. I'd think your idea of using LiPoly should be viable.

- Mini-Zs are 1/24th scale, XMods are 1/28th...The sizes are VERY close, there might be some cross-compatibility for parts, don't know at this time what will or won't work.

- Haven't done a hardline comparison yet, but so far what I have done leads me to believe the Z is still the champ (as far as steering goes)...Other than that, Stock vs. Stock I have to give it to XMods. The performance and options available, for the cost just plain KICKS ASS.

- For the entry fee of $50 my impression is 'Nice'. Add a little Body Kit and some different Motors and then you're in kick ass territory.

- With the Starter Kit, which the Acura RSX is what I got, it comes with 8-tooth gearing (which means high top-end, so-so torque)...A nice big roller rink would be ideal for this setup.

- Car uses Triple-As. I've never used alkalines, always NIMH. With the Mini-Z, runtimes are up to an hour. I'd think the X would be similar or even a little better.

- JBweld not needed, the Hood, front & rear bumpers, side-skirts and mirrors are all interchangable and they're all screwed-on.
