Post by CaboWabo » Thu Jan 22, 2004 4:03 pm

you're gonna have to find what works best for you.

the most common methods are:
- use needle nose pliers to pull the pin out. (some heat up the pin a tad with a soldering iron - others dont)
- use small flat head micro screw drivers and pry the pin out.
- cut the rim right off the pin and then pull the pin out.


I prefer using the needle nose pliers method.
good luck pimp, it's well worth it.
