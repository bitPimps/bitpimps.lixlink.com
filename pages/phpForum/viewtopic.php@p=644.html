well if they have side mirriors the generally are not vaccum molded. im not saying that you couldnt have them on a body. but you would have to have a 2 piece mold or a mold that would disolve once the body is formed. these solid mirriors may also be plastic welded to the body so it looks like they were originally formed that way also. if you saw how an auctual vac molding machine worked you would understand what im talking about.

I am refering to the type of vac machine that pulls the plastic over the mold. i dont know if there are other types of machines out there.
