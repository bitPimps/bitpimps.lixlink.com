Well , I went flying today ( rc planes ) I have a RCM Trainer .40 , with a Hi tech laser 4 radio , and a Magnum .46 BB 2 stroke engine. So , my first few flights went good , until my 5th flight I was flying a figure eight. Of course something had to go wrong , my left wheel fell off , I got scared but kept calm , I attempted to land once , but got scared and pulled away. I lined up again and once I got close I killed the throttle and turned the throttle trim all the way down thus shutting off the motor , once my wheel touched I slammed the stick to the front/left keeping the axle the wheel fell off , off of the ground , and started to add some left rudder , and when the plane slowed down enough the axle finally hit , and dug into the ground and spun the plane around. I bent out the landing gear and put on the extra wheel I keep in my tool box it was a bit big but worked ok , the rest of the day went good Charlie got out a 3d plane he put a smoke system on witch was really cool.

Loctite is your friend .
