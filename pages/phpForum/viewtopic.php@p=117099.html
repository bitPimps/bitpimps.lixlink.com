Yes, most ZZSE bodies will fit over the Transformer chassis. Something to note is that the rims on the Transformers sit deeper on the wheels, which makes the tires look bigger. If you put other rims on, it looks normal. I also found that the Starsky and Hutch body (Ford Grand Tureno) body that I put on looks like it's been lowered and therefore more to scale. Nice.
