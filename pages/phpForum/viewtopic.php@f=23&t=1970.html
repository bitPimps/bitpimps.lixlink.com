Everyone loved my flames and graphics so much, I feel obligated to keep producing them, and keep trying to one up myself, but sometimes I like a car to be a solid color. I never bother showing you guys these cars, because I'm afraid after my other paint jobs you guy's would be unexcited about them.

Well I found this brand new can of paint behind the washer, left over from when I painted a guitar over a year ago. It was the exact color I wanted my 911 to be, but I just couldn't picture it with any kind of graphics. So it's just a solid color.

I love it, and wanted to show it off, but didn't want to bore you guys with it. I sat on this for 2 days, before I decided, screw it, everyone else can post their single color paint jobs.

So here it is, hope I haven't let you guys down.
