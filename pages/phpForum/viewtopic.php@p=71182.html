Post by sg219 » Sun May 22, 2005 7:12 pm

Make sure the servo horn is in the correct slot on the steering rod and that the steering rod isn't bend or jammed on a pebble or anything.

It also might be that one wheel isn't spinning freely. Hair, debris, over-tightening of the wheel nut.

Your diff might be installed incorrectly. I know I've had problems with the axles and the "D-shaped" ends fitting into the diff. Try making sure that both sides are all the way into the diff.

A bad bearing?

Bent axle?

Try that.
