Post by frizzen » Sun Aug 07, 2005 9:21 pm

I got kinda tired of the stock ZZ truck bodies, they don't look quite enough like real trucks to me. Even with offroader body mods, they still look a bit much like poser trucks people commute with or use to take kids to soccer in. Well F-that!

Trucks are to haul stuff, not people; obviously I need more bed than passenger room! Plus I've yet to see anybody make a singlecab, and it'll look way more badass on a zz-m chassis. So, let's customize!

Step 1: Make truck all kinds of naked by removing everything you possibly can.


Step 2: Break truck. <Pimp secret: thread.> You can make longer tonnau cover if you wish. <Optional: I also cut out part of tonnau support to make bed, also rear LED mounts removed>


Step 3: Break further, file flat, mockup, apply CA. Removed roof section cut up and CA to bed rails


Step 4: Wait for CA to flash, file down flush, <Optional cut rear window>, apply filler of choice to unwanted lines <Pimp Secret: CA + Talc>, file in new body/bed line, <Optional rework to front end and taillights for a secret mod>.


I'll go on more next post.
