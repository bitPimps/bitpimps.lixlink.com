Well shit, I did it... the stoopid way. No clear coat, no nothin'.
I just decided fuck it, I can make another set of decals if this
goes bad... I didn't want to clear coat the decals, then have it
go sideways... then I'd really be fucked. So, I just cut a very
thin strip of tape and masked off the top... right on the decal,
but the tape (Tamiya) was only about 1/16 of an inch wide,
which also made it easy to bend around the curves. Then, I
used lo-tack blue tape that I stuck to my pants to pick up
some lint, making it ultra lo-tack. I got that into place and
actually taped the blue tape to the Tamiya tape with the
leftover thin strips... and it worked! There's a tiny bit of bleed
on the rear spoiler, but that'll be an easy fix. Nothin' went
wrong around the decals, thank 'ol what's his name... Regis?
