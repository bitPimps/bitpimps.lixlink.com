A quick little tip on frisbee golf: That's all it is. It's pretty fun, and you don't need a real course to do it. You just have to make up your "holes", each with their own tee-off area and par.

Like, tee-off area is behind this drinking fountain, and the hole is the green garbage can at the bottom of the hill around the corner. It's all about how few frisbee tosses can you make to get from one place to another.
