I've got all the bits and tiny cars packed away. Been meaning to drag them out for a group shot in some semi-sunlight, but for some reason I was afraid of being bombarded by kids. Well there hasn't been many kids around here lately, so I was feeling pretty safe. When out of no where... my worst nightmare, I was bombarded by kids!

I don't know why I got so agitated, I started sweating profusely, because they were just saying, "I like the blue one, I like the black one." One kept saying, "You're so lucky, huh?" I wanted to say, "Not really, I spent alot of money and only half them survived." In retrospect it wasn't so bad, and I probably should have charged up a couple, but I don't do well with crowds.

So anyways, here's my 50+ micros altogether.


I guess I am pretty lucky.
