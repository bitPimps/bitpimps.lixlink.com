While these are not being made for Bits, a similar process could be used.

Tire Casting 101

Without spending alot of cash to test the idea you would use plaster in place of resin and silicone in place of the RTV. I would imagine you could use Moldbuilder [?I think that's the name] latex as well.

Most general purpose clear silicone's can be cut with mineral spirits to make it a little more flowable. And then there's always flowable windshield & glass sealer available in the automotive section at Wal-mart among other places [42g tube sells for a couple bucks]

I may have to give this a go myself. Those Hot wheels sourced wheels he's got the stubby tires on rock!
