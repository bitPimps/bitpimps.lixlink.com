Post by payaso » Mon Feb 07, 2005 6:59 pm

sg219 wrote:XXX-NT

I got the XXX fired up with just a new plug. Damn, I haven't had this much fun since I first got into RC's a year ago!!!!!!! 6 tanks already!!!

The only problen I'm having with the XXX, is that it lags when you hit the throttle.

What is the fix for this?
That sounds like you're fat on the LSN (low speed needle) After you come to a stop, the crank case will load up with fuel causing you to loose your snappy take off, or even kill the engine from flooding it... Retune will fix this.. There is a learning curve with nitro, and it seems like you're exactly where you should be.

When making adjustments to the carb needles, remember to make small turns. ie. 1/16, and 1/8 of a turn. (one full turn = 360 degrees)

Make an adjustment, and then run a few minutes. You won't get instint results when tuning, and they need to small.

You will get the hang of it after running a few more tanks, and eventually gallons.
sg219 wrote:T-Maxx

However, the T-Maxx won't turn over and stay running.

I removed the air filter and put a small amount of fuel down in there and it would turn over for a coule of seconds. So after that, I figured the problem is that the engine isn't getting any fuel or enough of it.

I removed the fuel line from the tank to the engine and let it syphon a little fuel out to make sure it wasn't kinked, clogged and to let any air bubles out. No problem.

Tried to start it up again and still nothing.

I am going on the assumption that it is just not getting fuel to the engine.

What adjustments can I make to get the fuel to flow?

Am I just overlooking something trivial or really simple?

It could very well be that no fuel is reaching the engine.. It's possible for the carbs on these engines to gum up with left over fuel. this is caused by not performing the correct after run procedures.

If the truck is going to sit for a day or two, you should remove fuel from tank, and burn the rest out of the crank case, and tehn drop some afterrun oil in the plug hole, and in the carb bore.

I do not perform this, cause my trucks don't usually sit for long without at least a quick fire up, and I don't mind tuning/wrenching on em. Both run like fuckers.


If you feel brave enough, just rebuild the carb, and go to the O.S. site and DL the specs, and default settings for that carb.. not hard to rebuild



It could be the plug as well.. What plug are you using now? Brand? Hot?

If you turn it by hand, do you have good compression on the flywheel?


You might want to trade some of the stuff you won't use, and get a "Drop-in" engine for it. There are plenty of good mills out there that will except the EZ-start system, and make great power. Lots of people fuckin hate the ez start shit, but I love it.




Let me know if any of that gibberish helped..lol
