popped the cherry......

  • Quote

Post by HirotoR34 » Thu Feb 03, 2005 10:33 pm

Right now, I'm kinda stalled on mods. Waiting on parts, cars, and paint, so I was bored. I noticed this Zip, sitting on my shelf looking really dumb with a Ram shell on it.
I origionally bought this ZZ for the H2 shell, and having never really modded one, (let alone own one) which gave me an idea. First, the ZZ rims weren't cutting it, so I performed Dr. Finklesworth's import racer wheel mod .
Then I started rummaging through old bit parts and found some cells, dusted off the old soldering gear and went to work. The dremel was lookin lonely, so I started cutting and grinding (that shit gets everywhere)
Further digging through parts, I found a MT motor. Big fun....

Hemi Ram? possible SRT-10? No boredom here......
