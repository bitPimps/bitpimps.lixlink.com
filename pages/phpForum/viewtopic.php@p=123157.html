Just keep reminding yourself, if you got fuel, air, compression, ignition, it's gotta work. If it don't work, just start eliminating the variables.

You said it has compression, so that's out. Make sure your air filter is clean, I always forget the air part, and one time I was frustrated, because I didn't see the air filter got clogged with fuel when the truck was turned upside down. Then there's the glow plug. You may have wore it out when you were running too hot, they're only a few bucks, might as well replace it, I keep spares around. That just leaves the fuel, and that can be tricky getting the right amount in there, but I'm sure you can work through it. You could do the pinch test, but I've got pretty good at listening and observing my motors. If its running rough, and loading up before it stalls at idle, the LSN is probably too rich. Pull the glow plug when it stalls and check if it is wet with fuel. If so it is definitely too rich. Blow out the plug, and burn off the extra fuel with your glow starter. Then lean the LSN about an 1/8th turn. But since your motor is not wanting to draw fuel, assuming the tank is ok, I'm guessing it is probably too lean, or you're not getting enough pressure. I would guess you could pressure test your tank by dunking it under water, and blowing in the hoses.

I also like to turn up the throttle trim on my Tx just until the it's running at temp. I forgot to turn it down in my video of the Mad Beast, you can hear its idling hard, that's bad on the clutch shoes. Any rate, I'm just getting the hang of nitro, hopefully I was helpful.
