Post by sg219 » Mon Jun 14, 2004 7:09 pm

Yes there is.

Get a small pot of H2O boiling and drop your treads in for a minute or so. Not too long or they will shrink too much and twist. Bird tried this technique when he got one and it was just enough to do the trick.

The only other thing to do would be to splice the tread and reglue it.

I bought a couple of those tanks awhile back on clearance for $4.88 each!! There fun to climb over and around stuff on the table or to have two people go at it and try to flip each other.
