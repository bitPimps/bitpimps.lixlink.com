OK dudes

so teambonzer has been on tour - 2.5 days in hong kong is a short stay, but still time to play

Met Steve at Toyeast (really nice guy - even bought me lunch). Picked up my boxed set of 20 mini's - opinion - Bonzer! - paint work is first class, chrome where chrome should be, working lights (headlights on all and roof lights as well for police cars - for the cars without a roof light, the wiring is in place so I guess you could add your own extra lights somewhere else.

Handle really well, fast out of da box, ran for 10 mins on first single charge.

Auto slow down on turning is cool.

Nuff said - top cars.

As soon as I get time I will film them racing against bits and zips so that you can compare. Also picked up one of them micro wireless vid cameras (10mmx10mm), already strapped it to a zz and tested on track - should make for some nice "in da cab" vids - watch this space.

PS the micro pro 4.2 motors he sells go like stink (thats good, for you yanks ), even with the camera and a 9v battery the zz rocks.
