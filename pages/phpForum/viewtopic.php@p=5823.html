Post by hogjowlz » Mon Apr 28, 2003 2:32 pm

ok i just picked up a jonny lightning thunderjet. its 1/64 pullback. the bodies are meant to go on slot cars and are fairly detailed plastic. windows are a separate piece of plastic which can be removed fairly easily. looks like it will fit on a bit with a little dremmeling on the chassis. its a baja bug. they gota buncha muscle cars as well.

wheels: fronts are really small diameter. rears(with tire) are as tall as a bit rim(without tire) and are about as wide as tomy pullback wheels. tires are pretty grippy like tomy pullbacks but thicker.

anyhow for $4.50 i got a sweet body. and a new set of wide grippy wheels.
