Post by crazydave » Thu Jul 13, 2006 5:56 pm

I guess I just expected more from the Losi name. My XXT and RC10DS of past were perfect. No issues, everything worked, and worked exceptionably well. My Stampede, it had issues. The shaft that the outdrives tighten around was too small, so it would strip often, even with the steel outdrives. The slipper pegs glazed over way too fast, and the slipper was difficult to adjust properly. The plastic motor mount would melt, distort, and shred spur gears when I took a hard knock and it was good and soft, allowing the motor to move around.

But then the Stampede was made overseas, and the MLST is made in China. That could have a lot to do with it. Regardless of the origin of manufacture I still expect a $200 toy to work correctly to begin with.


As for what I think the MLST needs. I want some of those aluminum wheel hexes. I'm interested in Driven Production's Delrin gear sets. They're gonna be selling the set with the pinion too. Apparently the geometry is off on the Losi pinion. Unfortunately a bunch of brushless whiners raised issue of the Delrin gears holding up to brushless, so now the release of those gears have been delayed until further testing. I would also like to get a MIP center ball diff, and center CVDs. Other than that, everything seems to work fine on my Losi, so I don't want to invest more than that. It's just that I see the potential in it, and want it to work properly, without having to worry about it, that's all.
