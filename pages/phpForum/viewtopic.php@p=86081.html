maxx- that car's AWD right? oversteer shouldn't be a problem at all if you lock the rear diff.

i did hear about your servo problem though, so while you're working with that:

keep the really stiff springs on the rear, and soften up the front; when you turn into the corner, turn a bit less; the looser rear end should break more easily but not come all the way around. should give you a little more time to countersteer.
