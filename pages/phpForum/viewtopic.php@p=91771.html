I don't know, this came out well enough, but when it came together it was just so typical of me, that it almost seems generic.

I have a few of these Lancer bodies from MS Speed Sets. I've already botched a couple, and did another I didn't like. When I sat to draw the graphics, I didn't know what I wanted. I doodled for an hour, and went in 5 different directions. It seems to me the Lancer doesn't really work well with too wild of graphics, and I was limited on colors also. So I said screw it, I'll just do some plain ol' flames again. Just to give it a little twist, and tone them down for this body, I made them kinda of a single lick going in almost like a band.

I think I got some good flow on these flames licks, and I think I should be happy, but, like I said, I don't know, its just kinda been there done that.
