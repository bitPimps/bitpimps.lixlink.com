You get to choose who wins Ride of the Month ("ROTM") each month - here's how.

Each week, the bitPimps will continue to pick a Ride of the Week ("ROTW"). Once the month is over, the bitPimps will create a poll (like this one) which will allow users to vote on the cars entered that month.

Most votes wins.
