As far as your voltage checking method; you don't have to go that far for a reading, you only need to touch the testing prongs fo the voltmeter to the charge points on the bottom of the car.

How long did it take them batteries to get to 1.395v? My thinking here is it's a false full reading...While it might say 1.4v, it might actually only have a slight charge.

Here's another thing you could do to test the batteries; get the smallest light bulb you can find (like a christmas tree bulb) and rig it up with longer wires to connect it to the car batteries. You don't have to take the car apart, just make the connection via the charging pad.
