Yep another Skyline, sorry, but I like 'em.

I'm not much in the mood for any major modding lately, but I have a few MS speed sets laying around that I just can't quite leave stock.

I happy with the way it came out, but these pictures are kinda shit, it looks better in person. That's ok though, its just a quickie project and I gotta leave for work now, so we'll just deal with it.
