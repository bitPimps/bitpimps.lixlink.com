Just got done playing with it for a bit...

First thoughts

- Well built, nicely detailed.

- Built-in lights are a nice feature

- Controller is not the greatest (typical knock-off style)

- Runs for quite some time - haven't timed it yet, but it seems to run a lot longer on a single charge than any bit I've owned.

- Way over-packaged.

- Antanae is not flexible. Gives it a nice solid feel out-of-the-box, but it's going to get crushed at some point. Difficult to fit with other bit bodies as a result.

- Tires are too small up front and too hard. Tread on tires makes it bounce around.

- Does not fit any of my bit rear axles!!! This is a major problem. Looks like bit wheels will fit without any mods, but I'll have to fit bit rims to the Mini axle.

- Body fit is tight - really tight. Will need to be modified to fit other bits.

Overall impression is good, especially for the price. I can't help but think how cheap these would have been without all the fancy-schmancy packaging. It handles well, stock motor is good (3.8?) and seems to be well-built.

That's what I know.

Maark

Speed was good for an out-of-the-box bit. It has a 3.5 motor in it. I do like the variable speeds (65% on corners and 100% or 65% on straight-aways) - great control for those tights spaces or difficult courses. It's a bit slow for me, I'll be playing around with different motors this week (3.8, 4.0, 4.2). Haven't even touched the gears yet.

I do hate those tires. The detail is nice to have if it's just sitting on the shelf, but they are rock hard and the tread causes the car to bounce around a bit. Probably have to make some new ones by hand or go with the aluminum rims/tires.

Overall I think it's great - needs some little improvements here and there. You are basically buying it because it's a Mini. It doesn't work well with your other cars, but it's a cool thing to have.
