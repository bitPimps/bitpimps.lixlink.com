Ok guys so I got this sweet little honey yesterday and here are some of my thoughts on it;

Pros

Takes the place of 4 controllers (obvious, but one of the best features)

Better design than stock controller ie; more ergonomic

The buttons rock, 8 points of movement

No clicking when buttons are depressed, lack of momentary buttons (pics coming soon)

Saves money on batteries lol

Cons

Only one, that I have so far... Range sucks!
But we already knew this, Telescopic antenna mod is a must.

As far as the mod that hog loves so much, the battery mod... unfortunately that may prove to be a bit more difficult. Because of the cases streamline design, and 1 battery being on each side of the back of the controller (for those who aren't familiar with this config. pics coming shortly) it leaves little room for anything inconspicuous. Time will tell though.
