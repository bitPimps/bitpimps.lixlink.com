Post by sidewinder » Sat Jan 30, 2010 3:24 pm

I've got a VXL Bandit and love it (same as Rustler but shorter arms and buggy wheels). It'z kind of amazing how tough the thing is. I've run it head on into a steel pole at top speed (on 7 cell battery--so maybe 40-45mph) and all that broke was one A-arm, a wheel and a rod end on the shock. After fixing that I hit a tree and took out another wheel...not even a hint of a crack in the frame. And the good thing about the Rusty is you can get RPM arms for them and eliminate the weak point that the Bandit has so it should be just about bullet proof.

IDK about the others but I can give a thumbs up to the Rusty. Oh, and BTW, with a 20c 3S lipo the Rusty will hit 70+mph. I borrowed a lipo for my Bandit once, it was insane--it got really hot, but then afterward I realized i was supposed to change the gearing. The lipo and gear change is absolutely on my summer must do list.

Post by PimpFloyd » Sat Jan 30, 2010 9:38 pm

I can vouch for the Traxxas electrics on your list. I have both the Bandit (XL5 at the moment) and the Stampede (VXL/LiPo). I really like my Bandit but just keep in mind that buggies are somewhat limited by ground clearence.

The Stampede is a GREAT all around basher and is at home on most all surfaces. Like most Traxxas offroad stuff it's silly tough, inexpensive to maintain and parts are more available than porn. With a VXL/LiPo combo, it's the only RC I have that consistantly makes me giggle out loud. I run mine in the snow and with the right tires and frozen snow, the whole freakin planet becomes the Stampede's play ground.

I'll throw the Slash in here as well...I REALLY like this truck ALOT. It has alot of scale appeal both in the looks department and the driving department. It may not be the first truck you think of as a basher but that's what I do with mine and it's great fun and competes with my Stampede for the truck I'm most likely to grab on any given day.

I can't vouch for any of the nitro on your list except to say it seems I've read nothing but good things about the Savage. Much like the Reaper mentions, people seem to be fiercely loyal to that truck. It does look mighty cool!

Good luck!
