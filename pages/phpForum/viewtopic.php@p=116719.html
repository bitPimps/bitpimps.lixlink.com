Starting to get back into r/c again...

Took a bit of hacking to get the rc18b body to fit on the xray mt as
it has less available space.. mostly due to the MT having 8 shocks and
dual shock towers. Had a bunch of lexan left over..so i made a front
air dam to spice it up.

this will be my primary fun car for the summer. The ride height of the MT
makes it quite usable off road.

as for mods..(only whats necessary)
mamba 6800+2s lipo
8 alum oil shocks(4xray+4atomic adjustables)
ball diffs


blue pearl and color change white(blue->purple->pink)
