Post by CaboWabo » Thu Mar 06, 2003 6:43 pm

Here's a little preview of the pullback mod's I've done over the past couple of days along with some new paint jobs. I'll write an article and stuff when I get back. The gist is, I took 2 pull backs, and 2 bits. Took 2 pullbacks and took off all 4 rims/wheels, giving me 4 rear and 4 front. Took 4 rear, modified a bit and viola - a bit rollin on dubs, all four back rims/wheels of a pullback. Then I took 4 front, modified a bit and viola again - a bit skatin on daytons, all four rims/wheels of a pullback.

Thanks Yag!

After doing the pullback mod, I'm going to say that doing it with all 4 front wheels (from pullback to bit) was a little easier than doing it with all 4 rear wheels. I like them both, but the rear wheels mod causes you to cut more on your body to allow for the wider front tires to turn. You'll have to fabricate an axle for both mods - which seemed harder to do on the 'rear wheels mod'. More info coming soon.
