Don't know if any of you guys are into slot cars. I'm not talking about those little 1/64th or the 1/43rd. I'm talking about 1/24th cars, these are nothign like a 1/64th or 1/43 they don't have magnets to hold them to the track. You actually have to drive these cars or you will de-slot.


I just got back from the races and I'm quite proud of my self, I got 2nd place, the race before last I had also gotten second place. I won $6.00(Yeah now I'm rich) The way it works is first place gets %50 of entry fees, second gets %30 and 3&4 split whats left.

We ended the heads up 1.2000 Class (what I used to run) And are now doing brackets on Tuesday night. I decided I wanted to change my body I had ordered a ford ranger drag truck from WRP through the track and WRP sent a S-dime, so I got a mustang its a 98 style (same style my brother had). I got it masked I'm going to get some bright red paint tomorrow and lay some on it.





My shitty, horribly, crappy masking job. My dad finally told me to mask the damn thing already when it took me 5 minutes just to get the back window on. This will be my first lexan body paint to don't expect any thing to special.

Racing went really well tonight. First test run of the truck the crown wasn't tight and popped off so I had to put a new one on. second one went good. Chassis seemed to start flexing really bad so I put a brace in, once I did that my times went from a 1.323(fastest with out brace) to a 1.202(fastest with brace) Also the truck was hooking up better I was getting a 356 to the 60(with out brace) and then a 202(with brace).



Thank you,

That was the arguement between the the drag slot car guys and the round-de-rounders. It was like this.

Drag Racer: We like our straight line cars because we can get several races in one day.
Road Racer: We like the endurance, You guys can't handle anything over 3 seconds at a time.
DR: Our cars are more realistic.
RR: Euro-Sport, NASCAR, Indy/F1 are realistic also.
DR: Our bracket cars look like the cars we drive.
RR: Are you sure it's not that you can't handle the turns.

I heard one of these arguement go on for about 15 minutes.

Reminded me of the Valoline commercial years ago with Al Unser, Jr. Joe Amato and another driver I can't remember at this time.

Al goes to Joe saying that he couldn't handle anything more than 5 seconds at a time. The other guy tells Joe to turn and face his acuser. Joe says I'm a drag racer I don't have to turn.
