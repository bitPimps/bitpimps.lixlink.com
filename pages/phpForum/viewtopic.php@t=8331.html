Back in the day I didn't see anyone else running a baja bug body on their Losi XXTs. The one I was running was intended for 1/8th scale buggies. So when I got a Mini T you know I was gonna have to get Losi's baja bug body for the Mini-T. Luckily Bird had one for me. Thanks Bird.




Since the bug body leaves the rear end wide open I got a couple goodies to pretty it up back there. I'll review the goodies in my Mini T log after I've had a chance to test drive it.


Back in the day I had also painted my XXT to match my Stampede. So my Mini-T's color scheme matches my Mini LST. Kinda makes me feel like I got my old cars back, but I like the smaller cars better. Its nice being able to watch TV while I'm racing.
