Thanx Pimps,

When I started I knew I wanted to do a drop top and the first thing that
came to mind is an early 90's Mustang Convertable with this particular
body. The only thing that took a while was a decision on a rear spoiler/wing.
Anything I came up with would take away from the drop
top cover so I decided to just keep the lil stock lip spoiler and it worked
out perfectly. I had to make the tail lights since this thing didn't have any.
Wheels are gold also but hard to see, the sun and the color of the wood
drowns them out. The hood scoop came out great, just like the one's on
the early Rousch machines. Glad you Pimps approve !
