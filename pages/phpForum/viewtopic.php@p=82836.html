Post by crazydave » Tue Nov 01, 2005 5:29 pm

Ok, so Sunday morning I mounted up the alloys. They were a little too tall and wide, to lower the car at all, in order to make it look right, but they looked so much better than the stock ones, I spent half the morning considering it. Then I was watching Car and Driver, and they just happened to show a Classic Car and Driver anniversary show, that featured their picks for the top European sports cars of all time, and James Bond's DB5 was featured. After watching that, I realized I had to lower it, to make the body lines work.

So after I lowered it, I'm really glad I went that route, it looks so much better. Now I just gotta find a die cast with some spoke wheels that are the right size to rob off of it, and I'll be set.



It was little tricky to keep the lights this low. I had to take out part of the top of the window, so the light's PCB sat tucked up on the window. I still need to shave the mounts down for the rear lights, to let the gear spin, but that shouldn't be a problem. I'll take a pic of the inside when I get it shaved all properly.
