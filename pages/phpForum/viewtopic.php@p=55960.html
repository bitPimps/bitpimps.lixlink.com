Post by SuperFly » Wed Sep 22, 2004 7:57 pm

Dual cells are definitely faster, no question. But I think a fetted car with the right motor and gear combo will outperform it. That second cell weighs a lot when you consider the entire weight of the car. At bit scale, that weight makes a big difference on how much speed you can take into the corners and how quickly you can accelerate. Your wheels might be spinning faster with a dual cell, but if your tires can't hook it up, what good is it? Fets give you almost as much torque, and if you gear it with teal or KIT racer gears, you'll get a pretty fast top end.
