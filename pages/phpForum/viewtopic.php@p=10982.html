Post by crazydave » Sun Jun 15, 2003 8:38 am

The Biglots by me finally opened yesterday, so I finally got one of these Thunder Charger cars.

I have got to say, these things do kick some serious butt. Tracks perfectly straight with the adjuster centered. Turns on a dime and gives 9 cents change. The pink endbell clone motor is very good. I would've liked a little more top end, but accelleration is excellent. I can see why these motors would work out well for dual cells. The gears are quiet, and the tires are flat wide, and fairly sticky.

My only complaint is that I don't care for the cheesy bodies, and the chassis is just slightly too long for bit bodies. Cannonball clone bodies fit perfect, but I only have a couple of those that I'm not real fond of.

But for $5 I'm not complaining. I only got one to check 'em out, and because I don't get paid until monday, but on monday going back and buying as many as I can!
