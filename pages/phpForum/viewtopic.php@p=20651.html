We've all seen the new Super Bit Char-Gs, now here's my Super Microsizer...

Porshe 911 GT2 body, mosfet mod, Perfection 4.2 Pro, and pullback rims make this puppy one bad mofo. Bring your Super Bits on. I'm ready.



I didn't have the pullbacks out of the package more than 10 minutes, before I became really irritated with how high the Porshe body sat. So I got the hacking, and lowered that puppy.

While I was mod mode, I jumped on the pullback wheel coversion. I did the less nicer looking Countach rims first, and spray painted them silver. I was disappointed I thought the Countach pullback had Countach rims. (The 5-hole type), but they look pretty nice with this body, now that they're painted.

I love the way the pullback wheels fill out the rear wheel wells on the 911.
...crazy man, crazy...dig it.
