Ok, heres the problem...

The bit sales is going bad - really bad, to be exact no one buys bits anymore in denmark. Thats why i thought it would be cool if some of you guys would come by and post your cars in the Micro-garage on http://www.rcinfo.dk/forum/index.php?ac ... d=sc&cat=5
If you want to do that click on "nyt billede" which, translated, means "submit new picture". From there it should not be too difficult.

Of course you would need to registrer to post pictures, but that is not difficult. Only remember that "brugernavn" means "username".

Before http://www.rcinfo.dk came, the site was called http://www.racetin.dk (ask DarkTari) and he posted some cars once in a while. When he was there the bits was always out of stock - they got that popular. And that forced the owner of http://www.rcinfo.dk to buy more bits... When the new site came, and DT left, the sale just died.

So... This is my prayer...
