Other than the speed is quite slow and it is not 4wd, it is a pretty good micro truck. Here is a spec of my measure ...

width: 51mm

tire width: 10mm
drive train: 2wd
turning diameter: about 1 feet and 2 inch
climb up to: 40 degree
control: IR
suspension: full soft suspension

battery: 3 x LR44 battery for the truck. 2 x LR44 for controller.
