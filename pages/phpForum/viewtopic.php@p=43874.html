What is and is NOT alowed from RS

  • Quote

Post by cj » Fri May 14, 2004 8:10 pm

Only RadioShack XMODS race car chassis, shells, electronics, parts, and upgrades are allowed. No shims are allowed anywhere on the Car. No modifications are allowed to original XMODS chassis, shells, electronics, parts, or upgrades including weight saving measures such as drilling or cutting. All parts must be installed according to the directions provided in the XMODS Owner's Manual or upgrade directions. Power boosters of any kind are not allowed. No tire treatment of any kind is allowed (i.e. traction compound). Only AAA alkaline or AAA rechargeable batteries may be used. Batteries (4 only) must be installed as described in the "Installing Batteries in the Chassis" section of the XMODS Owner's Manual. Additional batteries are not allowed at any time or in any configuration. No batteries will be provided for Cars in the Pre-Season Tournament. Once a Car has gone through inspection, batteries may not be added or switched for any reason prior to the ensuing Round.
