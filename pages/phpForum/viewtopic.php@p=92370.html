Post by crazydave » Fri Apr 28, 2006 8:10 pm

The DB5 James Bond car was one of those that I thought RS did a really good job on, just needed to lower it on the chassis, and it looked pretty cool. The only change I wanted to make was find some more realistic spoke wheels.



Then sat4life posted a pic a of a DB5 racer, and I loved it. I didn't want to step on Sat's toes too much and do an exact copy, but I had to do something influenced by it. When I removed the bumpers the body looked so much better, and sportier. I decided to just give it a white stripe and make it kind of like a track car that gets driven on the street.





Thanks for the inspiration sat... I prefer this version much better.
