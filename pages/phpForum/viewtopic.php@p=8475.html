Post by Buzzbait » Mon May 19, 2003 6:49 am

My chrome RX8 body was quickly replaced by a muscle car body last week. I had full intentions of using the Challenger body, but that Camaro is shaped sooooooo sexy. The Camaro is definitely my fave.

On a side note, my hybrid turbo Bit/Zap bit the big one Friday night. I don't know what happened. I was driving around my Camaro and the thing just died. No smoke. No fire. No nothing. The PC board must have given up the ghost. I scavenged another MSPro for its pcb, and everything is sweet now.

I did do things a little differently with the batteries when I swapped the pcb. I used those little files that Radio Shack sells to scar the tips of the batteries. They worked perfectly for getting the solder to stick. Much better than sandpaper. It would take a jackhammer to remove my battery connections now. I also took some solid core wire and hammered it flat to make the connections between the batteries. After hammering the wire and soldering it in place, I used my wife’s super-duper emery board to sand down the soldered connections, so they were nice and thin and flat. That emery board rocks for making the solder connections nice and small. No battery fit problems whatsoever.

I did remember to draw a wiring diagram, for connecting an MS Pro pcb to a Zip Zap chassis, this time around. I’ll post up a picture of the diagram tonight.
