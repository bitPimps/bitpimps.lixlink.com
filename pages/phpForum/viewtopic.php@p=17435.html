noxorc wrote:cool. good job...

two questions..

where do you get the 6v from?
The 6V is the circuit voltage that was given with the schematic. It doesn't need to be this. I will be running it off 5V using a DCDC Converter. This will run off the standard 1.2V battery that's in the car. I will be using a very low power version of these IC's, the TLC555 and CD4017 chips which are both CMOS (meaning ultra-low power drain). In fact, the LEDs will drain more current than the IC's.
noxorc wrote:how do I interpret the black arrow going in the middle of the 500k resistor?
As Type said the 500k resistor is a potentiometer (or variable resistor) and the black arrow corresponds to the where you have turned the shaft and what resistance is there. In the video posted earlier you can see as I turn the pot (top middle of the screen) the LEDs cycle a lot faster. I'm toying with the idea of leaving this in the final circuit or doing away with it and putting a fixed resistor in it's place. I think variable speed would be cool though. It's a matter of space.

Also is the problem of turning it on & off. I hate having huge switches sticking out of my bit's but! I might just leave it on all the time........

This is a "spare time" project whilst I'm waiting for some more parts to arrive for my H-Bridge project.
