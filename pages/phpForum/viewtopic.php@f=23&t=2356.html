The Projects.

I start out saying I HATE the Toyota Celica body. I have a few of them and I just don't like them. The First project is called Tricks. It's based on a Toyota Celica Body. I don't know if I'm going to continue this project or not, it depends on the feedback I get here.

Project: Tricks


The Second project started when my Nakamichi Racer was dismantled for the wheels for the GT-W project. I broke more on the chassis and I decided not to rebuild it at this time. I had a Truck body with a cap on it that was OK but I felt it needed something more. I removed the Cap and custom made the rear of the truck's cab and added a bed cover. I lowered the rear with a hidden mount in the body and I shaved the front mounting screw to lower the front. I took a Precious chassis I had laying around and put a hole in the front tab to mount the body with the original screw mount. I'm still debating on the color, I've been having images of Candy Apple in my head.

Project: Happy Hauler



Third the Question. I bought this car on eBay (it was advertised with a 4.2 Pro motor, the motor is fast so it might be one) for $.99 plus shipping. It's on MHz (weird) and I would like to know what brand it is. If somebody says it's a Mitsubishi I'll have to send Gandalf over to knock some sense into them. Also what color endbell does the 4.2 Pro have (the one in it is blue like the Perfection 3.8's).
