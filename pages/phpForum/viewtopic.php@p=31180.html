  • Quote

Post by crazydave » Fri Oct 24, 2003 9:23 pm

I just played with My mustang SE for the first time. I am impressed. It handled great, and had about as much torque as my FET'ed SE that still needs the bugs worked out.

What really got me was that I got a 10 minute run from a single charge, and that was almost non stop running with the headlights.

I didn't mind the jitter in the steering when it handled good. This is much better than the RX-7 I bought.

I was in shock so I had to run a stock Zip Zap 350Z to compare, and it did have better off the line punch, but they were much more closely matched. I then tried my FETed SE and it was about the same as the Mustang, but it only ran about 3-4 minutes on a single charge.

I'm going to go stick some FETs in this right now, to see how it responds. I'm also gonna go over the PCB to see if I can't spot the change from the first one
