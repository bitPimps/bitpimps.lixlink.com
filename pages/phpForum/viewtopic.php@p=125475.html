Post by frizzen » Fri Jan 01, 2010 3:54 pm

I *REALLY* like the looks of the ZZ bodies on this chassis! The bigfoot makes me think of the Tamiya Highlift F-350.

All I'd done to my yellow truck body like you have was:
Relocate rollbar (drill 4 holes, shave the raised areas in bed where it used to mount, then I made bed black to hide the old holes),
Open wheelwells (knife for rough cut, dremel large sanding drum for getting it round),
Color front end (headlights silver or white, throw a little black or orange at the outboard recesses in bumper, black where the winch would live - Sharpies work well enough if you don't have paint) .

It's subtle stuff, which got me to that point, but you can see how fast it changed the truck because I didn't touch the stock yellow and silver paint, yet you think my paint looks better. Oh and there was a spare tire, but you don't really need one.

Now, on the wagon I'd pull off the front end, do a little file work so that it can be tucked farther back into the body. You trim the support lugs off the body pins, and contour the header panel behind the headlights to match the fender. This lets you bring it in about 0.080" before you have to start worrying too much about trimming too far. (I just did one while making the post so I'd remember what to tell you)
