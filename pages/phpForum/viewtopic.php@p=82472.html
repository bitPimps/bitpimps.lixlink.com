bought some interesting new stuff for my mr02 recently and thought i'd share.

first up is the f.r.p.t. plate. it's basically a tough plastic hplate with interchangeable springs (roll bars) that mount in the hplate to tune the stiffness.
in use it's excellent, i got the hard one with the hard spring installed. gives just the right amount of up/down spring and is nice an firm in cornering.
this should replace every rear spring you'll ever see.

http://www.rcmart.com/catalog/default.p ... 8173bdf22c


next up is rims. i've always wanted dish rims for my z but didn't want to shell out for those topcads. and you never know what the offsets are from the pics.
but atomic got a full range of dish rims in black or white, front or rear (narrow or wide) and in every offset you could want.
they're much thicker than any other plastic rim i've seen, really solid. especially where it counts, around the inside bb recess. and they spin really true.
i think they look cool too!

http://www.rcmart.com/catalog/default.p ... _21_69_153
