Is it the wheels wobblin or the whole axel?
I've had some wobbly axels on bits but never a rear wheel wobblin on the axel. You could take one of those plastic round coffee stirers and put a slit in it. Then you shove that up into the axel cavity in the chassis. It should tighten up the axel. there's also the zip tie method and the hogjowlz jbweld method but I'll wait till I fully understand your problem.

i've had wobbly wheels on clones too. and wobbly spur gears. sometimes they're even off centre
(wobbly = not spinning true)

you'll most likely have to pull the wheels off the axle, redrill them and remount them.
but if you're going to that much trouble you may as well use some custom rims, lego wheels, die cast wheels, slot car wheels, etc.
save the axles with dodgy wheels for a wheel mod
