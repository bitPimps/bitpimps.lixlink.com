Here's a new driver I just finished...

- Aluminum-Tubing stretched Thunder Charger chassis
- custom Import Tuner hubcaps
- mosfets
- mystery 3.8 motor
- teal gears
- spring suspension

This body is a bitch to lower, with the full-size wheels on it... the way the back end
tapers under makes it hard to go any further without major modification. Even though
I wanted it to be lower, I actually dig this ride height... this car drives just like it should.
The extra ground clearance makes it able to run over a fair amount of shit, just like
the Bandit, yo. I think these wheels really make this car look a shitload better than it
does with the stock, gold painted Cragar hubcaps. They don't look just like the stock
T/A wheels, but they seem to look right on there. This one's a blast to drive.
