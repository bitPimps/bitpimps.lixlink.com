So I went to a Target store tonight for some Nintendo games, and a set of Radline oily shocks for a project...

Hey, what's that? It looks Micro. A NICELY scaled Toyota FJ Cruiser, a Hummer 2, they look damn good for what they are, but I'm not really feeling it. Wait, what is THAT in the back??? VW Toureg Dakar with Redbull livery? Mine!

Maisto "Custom Shop RC" 1/64 scale. On surface, the Chassis looks magnet-steer ZZ clone, body mounts ZZ style, integrated motor clip into PCB cover,

Tx is weird. Normal rocker switches for Fwd/Rev, steering is a wheel on the right side. Takes 2 AA batteries, so probably single cell car.

$11, this dude is SCALE! Ok, it's got the Big&Little tires, but if you look past that, you could mistake it for a nice diecast car.

Post by frizzen » Sat Mar 07, 2009 2:54 am

The chassis is interesting. I figured it'd be more American or Japanese, but the French didn't let me down on designing something weird.

Magnet steer, 1 coil horizontal over 2 magnets. Centering spring and all the normal stuff.

Rear axle fully captive. Looks like standard Bit/Zip gearing 7T pinion 7T spur.

Front body mount removable, it rests over a screwpost, fully captive between chassis and steering top plate. (We can change front overhang easily and non-permanent!)

No front suspension, but with a couple trims, easy enough to add.

Post by frizzen » Wed Mar 18, 2009 12:16 am

They're Ok.

Tires are HARD. Really. Hard. Think ZZ sized drift rings with an agressive offroad tread. I threw a set of Stock ZZ tires on the back, Bit tires up front. Much better! Though it looks more Mall Crawler than Dakar now...

With RX antenna inside body, it's a bit glitchy, but that also might just be low batteries in TX. Probably going to try guitar string cut to wavelength under body before I resort to drilling body.

Ground clearance limited. Not even a swaybar for suspension. Should be able to modify in about 1/8" travel if you can find springs. But, that'd probably kill the little steering you DO have...

Speed is ok for a single cell.
Steering is only a single coil above a single magnet. I would say it's disappointing, but that would be harsh against things that disappoint me. Best case, another magnet and another cell of voltage would be able to improve things.

I HATE THE TX!!! After 30 sec, the novelty wears off and you want buttons! Luckily, a ZZ TX is exactly the same signal pattern.
But the charge base is weird, so you're kinda stuck. The charge base is fiddly.

I like the body, it's pretty.
