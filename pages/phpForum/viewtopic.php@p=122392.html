While I'm not a big fan of Duratrax, the Firehammer seems like a decent route to go for a large scale monster. Mostly because there are not as many places that sell the large scale, and besides always being able to count on Tower Hobbies for support, there seems to be a few other places that don't normally specialize in large scale that also stock parts for these cars.

If you get a good deal used, I would go for it, but if I was buying a large scale car new, I would save my money for a Baja 5B. That's where the support is at right now.
