Last night I decided to look around the forums, looked at Crazy Dave's ride for a while, nice ride, but im determined to beat it... came up with something to make 'sane' 'insane'...

Features:
_Rear ball bearings, possible front ball bearings.*
_Full spring suspention with inverted rear swing arm.*
_4mm ground clearance rear, same proposed for the front.*
_Dual zzse batteries running 4.8V.*
_Modified pcb with proposed 'true' propo logic.*
_Lower profile steering assembly.*
_Soldered hardened steel space frame.*

thanks guys, i was gonna do a chassis out of like resistor leads, but i remembered that guitar strings are hardened and you can solder onto em. i did "the drop test" and dam, this thing is tough!!! my brother just gave me his broken strings except for the one i wanted most ... anyways the spring assembly is almost done, i made the spring itself out of the thinnest string, its a scissor type spring mounted on the very back of the car. i tested it, it holds a "standard" 1/10 scale RC servo motor, while keeping the suspension soft. after mounting the first spring i think i mealted the hinge a bit, so thats gonna be needing replacement... im trying to keep it so disassembly is easy, so i hope i can change the hinge easy.

you can read up on some odd things i was working on at tinyRC.
