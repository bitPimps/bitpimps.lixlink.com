Hi all , this is my 1/43 Superslicks project , I received it with a so cal "charging problem" but had alot more than that. So I gutted the little bugger and started thinking.

I then installed a ZZMT pcb , as the original one was pretty much stuffed to some extent.


Now, this thing is quickly becoming one of my favorite cars. With the 3cell pack i'm running, it's insanely fast, and is great on carpet. Touch a hardwood floor and it'll just spin right out, same with linoleom. Carpet for now then, but I plan to run this at a small carpark not too far from my house, super smooth tarmac and no cars......

Currently the only mods are :

Iwaver antenna
ZZMT 49MHZ pcb board /TX


I have a little more planned :

Single cell Lipo (maybe kokam 145mah)
Better tires (cant even drive on hardwood with a 3cell atm)
Upgrade motor (still on the stock one)

I may have to switch back to the stock motor , or at least gear down some , because there's alot of power even with a 3 cell on the stock motor , add a lipo and an upgrade motor , and I suspect it will almost pull wheelies from a standstill... :blink:

Here's some pictures of my new favorite toy B)




Size comparison to a ZZSE


Chassis with the ZZMT board inside (Sit's nice and low , and PLENTY of room underneath for lipo.

You can see the rest of the picturesHere.



My next dilemma is turning , it likes to roll alot when turning. Without propo throttle , I cant slow down enough. I have 2 options here.

1. Is to wait untill I get ahold of the lipo cell , and hope the weight loss will even it out a bit.

2. Is to fab up some suspension , as you might know they have NONE , that's right , NO SUSPENSION , So i'm thinking of a rear H plate and some extended knuckles upfront to give me bit like steering.....

What do you think guys ?
