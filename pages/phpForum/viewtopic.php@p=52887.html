This is my Chevelle. I bought the ZZ Euro body set with the intention to do something with the Mercedes body, but I liked how the TT sat on the SE. I figure I drive my SE more like a road car than a muscle car, so I decided to do a touring class styled TT racer.

I lowered the front and rear tabs. Did some body kitting. Gave it a racey paint job, added some decals, and added some pen tip exhaust. It's got the white wheels from the Zip Zap wheelset, with the racey looking sport tires.





...and it's still got the lights.
