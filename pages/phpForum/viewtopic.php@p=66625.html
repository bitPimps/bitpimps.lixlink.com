Hue...you hit the nail on the head about Mr. Shelby. I like my late 60's/early 70's Camaros, Firebirds, and GTO's, but there is something special about Shelby Mustangs....and Cobras and Daytonas for that matter.

I know you guys will always love the OG bit chassis....they started this whole thing, how could you not. I really like the simplicity of them and from a modding standpoint, its a lot easier to make a shell look nice on an OG bit shell, then a ZZSE....for reasons we have already gone into in other threads.

But....if you haven't picked up a ZZSE lately, do yourself a favor and pick one up. My last two....the Mach 1 and the Shelby, have been great from a performance standpoint. The steering has been greatly improved and it is just a hell of a lot of fun having that much steering control in a 1:64 scale micro. The charge lasts a hell of a long time too.
