This was my first Zip Zap. I hacked it up many moons ago trying to lower it. I decided to hack it a bit more to use as my "performance" body with my alloys. I call it that because it's been trimmed so the front wheels don't rub.

I was attempting some of those extreme flames, but they weren't coming out right, so I decided to try another traditional hot rod theme. This was my first try at scallops, and I think they came out really good.

@Superfly, flames and scallops are a different hard. Flames are more of a creative hard, because there's so many styles to emulate, and they need to flow with the body lines to look good, but scallops were harder to lay out, because everything had to be measured out. It's just more time consuming, but it's not like I had to think and plan as much, you know?
