I did something today while being kinda bored at tha store.

I bought a 1/64 Modifiers Escalade for the purpose of a dub job.

The hubs are 10 times easier to work with than the 1/43 scale ones which the holes are to big and a pain in tha hiz-ass, whereas the 1/54 ones have holes that have to be drilled to fit....a blessing from the Pimp God I assume.... Plus obviously, other truck rims will fit from the series too, but I assume from the small size of the car rims, they would be too small.
To top it off, bit front tires fit perfectly on the rims!!!

Bottom line, I pulled off my first non zip/bit wheel job and it was fun. It looks sharp as I won't post pix as everyone has seen the mod a ba-zillion times over. I got plenty room to slam the body down....ready for some racing action...

I don't get into those damn "My First....blazeh, blazeh" type posts but I just felt like I accomplished something and wanted to share with my pimps.
