Actually thanks for digging this up. The old comments helped give me encouragement for the current moderator contest.

Project: Prostocker is still allive and presumed kicking. I haven't driven it in over a year now.

Since this I have cracked the chassis in half, after rebuilding it, I repainted it charcoal. Lowered the body at the same time. I found some blue wheels for the wheelie bar, and it has a matching blue CAT5 antenna. The front tab is half broken off, and the thin strip under the tab on the body has broken out. I just glued it back in and didn't bother to touch up the paint, just because I thought Hog would appreciate that. Seeing a few cars with larger motors that may have been potentially as fast as P:PS, I went with a larger Superslicks motor, and the fastest red KITT Racer gears. It actually seems kinda sluggish now compared to before. Don't know if its the gears or the motor. Did those at the same time. Bad idea, but I'm leaving it as is for now because it might turn out to be faster over a 21 foot scale quarter mile.

A sequal has been in the making for well over a year. I discovered it was a fluke that the original worked so well, and I had to to scrap my first try. Right now I'm nearing completion of Project: Prostocker 2.01. I'm building this car with a Thunder Charger motor like the original, and Blue KITT Racer gears. They'e like 4 something:1. I'm building the second car for Hue, and hopefully this Spring I'll be able to fly out to drag with him. We'll see what pans out, big motor/high gear ratio, or small motor/low gear ratio.
