Micro Zoomers Pajero Rally Car

  • Quote

Post by sidewinder » Sun Mar 01, 2009 2:12 am

I was on ebay selling an old Cox Dragster I had, I decided not to sign in and see how easy it was to find my listing...because I get paranoid and figure the search function give priority to your own listings to fool you into thinking a lot of people can find your junk. Anyway, I found it, the search "Cox, Dragster, Racer" pulled up a bunch on non Cox related stuff. Right below my Dragster was a Micro Zoomer Mitsubishi Pajero rally car, 1/64 scale, it was a buy it now and the price was cheap...so I couldn't resist.

Well, it arrived today. It uses button cells, and has a cheesy remote that sort of looks like a cell phone. I expected it to be total crap...I was wrong. This is one of the better built clones I have seen, and I think it may be the fastest out of the box bit/zip/clone I have seen. It is nice and light and actually turns with a slightly tail out attitude, not really drifting, but as close as I've seen a bit get. There was only one other listed or I'd give you the link, but I'm about to go snag it. I did contact the seller and I asked if he would be getting more, he said he was pretty sure he would, he had ten and all but that last one sold withing two weeks...which I'm guessing is pretty quick for a micro. If he gets more and re-lists them I'll give everyone here a heads-up.

I don't have a video camera right now, but I took a bunch of pictures.

i got one of those at our local kmart for $10 aud.

mine had a slightly deformed front tyre and the front tyres hit the chassis at full lock.

the wheels are the same size as microsizer/bcg fronts so i swapped the tyres out for hard fronts and soft rears. i chose this combo as the speed is nuts out of the box, it wants to throw itself into donuts with full grip all 'round.

to solve the tyres hitting the chassis prob i just chopped a couple of bits off the chassis to give more clearance.
pajero.JPG
the "cell phone" tx was a pain is the arse to use so i whipped out a microsizers 27mhz tx. forward/reverse was reversed so i just swapped the motor wires.

i also tried to tame it using a standard ms 1/3 aaa cell but it appears that there's not enough voltage to get it going so we'll have to make do with the stock blistering speed!
