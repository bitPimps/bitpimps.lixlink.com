they're in!

Not quite what I expected - the images I had put up are quite different from the guts of these, which leads me to believe that these are a knock off of the graupner rather than a direct factory supply of the real thing.

The electronics are pretty rubbish, but the size of the combined ESC/RX will be good for a 1/24 build. I'm in the process of gutting mine over to stock micro T electrics - motor and servo are the same size. Stock Gearing is pretty high, and motor gets pretty hot running a 2S lipo. I get the feeling I might cook the stock board on lipo.

The strangest thing is that these are completely overbuilt in some areas and a bit fragile looking in others; ie, bearings everywhere but plastic CVD's. I'm seeing if the factory can supply some metal CVD's, and if so I'll send them out free of charge to everyone who's picked one up.
