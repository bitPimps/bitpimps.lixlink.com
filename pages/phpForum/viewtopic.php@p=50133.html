SCRATCH BUILT all the way

you actually cant really see the grill, but i took the xzcto knife and some clay, i flattened out the clay and then i rolled the dimpled area of the xacto knife into the clay and then i took some wood glue and spread it unto the clay and when it dried i pealed it off and cut to shape then clued it onto the front, finished texture is a mesh grill.
