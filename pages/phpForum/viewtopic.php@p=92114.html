Post by stampede_dude » Mon Apr 24, 2006 6:01 am

I dont want to piss anyone off but the stinger motor is shit if you don't put the arm in a rebuildable can. All of mine kicked the bucket after about 15 runs without a reason why. If you have a rebuildable can to put the armature into though it is a pretty nice motor.

A stock motor is fast for 27 turn, but they have extremely advanced timing to pull the most out of a motor sacraficing the temperature of the motor. They don't have alot of torque, and since drifting puts stress to a motor and requires alot of torque, I would suggest a different motor. I have had good experiences with the Speed Gems series of motors.

http://www2.towerhobbies.com/cgi-bin/wt ... LXCUP9&P=7

If that motor has problems or gets hot, you will need to gear it down some. That is one of the best motors, IMO, if geared correctly. The ESC Scorpion posted would be fine, but novak makes some good escs aswell. I don't really know what you are looking for in an ESC so i'll let someone else help you. The only advice on that I can really give you is to buy a used one. ESCs almost never wear out if they aren't stressed.
