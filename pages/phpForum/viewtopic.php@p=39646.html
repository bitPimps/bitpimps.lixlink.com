dude, in the first (good quality) pic of the car you can see all four batts wired up. the front battery has the +VE positive terminal that goes to the pcb. the back/last battery has the -VE negative terminal that goes to the PCB.

On these two terminals (front and back) I've also soldered on a pice of wire to allow me to "clip on" to the wire using little clips. These are known as alligator clips because of their "teeth" like design....

These are the only two terminals you can hookup the charger to. Between the +VE and -VE terminals the batteries are all connected in series with each other, this adds up to a total voltage of 4.8V being available (4*1.2) for the car.
