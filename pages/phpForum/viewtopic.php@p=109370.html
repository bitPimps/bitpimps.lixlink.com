Here is my build up thread.

I got my Micro-t on December 18th 2006, I ran the car for a while then broke a arm mount and shock. I let it sit for a couple months while I kind of drifted out of RC for a while. this past month I have gotten to parts to fix it, as well as slowly turning it into a full aluminum Micro-t.


14, May 2006.

I get home from work and see a padde envolope sitting on the counter, open it up and see that all my parts have arrived. The package consisted of.
1x GPM blue rear camber link set
1x GPM blue front camber link set
1x GPM silver skid plate
1x BRP Ball Bearing set

From the shop I work at I had brought home a screw set, shock set, and a suspension mount and bumper set.

I installed all the parts and charged it up, first run was a success, bearing made quite the difference, I don't know why I didn't fix it the minute it broke.
