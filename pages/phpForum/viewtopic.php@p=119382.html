Our friend crazydave came up with a great idea.
Let's make some forum signatures we can use on sites to direct people to our own albums.
Here's a quick one I did. Feel free to use this or any other signatures that may be created.




How do I use it?
Be sure to replace "cat=XXXXX" with your own album number. You can find this by clicking on your album.

BB Code:
Here is some BB Code for you to use:

Code: Select all

[url=http://bitpimps.lixlink.com/pages/phpGallery/index.php?cat=XXXXX][img]http://bitpimps.lixlink.com/pages/phpGallery/albums/userpics/10001/bpsig01.jpg[/img][/url]
HTML Code:
Here is some HTML Code for you to use:

Code: Select all

<a href="http://bitpimps.lixlink.com/pages/phpGallery/index.php?cat=XXXXX"><img src="http://bitpimps.lixlink.com/pages/phpGallery/albums/userpics/10001/bpsig01.jpg" border="0" /></a>

Can I make my own?
Of course!


I hope you all enjoy.
Hopefully there will be plenty more signatures to choose from.
If you have any questions, post them here.
