Post by crazydave » Mon Jan 31, 2005 10:31 am

For me the hobby never hit home. Limited places carried Microsizers, and most of them still have a few left. I never saw any NSXes or any or new releases this year. Just a few left over from last Christmas. I'd snag them, but they're full price, and there's still clearance deals on the net.

I don't know about else where, but I really think the biggest problem is that first wave, when everything was sold out before Christmas, Lots of cheap dome clones in the mall that year, and from my experience, most were crap. Then there was a batch of Microsizer Pro and Sports that hit that toy stores with steering knuckles of uneven lengths, causing the steering to be too tight in one direction,and too wide in the other. I know this was a widespread problem, because I've FET modded more than a half dozen of these cars with the same problem for people.

I really think the reason why Zip Zaps have won out over their smaller bit counterparts, in America, is because a lot of people, and kids in particular, just experienced another cheap toy, and blew them off.

It's like we know what cars to buy for which parts, because we've been around the block with these cars, but Joe Blow is just gonna walk into a toy store, have a 50/50 chance of picking up a piece of crap, and being done with it.
...crazy man, crazy...dig it.
