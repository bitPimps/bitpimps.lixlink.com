Post by crazydave » Tue Jul 27, 2004 11:21 am

These cells are 40mah, there's a little less capacity, so shorter charge times, but I'll charge a stock ZZ 100mah cell for 6-7 minutes, for a 45 minute runtime, so I charge these about 3 minutes for a 15 minute runtime. (The runtime is also shortened I'm because using 2 SE batteries pushing more voltage)

You can charge dual cells with a 3v source, but that will take longer. I prefer to charge dual cells with 4.5v. Not sure the mA, because I'm still charging with batteries.

Or in other words, a Motorworks, or NKOK charger will work for ya.
