Since there's no history to go off, and I can't see it first hand, it would be hard to say, but my guess is the battery is probably dead in the MAT car, the Tomy cars came with NiCds as opposed to NiMH. The NiCds don't seem to sit well, and these cars have been sitting on the shelves for... crap, what like up to 7 years by now.
So try a battery from a clone.

I'm assuming the New Beetle is a Bit Char-G, not a Microsizer, since you got the MAT car? If that's teh case, later models had "crip caps", either in the car's PCB, or the controller. There was an article at quest for madness, or at least that's what I remember it being called, but can't seem to find it, maybe someone else will know what I'm talking about and be able to find it. Meanwhile you could try adding a telescoping antenna and/or an extra battery to the controller, and a longer antenna to the car.

Hope that helps.
