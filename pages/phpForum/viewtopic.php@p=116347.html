Sweet. Nice to see an MT build again. Like the tube work. Soldered paper clips?

As far as weighting the front wheels I used small strips of lead from the tackle shop and wrapped them inside the wheel, not inside the tire if that makes sense. Put in as much as you can, as long as your steering still works you should be good. Some in the rear may help too but I ran mine in the front only.
