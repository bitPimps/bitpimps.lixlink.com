ive been doing it my whole life.. but this is the first time im working on this rally. i usually work on the Lake Superior Pro Rally as a Stage Captian.... That rally has the best photo opps for jumping cars.. the stage is brockway mountain. its a paved section thats three miles long that goes up the ridge of a mtn..

here is a pic of a subie coming in for a landing... that is the finish of the stage.. at the top of the hill. its a flying finish, so the cars can be going in excess of 100+mph and flying over 150 feet befor touchdown..
unfortnately only cars with some kick ass suspension will gun it at the finish for the better time.. but other rookies will do the same with their rally neons and be launched.. controlling the landing is the only problem.. theres a clif off to the left of the landing and a herd of people to the right.. that photo was taken the last year they let people stand on the side of the road..



edit: heres a evo jumping.. didnt get as much height...



subaru spends a lot of money taking photos on that rally because there are tons of great photo spots like that one.. many rallys have simmilar but none have a jump like that one that im aware of...
