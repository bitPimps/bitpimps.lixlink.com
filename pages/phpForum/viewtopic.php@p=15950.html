there are several methods you can use to get the rims off the knuckles.

you can use a small finishing nail to try and push the pin out from the backside of the knuckle.
you can use a soldering iron on the tip of the pin to heat it up just a tad and then pull it out with pliers.
you can use pliers and try to muscle the pin out.

there is a technique to the muscling them out.
payaso bout shiat when he saw how easily I pulled them out - without a bend.
takes 2 pairs of pliers, 1 normal pair and 1 needle nose.
lately I've been trying the soldering iron method - I'd suggest you not pull on the rim to get the pin out or you're going to melt and deform your rim, pull from the head of the pin with needle nose pliers.

haven't tried the screwdriver thing because I can't seem to think of a way to pry on the pin without completely marring up the rim with the screwdriver.
