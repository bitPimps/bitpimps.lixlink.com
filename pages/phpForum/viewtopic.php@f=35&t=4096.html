Post by betty.k » Wed Jun 30, 2004 6:04 pm

sorry to confuse, i meant that that perticular tank just drives around. some of them have infra red "cannons" in them enabling 2 or more to have a shoot em up contest, like the digi q. they will fire by flashing an ir led and an ir sensor on top registers a "hit" by stopping for 10 seconds or spinning around. the digi q's will even recoil when the cannon is fired and simulate damage. some are completely ir controlled, some are radio controlled with ir cannons, and some are just rc tanks with no battle game capability.

so if you just want a little tank to drive around then the one i posted above is for you. but if you want ir battle games with 2 or more people you'll have to buy a digi q (expensive) or another sort (cheaper).
