cool man, I worked for Kenworth Australia for 4 years. mad trucks, premo price though....what rig you ride?

also, re the motor stuff. go brushless as soon as possible if you can. the upkeep on brushed motors is a pain in he arse. I went brushless from day one with my half 8 and have been very happ with the zezo maintenance too.

I have an AON4900 from aonelectric.com it's not as fast as the mamba motors but it has excellent torque, specially in the half 8 which is front and rear gear diff with a centre diff as well, so the friction is healthy in this drivetrain.

The problem with brushless is that it has this association as a crazy fast motors that make all sorts of 1:18 chassis available go nuts. or that without the right batt/esc progaming it will just cog (or dump) and perform like shit...

the truth is you don't need crazy kV ratings, just a solid, torque motor that will run cool on a 6-7 cell nimh or lipoly (best) supply. the aon I use is only 4900, mamba's have higher numbers like 6800 and 8000. It means the amount fo RPM's per volt.

so at 7.2V (6 cell nimh) a 8000kV motor would be 57.6K RPM. that's afst even in brushless terms so a HIGH KV motor will usually run hot (orhotter) than a lower kV motor.

so just gear the bugger up a bit. I'm getting a ground speed of 50-60kpH running the AON4900(15t pinion), with 11.1V lipoly and the mamba-25 esc, . That's crazy shit right, lol, but still, even at that (extreme) voltage the motor doesn't get MEGA hot, healthy hot, but not I'm gonna fry shit hot....if you get mah drift....

a good, solid torque, (relatively) low kV motor will always be a safer longterm choice, gear it up and with that minit you should get nutter speeds... on top of this there is the (proverbial) ZERO maintenance..

I dunno, sorry for the rant.....but if I was to ever get into 1:10 (final frontier, lol..) I would go brushless from the start...
