That with the wide rear (and front if I can get the wide tires on the front too with body mod) That with the 38k (I think) motor will be super fast I hope. I hope it doesn't fry anything trying to pull that much juice.

The ZZMT is going to be lipo'd, FET'd and I believe I will attempt to take a few turns off (maybe 5-10) of the orange motor to take some of that torque away. I'm thinking I can mount a 3.7v powerizer plug into the ZZMT no problem.

The second ZZ I bought will be FET'd (first one in case I suck) and the ZZMT dual cell pack will be moved to it because that is going to be give to a kid who works for me.
