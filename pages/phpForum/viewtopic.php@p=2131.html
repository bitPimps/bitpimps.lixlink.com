Post by Namuna » Sun Mar 16, 2003 2:05 pm

Ahhhh the Overland.

First, my overall impression is that I LIKE my new Overland. It's fun, it looks great and it's a lot more 'forgiving' of what surface you run it on, because of the higher ride-height (low to medium pile rug hasn't been a problem).

A couple of things I DON'T like:
- Mounting the body to the chassis! What a PAIN IN THE ASS with the Overland bodies! I'm ALWAYS afraid I'm going to pull the stupid thing in HALF when mounting.

- The 'Electronic Brake' (In my opinion) is less feature, more annoyance. When I'm going forward and jam my finger in reverse, I want my car to follow suit. But instead, going in immediate reverse only stops the car...To actually go in reverse you have to release the trigger to neutral and THEN press in reverse again.

- I had thought the Overland was 4x4, it's NOT. So I was a little disappointed in that.

There ya have it.
