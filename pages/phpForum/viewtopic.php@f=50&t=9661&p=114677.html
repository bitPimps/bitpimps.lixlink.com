The jittery steering on the SE was a comon problem. I think if you search the forums under jittery there was a fix...I don't have time to look for it right now but if you do a search you should find it.

The tighter steering one way can be a bunch of things. The first thing I'd look at is the steering arm. Is it turning the wheels the same amount to each side. If not one of your tires could be rubbing. If it does look like they are turning the same amount check and see if the wheels both turn easily...they can pck up hair and carpet fiber. Also, this didn't happen much (I don't think it did anyway) but on occasion the cars would come new with warped wheels... to check spin the wheel and wacth to see that it doesnt wobble back and forth. If you have a bent wheel your in luck. There are all sorts of cool wheels from diecast (Jada makes some cool ones) you can change them easily...check out the tutorials. Check these things first, if its not one of these maybe somebody here an help more.

First off, we have to be clear, is this 1st generation magnetic steering, or motorized steering of later ZZs. With the magnetic steering, I've found some steering coil are slightly wider than others, or the magnet shifts. In either case you can fix it by moving the magnet over, and shaving teh excess palstic off the other side.

If you're referring to the motorized steering, you can fix that by taking the flat gear out, turn the car on to center the steering, then slip the flat gera back in, as centered as possible.

As for you battery issue, assuming you have fresh batteries in your controller, then the battery in the car must be going bad. These are fair inexpensive to replace. Do a Google search for "1/4 AAA", unless you have a SE, Cars, or Transformer ZZ, then those batteries need to be special ordered from RS.
