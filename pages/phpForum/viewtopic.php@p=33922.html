Post by crazydave » Sun Dec 14, 2003 9:37 pm

Thanks guys.

So you want me to spill how I come with 'em?

Not much to spill. I was trying to do some extreme flame kinda thing, but it just wasn't flowin' today, and not much room to work with on the bit body, I do that kind of stuff better on Zip Zaps, so I just did my basic flames. I added the racing stripe to fill space since I couldn't figure out how to make the flames work with the vents on the hood. I picked the colors, with the intention of keeping it cheap by using my scruggin's. The red came on the body. The white is actually primer, I was surprised how well my fade came out, because that stuff doesn't lay nearly as smooth as laquer. Then I sputtered out my last drops of charcoal, to barely eeek out 2 coats.

I got really nervous it wouldn't come out, but when I peeled all that masking tape off, well, you've seen...
