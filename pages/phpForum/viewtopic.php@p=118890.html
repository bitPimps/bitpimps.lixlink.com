Post by payaso » Tue Sep 02, 2008 7:24 pm

You'll have way more fun with a nitro and the new electrics out there are going to cost you a fortune.

Betwee a BL motor and ESC and RX and lipos, you'll be out a grand. Yep, a grand.....

To make BL as much fun as nitro, you'll need a couple batts. Those alone will run you between $400-$800

A 4s 8000mah lipo is around $400 for one. You'd need at least 2 to keep from waiting on batts to charge. There are cheaper batts, but you get what you pay for.


Not that nitro is cheap, (especially at $35 a gallon) but it's way cheaper than BL for monster trucks.

Ok now I can elaborate on this. I recently reviewed the Exceed Techtonic, it's a 1/8th monster that sells at NitroRCX.com for $270. The radio gear is crap, but the truck itself is solid and worth every penny. The E-revo was reviewed in the same issue so that gave me a good chance to make a direct comparison.


The E-Revo runs $450, and was tested with a pair of MaxAmps Lipos, which would run just over a hundred a piece, and you'd need the appropriate charger. Altogether about a $350 power package, so you're looking at a grand total of around $800, and that setup tops out at 27mph

The Techtonic on the other hand is $279. A glow starter and a quart of fuel will run you about $25, so you're up and running for just over $300, and it tops out at just over 35mph.... seems like a no-brainer to me.
