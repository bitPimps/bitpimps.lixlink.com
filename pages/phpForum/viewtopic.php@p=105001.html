I am supprised no one has worked on this body yet then I started workin on it and now know why!

this is not any easy project I am taking the tow mater body and making a 55 chevy Truck out of it. It will be hot rod style trucking.

The hood you see in the pictures took 2 days to build its in 3 parts. I cut the hood off the black car to use and had to cut it in several pieces. I then added styrene as a base then put the hood over the top of it, lastly the putty.

the back fenders are coming off the Skyline in the picture. I am also going to chop the top as the top I think is to tall a chop will give it a tougher stance.
