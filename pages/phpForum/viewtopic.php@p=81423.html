an exercise in minimalism. ZZMT Crawler

  • Quote

Post by Clint » Mon Oct 10, 2005 9:56 am

didnt use a whole lot of parts, but this worked out well for a crawler.

2 xmod bearings
1 lipo
1 zzse pinion gear 6tooth
1 zzse transfer gear blue, for slow crawling speed/high tq.

pretty much just keep cutting until it works.



all 4 wheels touching.



bearings, one to stabilize front, one to stabilize rear, ca'd in place. aluminum tape wrapped around the driveshaft to make it slightly thicker so that it snugly fits the xmod bearings.



next step is a spring suspension/body mount and to fill the wheels with BB's for weight. so far it is going well.

-Clint

Only the rear bearing is pivot, the front bearing is to locate the driveshaft and keep the front end stable. I did not want any lateral pressure on the driveshaft and diff gears, as lipos can get quite beastly.

I'll have to calculate the new rear end gear ratio, but I suspect it's about
100:1? +/-
I'm using the red zzmt motor btw.
