Post by SuperFly » Tue Jan 03, 2006 4:27 pm

For all you guys that are interested in this, I implore you to check out a Kysosho MR02 or at the very least, a Firelap 2.0.

I've gone down this exact same path, and would hope you can learn from my experience. I invested well over $200 on xmod stuff, then well over $300 on MR01 stuff like the ones pictured above. Currently, they all sit unused. If you're at all serious about getting into 1:24 scale cars and you haven't bought anything yet, go straight to the top and get a Kyosho MR-02.

The platform is so superior, and none of the parts are interchangeable. If you put every available upgrade on an MR01, I'd bet money I could beat you with a stock MR02 with an Xspeed motor. It's going to cost you just a little more out of the gate, but unless you have to have AWD, there's just no comparison.
