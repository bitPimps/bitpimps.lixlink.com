Only difference between the two are the bodies.

I own the yellow F-150 with all the hop-ups installed and have all the GPM alloy for the complete truck and car on the way, should be here in a day or so.

As of right now, the only aftermarket for the EVO Xmods are GPM and their aluminum parts.

Check this out for aluminum parts. Great deals!!!

http://bitpimps.lixlink.com/pages/phpFo ... php?t=7145

You can pick-up some CF rod and make yourself a sweet lightweight driveshaft.

You'll have to get the bearings from VXB/Irvineman on Ebay.

Tuner1989 wrote:Hot racing released some aluminum parts on rcmart and the bay recentley.
Good someone mentioned the competition.

Lets compare Rear Knuckles.

HOT-RACING


GPM


As you can see both CAN NOT use stock brake disks. The GPM is thicker and appears stronger. HR has a twist on Brake disk like cap to hold the axle in VS 3 screws to hold the bearings/bushings. Let me tell you what you don't see in the HR version. The HR version comes with Allen head screws were the GPM clearly comes with Phillips. No need to buy more tools with GPM parts.

Also with the group buy going down you can save almost 50% off the ebay prices on the GPM parts.
