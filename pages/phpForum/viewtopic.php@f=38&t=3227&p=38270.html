Porsche baby







I got the body from toyeast, came out to be about $8 with the shipping. The molding could be better but aint bad at all. The medium wheelbase (rsx) is a perfect fit. All you "NEED" to do to mount it is velcro. A lil square on the battery guards/heatsinks (what have you) and then a square on the inside of the body and you're good to go. I've taken a couple of good whacks and the body is still attached to the chassis with minimal travel from the original mounting position. This mounting method also allows for ride height adjustments, easy. I plan on adding a tab holder to the front of the body just to be extra safe. I bought the ferrari body too but becuase of the rear ant holder peg thinger on the chassis, that body is a no go for now. I did notice today that toyeast has the lxx mini z sets up for sale again so I may buy one of those if only so I could use the ferrari body.
