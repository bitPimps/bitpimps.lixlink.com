Well RedRustBucket, to tell you the truth, they are awesome! I have two Q-Steer's with plans to get another or two. I have the red Mazda RX-8 and the red and black AE 86 Tureno with drift wheels. The controllers are about the same size as the car and have the standard two buttons to controls forward/backward and left/right movement as well as a turbo button for those NOS moments. You can get different gears, motors, and wheels (plastic ones for 'drifitng'). They aren't particularly fast but they sure are fun. The motors have good torque to them as they can climb inclines very well. They do have the slot in the back for wheelies and doughnuts. If you look on youtube you can see people who tape coins to the front of the car to shift the weight and actually drift them, countersteering and all. There is also a guy on there who drifts with nothing on the car at all! Here are some cool youtube vids about them.

Coin Drifting

I think what makes them so fun is their tiny size and quality. Gotta love 'em.
