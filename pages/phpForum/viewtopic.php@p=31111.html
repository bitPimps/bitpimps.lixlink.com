Reason 1) As far as custom chargers for SE's, heres an easy one:

http://tinyrc.com/forums/showthread.php ... adid=13862

Reason 2) Packing another controller. I cary a tomy 4 band controller that works for all my cars, even Zip Zaps. I wire the motors backwards in the zip zaps so they work right. I try to stay away from 49 mhz to avoid extra controllers, but I do have a couple. But, you could easily add or swap out a crystal in the 4 band controller to control 49 mhz. So that would drop you to one controller, plenty of room for an SE controller!

Reason 3) All zip zap bodies fit on the SE (old ones too). Plus, using the older bodies makes the SE lighter and faster, as well as handling better.

Reason 4) http://tinyrc.com/forums/showthread.php ... adid=14242
