  • Quote

Post by honda_s2000 » Sat Sep 23, 2006 8:19 pm

So I was at a local market , and a guy had a load of those awfull whirly doo stunt cars and such , kept on looking and these caught my eye.

Little offroad looking , bit sized cars.
But what I noticed even more , was the label that said shen qi wei
Now usually , we never get anything more than fitracer's , so he had a total of 3 of these on the stall. I took the lot.

He wanted about $9.50US for them each , and I wasnt willing to shell out almost $30USD for 3 cars that i wasnt sure about. So I haggled , and haggled , and haggled so much he gave in , I Got the lot (all 3) for t $18USD

So onto the pics









The other two (mitsy pajero , and other orange one) are staying NIB for now.

They are only 2wd , I was kinda dissapointed actually , got all my hopes up , seem pretty high geared and are pretty slow (2.0 slow). The tires are nice and soft though , but magnet steering also dissapointed me. There's 2 cells under there , and i'm convinsed there's some kind of limiter.

So , anyone seen these before ? I recognise the controller , but not the actual cars.

So I better see about a better motor in one , and a 3rd cell/lipo could be fun too.
