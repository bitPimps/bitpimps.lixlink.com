Well, I'm glad I wasn't on the same schedule as the mods,
'cuz I was runnin' behind on this one. Anyway, she's done and
I'm pretty damn happy with it... it may be my favorite, of the
cars I've built. It'll be fun to ship it off to one of the pimp mods...
I wonder who?

Specs:
- stretched Thunder Charger chassis (aluminum tubing)
- tab-lowered
- shaved/sculpted rear tires, so they tuck up into the rear fenders
- shaved inside of body to make room for rear tires and steering
- custom paint (custom-mixed color)
- custom decals
- custom painted controller
- mosfets
- custom pcb cover
- kick-ass mystery 3.8

than normal on this one (I ran the shortest wire
under the tilted fet). I think I'm gonna make this
