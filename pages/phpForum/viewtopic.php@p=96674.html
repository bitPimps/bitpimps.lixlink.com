Post by betty.k » Thu Aug 10, 2006 5:16 pm

first a disclaimer: water isn't a good idea with electronics.

but if ya gotta

motors and batteries can happily work submerged, no need to waterproof them. (although i wouldn't submerge a lipo)
you just need to seal off the pcb, you could coat the whole thing in silicone but it will make replacing wires difficult.

it's a messy option but you could smear grease over the pcb. provided you cover it properly it should keep it safe.

after running in water you'll probably want to spray the motor with some wd40.



having said all that, i'd never purposely submerge any of my rc's but that's just me
