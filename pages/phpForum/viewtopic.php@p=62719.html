Where to begin...

OK... as you all know, I hang out with sg219 and he is building a killer Mini-T... well that has driven me into 1/18th scale big time with me getting my Duratrax Micro Street Force(waaaay fun!!!), my as of yet unbuilt HPI Micro RS4, and now we have this... My Mini-T...



Now you know that since I will be running with 219s blinged out T, stock just wouldn't do... I got this used off of mjime714 from Frequency-RC and BannedRC, and aside from the pimped-out paintwork he did it sports a 3Racing SSG chassis and shock towers, 3Racing ball diff, Hitec micro servo, bearings, CVDs, Losi oil shocks, Peak Racing "Raider" mod motor, Novak SPY esc, and also came with a Novak XXL fm rx and a few other extras(pinions and a Losi 6 cell pack and stuff)...



The esc and rx in this will be getting the velcro treatment to be swappable between this and my MRS4 once I get around to getting the fm Airtronics tx I'm eying up to go with them...

OH WAIT I'M NOT DONE YET LOL...

Next we have the number one reason not to go shopping on ebay right after betty.k posts his new RS4 Rally lol... I was looking to score a used Traxxas 4TEC with decent electronics cheap, and came across this... and with bettys RS4 Rally fresh in my memory, I couldn't pass it up...



I got this for a steal from a fella by the name of "cjdaboozer"(not to be confused with our own "cjdashaqpimp" lol) and it is a Traxxas 4TEC converted to rally with with Nitro 4TEC arms(longer), longer alloy shocks, and the RS4 Rally inner body set, Evo rally body, and HPI rally tires...



It has PEZ dispenser heads for the driver and co-pilot... lol dude used Garfield for the driver and Charlie Brown for the co-pilot... I think I'm going to try to find a replacement for Garfield and move ol' Chuck to the drivers seat lol...



It also came with a Traxxas TQ radio set, a nice GM Racing V6 esc(handles down to 12t, I think), and a Trinity Speed Gems "Cobalt" 14t double mod motor... This esc will also be getting the velcro treatment so I can swap it back and forth between this chassis and my trusty ol' Rusty... Oh, and he threw in a nice Peak Racing stick pack, too, lol... plus, all this...



That's the stock 4TEC arms and shocks so I can run it as a 190mm tourer as well, and a nice set of chrome 5 spokes with some really nice new looking tires PLUS an extra set of brand new dyable white 10 spoke sedan rims...

I got less than $300 tied up in all of the goodies I have shown in this post, so I think I'm doing OK...

I have plans for new bodies and paintjobs for both of these(and my Rusty, too lol) to make them my own... because right now I still feel like I have other peoples' shit when I look at them... I have a decent old MRC charger that I can use for all of the packs for all of my various 1/10ths and 1/18ths, but I see myself investing in a NICE peak charger REALLY SOON, on top of the fm Airtronics system that I mentioned earlier...

EDIT... Both of these rides will be getting their own respective threads in the "it aint no bit" section once I have given them the Bird treatment lol... I just wanted to kill 2 birds with one stone here real quick.

I'm going to cut a piece of 1/8th inch lexan to just the same size as the bottom of the rx and have a little outcrop sticking out, just big enough to drill a hole and mount the little 3Racing alloy antenna mount that came with the mini-t... the antenna threads through the side of the mount and the mount clasps the antenna tube like a drill chuck when you tighten it... anyways I'm going to set it all up, double-sided tape the rx to the lexan with the antenna mast mounted, and then put the velcro on the bottom of the lexan... essentially creating an easily removable unit where I don't even have to mess with the antenna EVER... just unplug the servo and esc, and pull the rx/antenna setup out of the car...
