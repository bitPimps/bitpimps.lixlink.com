You have great host there with Picturetrail just 19.95 a year and they will host 500 picks or 50Meg of space. They are who I use with my pics. To post a pick from them on here just click on (More Options on their site) then #1 (Image URLs) then choose your folder you have pics in. Find the pic you want and highlight the text url of the one you want and right click and choose copy.

Code: Select all

  (click Img button on here) [img](right click choose paste) (click Img button again) [/img]
 
Ex:  [img]http://pic8.picturetrail.com/VOL248/1115503/2102813/25430483.jpg[/img]
