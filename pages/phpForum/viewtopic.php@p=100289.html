On the outside it looks like a rather stock ZZ with a brush painted body.



But pop off the top and you can see that it is far from stock on the inside.







It is a "Cars" chassis with clone PCB, full river lipo, and power switch installed. The stock charging pads still funtion for charging on my modded lipo charger.
