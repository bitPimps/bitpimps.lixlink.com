Um, SWG kinda forgot to mention that aluminum doesn't like Primer any more than Paint... You might be able to get by with a good Etching Primer, but even then I'm not sure if it'd really work well. If you're going to attempt painting your aluminum body, I'd REALLY suggest you Alodine it first. Then I'd probably lay down a layer of Zinc Chromate primer, and move on to the normal paint.
