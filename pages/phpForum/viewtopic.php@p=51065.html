Post by Sdog » Thu Jul 29, 2004 9:35 pm

I won one of these Hitari sets on Ebay from the UK. Just arrived today and thought I would give you the lowdown.

The set comes with one chasis, two shells (WRX and Civic), two sets of hubcaps, three different motors and three different sets of gears. The controller islike no zipzap or bit with a flexible, rubber coated ant. It has a stand alone charger, which I don't care for too much. The controller takes a 9V and the charger two AA's.

Here is the box:



As Hog said, they are just like Zip Zaps. They are the same exact size, the chasis are almost identical, and they have the hubcab style wheels.

The ZipZap is on the left, the hitari on the right. Notice there is no hole for a pivot point suspension bar on the Hitari and the PCB's are different:



Similar spring suspension setup:



I really like the shells in this set. Great detail:









The Civic is on a ZZ chasis with ZZ wheels:



As far as performance is concerned...

They charge real fast and the charge lasts pretty long. They are pretty quick too. The turning radius is a bit large compared to ZZ'z and it turns a little sharper one way than the other. I bought this set used and one of the clips on the PCB was broken, so who knows what meddling occurred. Haven't dove that deep in to them yet.

Post by TeamBonzer » Fri Jul 30, 2004 5:16 am

The controller is totally different from ZipZaps (seperate charger remember). Good thing about the controller is that it has an awsome range (even worked through a brick wall ).

The other main difference is that the Hitari uses a central coil and 2 magnets for steering (see earlier pictures).

99pence is a great deal for these cars, the shell detail is awsome.

They were also sold in NEXT (clothing store with boys toys section) last christmas - but with different packaging and no upgrade parts.

The reason for the seperate charger is to get round the Niko patent.
