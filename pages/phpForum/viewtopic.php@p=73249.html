Alright then... I've been workin' on this for the past several weeks, and I've decided to call it "done"... this is my first video, and it was a shitload of fun to put together. I started out makin' the soundtrack (which some of y'all have heard) with some drum loops and some audio clips... then laid down the guitars, bass and some of the voices. I used GarageBand to record the tune. The Pimp interviews are audio I sampled from the documentary, American Pimp.

Next I moved on to the animations and QT video clips, which I made with my Coolpix 995 (old-ass 3.34 mp camera with some burned-out pixels). I used that footage, plus a shitload of y'alls pics from the galleries here, and put it together in iMovie.

It ain't small at 24.6 MB, but I didn't want to compress it any more. You dial-up muthafuckas (that includes me) may want to download the movie first, then play it (that's free advice from Cabo, so use it!).

Also, I didn't have everyone's Homies, so it was impossible to include everyone... and as far as the pic selection was concerned, I used what I felt were the best pics, as well as all my favorite rides. I wish I could have had everyone's Homies and all the badass cars in my possession, so I coulda made a fuckin' bitPimps epic... maybe that'll be the next one, who knows...

Anyway, just download the muthafuckin' video already.
