Post by gnubee » Sat Oct 18, 2003 12:37 am

my xmod has 15 discrete steps left and right for steering.i have not seen anyone comment on the controller feel though..maybe mine is just too new but i can feel it in my hand when i change between steering angles..i kinda like it..lets me know how much turning i have still available..if i adjust the sensitivity of the steering with the controller it is reduced to 12 steps each way
also i strongly suggest you pimps set aside that tiny useless screwdriver they pack in the kit..just get a nice fresh #1 phillips bit out for your multidriver and you will swear less//unless you twist too hard and break something
if you get the supra kit put in your light kit before attatching your front bumper..have some tape handy for the loose headlight wires..
this car handles like a dream out of the box and i cannot wait to mess with the awd kit and suspension kit to improve it. i do not care at all that there are only about 15 steps each way for steering this car is a blast..
