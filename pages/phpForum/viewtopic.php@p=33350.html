Are you 'hardcore'?

  • Quote

Post by crazydave » Sat Dec 06, 2003 8:50 am

Back in high school, when I was was little skate punk, there was this thing in the punk scene with calling everyone a poser. You know the real poser was that guy that wore eyeliner, and listened to Gene Loves Gezebel last week, and suddenly got a mohawk, and leather jacket, and is all about the Sex Pistols this week, but it was getting out of hand where everyone was calling everyone a poser. A few people that didn't know me would try to call me a poser. Well here I am 20 years later, 33 yo, and I'm still hardcore, and all about my punk roots. So where they at now? Only a few of them turned out to be truly 'hardcore'.

So what's that have to do with bits? Well when I first got on the forums there was a lot people that were like, 'I found them first, I'm sick of all the trendies', and I'm over here like, 'Well gee guys it's not like I didn't know about them, my wife spent the month of September in the hospital, near death, and we spent the remainder of the year trying to get our budget in order. So sorry if I'm not one of the cool kids that got the cool toys before Christmas.'

Well it's almost a year later, and where are they? Where the heck did all them trendies go? Seems bits and ZZs are last years cool present, and the Xmod boards are slammin' while the bit ones seem to be fizzling.

Well, I bought an Xmod, but bits are still my number one priorty. Like punk rock, I found something of myself in these cars, and they will be with me to the end.

So who's with me, who's 'hardcore'? Who's still gonna be here in 2005?
...crazy man, crazy...dig it.
