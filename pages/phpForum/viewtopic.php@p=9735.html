  • Quote

Post by hogjowlz » Mon Jun 02, 2003 10:59 am

someone on trc posted them a few weeks ago and well i went past a biglots and had to find food for the hunger.

heres the lowdown. they dont come in a dome. they are in a regular looking blister package like the toysrus truck.

the controller: a slightly modified rip off of the originall tomy. charger is on top not side and it has a floppy antenna and on/off switch and it feels better than the tomy controller.

the car: they have an suv and what kinda looks like a wrx with a buncha non working headlights. the bodies are not direct ripoffs of tomy bodies which is a major plus for clones. the chassis goes straight and turns well. the gears look cheap but are fairly smooth and make a quiet yet strange noise when running. the wheels are red. yes red and they work well and are like the tomy wheels but are 6spoke instead of 8. the pcb is pretty much the same but its flipped and instead of a piece of wire for the antenna it has a rigid piece of metal thats bent into a square thingy and well its got the best range out of any 27mhz ive had and believe me i know the limitations of 27mhz cars. the motor that came in it looks a heck of a lot like my "pink clone motor" and it seems to run like it!!

my final word[b/]
if you see these things, buy as many as you can carry. for the motor spring and red wheels alone they are worth 5bucks. i havnt modded much on her yet but i think the car itself is worth driving as long as it doesnt fall apart after a week(we shall see on that)
