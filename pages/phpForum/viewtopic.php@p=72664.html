Post by LBRC » Tue Jun 14, 2005 8:43 pm

It won’t damage the CB but if you mount it without the middle gear it will be slow, even with FETs, and the bypass mod it will be slower than the ZZ upgrade motors. Add a LiPo and it will still be slower than a NX motor with the same LiPo. With an Iwaver CB it’s another story altogether so I’m thinking that maybe Codesuidae’s Duty Cycle mod might just be what the doctor ordered when running a ZZMT motor with the SE circuit board, but haven’t tried one yet.
