Yeah I built it to be a drag car, I am racing my friend in a drag race, and we had to rebuild everyting and then race to see wich is the best car. I figured it would be cool to have skinny front rims. I glued the front tires on the rims.

I also did the spin brush hybrid mod to the motor, and did a power switch bypass mod. I shortened the driveshaft, and now I am extending the chassis. It isnt pretty but it works. I also lightened and locked the differential. By lightening it, it will be able to spin with less force having to be put on it.
