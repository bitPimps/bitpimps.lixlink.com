Track update: Pit road and railings superglued in place......


10 tubes of superglue. 5/8" high. The railing is pretty strong and "springy". If you try to rely on them too much, they'll turn you the wrong direction. That's good, didn't want them to be an advantage. btw, the cat has been taking a strong interest.


I noticed a minor interference when driving over a screw. Will probably use the Corvette "glue and screw" technique, and remove them and fill, now that the track is glued to the supports. Used urethane glue for that. The opening in the pit road is where the garages/winners circle will go. There is also a 3" removeable section of railing, just off the banking on the right, so you can drive into homie town, mel's diner, CabaWabo's chop shop, etc. (heh, the ideas are flowing.) The handle cut-outs make it easy to move around, and will allow me to hang it on the garage wall.

Stay tuned brothers, next week the track gets a paving job, and ground-breaking on the model-railroading scenery. **TAKING SUGGESTIONS FOR SCENERY IDEAS**
