Post by crazydave » Thu Nov 17, 2005 4:12 pm

If you get the 04, the front shock tower will break. I can pretty much garantee that, but replace the shock tower, and you should be good to go. I replaced mine with a GPM alloy one, and I haven't broken anything since. Here's a video I made a little while ago of me romping it.

http://bitpimps.lixlink.com/pages/phpGa ... 176&pos=12

Its just running an Iwaver Xspeed, and bearings, and its plenty fast for me. You'll probably end up doing something nutty with it.

The those new ones with all the shocks, I don't like all the shocks, and they're not oil shocks. I think oil shocks would have been a better improvement, but I suppose the catilever setup gives you more travel. The front suspension is independent, but I don't like how bulky the front end looks now. One clear advantage the new ones have is using standard micro servos, so you can replace them. The steering in my 04 is weak, and I've heard that's a problem of Iwavers. I just have to start it 2 notches to the left when I start, then after a few minutes I can center it for most of the run, then I have to keep bumping it to the right as the battery weakens.

Mine is also one of the firt run 04s, I read somewhere they improved slightly with time.
