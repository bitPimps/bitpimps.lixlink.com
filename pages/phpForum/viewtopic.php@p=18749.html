Yag don't use enamel paint in your airbrush mang. Its HELL to clean up after and you always have some residue left from previous colour.

I use acrylic only and mix 4 paint to 1 thinner.

If you use enamel I would do 2 paint to 3 thinner. Just seems to make the paint go on that much smoother.
