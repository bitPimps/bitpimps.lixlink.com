Post by kwikbb » Sun Mar 16, 2003 2:47 pm

Insanely Fun! This thing’s performance is outstanding, here’s what I ran it with;
I tested with a tomy 50 mah battery double charged
9.86:1 gear ratio
Bit racer soft tires in the front
Bit char-g stock mediums in the rear

Crazy torque, blazin’ top end combine for a sick motor… I love this thing, I think more than my R-spec… nah j/k. It’s got crazy speed and stability in the corners, I love the acceleration to… I think I’m going to switch the gear ratio to 12:1 for more acceleration. Plus the friggin thing is damn fast for linoleum, taming it down won’t be that noticeable.
There’s no doubt in my mind that it’s one of my favorites now.

I tried it in my Wild char-g too; it zips around pretty good for a wild now… probably comparable to a bit w/ 2.2. (again a double charge is a must). The added torque of the motor gives it that extra umf to get going.

Yo pimps-

Here's an interesting fact about the Formula 34 that makes it a little different than other motors: it coasts. I've tried a lot of motors, and the Formula 34 from Tiny and the Perfection 3.8 from Toy East are the only 2 motors I've noticed this important characteristic with. If you are actually doing some real racing, the ability for your car to coast a little when you let off the gas does wonders for handling in corners, and allows you to hop back on the gas without doing a torque donut. Check it out, peeps.
