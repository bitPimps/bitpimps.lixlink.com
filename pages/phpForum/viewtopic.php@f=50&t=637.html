Stoli's Dual Cell Motor Works

Post by Stoli » Sat May 24, 2003 10:23 pm

Well guys got bored tonight and since my RX-7's motor crapped out on me tonight I figured "what a perfect time to rip it for parts"

So I came up with a dual cell. These bits have a weire going from the PCB to a small red LED. I figured I would take the old battery connectors out of the old dead bit and transfer them to that pair of wires to see if I coul do dual cell without modding the PCB since I do not have a soldering iron.

Well folks it works now my ride be phat and fast. Stunts too. Hope you enjoy.
