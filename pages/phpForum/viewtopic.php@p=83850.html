Thanks to SG I have a great overland well I decided to do something with it and I built a VW Baja body just for it. The body is a VW body from AMT’s Bee model kit (the kit had a funky front end so I cut the front end off another VW kit and Bondo’ed it on.

I then had to Bondo the Top because it had a cast folding roof so lots of sanding and shaping to get the roof right.

Ok I love this blue (as if you couldn’t tell) after several coats of paint I added some decals I had (from a Integra kit) then added the bars. I still have a little more detail work to do but its getting there.
