Charge 'N' Go Gets Da Rip 'N' Tear Treatment

  • Quote

Post by crazydave » Wed May 05, 2004 9:54 pm

I got this Charge 'N' Go at Walgreens for 6 bucks. Wasn't expecting much, but it handles good, tracks straight with the steering adjuster dead center. Only problem was the griding gears, and front pins that hung halfway out the wheels. Seemed like it was begging to have the wheels changed, so I put Thunder Charger wheels on it, and Tomy gears so it's nice and smooth. Now it's a pretty decnt car.

I'll probably eventually do a body for this one, but I never had an Accord, so I thought I'd have a little fun with it. It was done really sloppy, so I cleaned it up and shaved all (most) of the molding lines. Then I filled in the front slot to give it a smooth bumper.

I wanted a ripped style graphic, I tried to draw one to cut out, but I couldn't do anthing I was satisfied with, so I just tore tape. Worked perfect, got just the effect I was looking for, and it was almost too easy, I feel guilty for doing it.

I added some tail pipes, painted dthe wheels black, and slapped a Tomy decal on the hood to finish it off.
