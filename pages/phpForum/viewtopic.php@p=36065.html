I'm always talking about them, because I loved them. (Wish I didn't sell them, but I love the guitar I bought ) Been wanting to show them off, but I lost the pictures when I moved a couple years ago.

Well I finally found them. So here's my babies that I miss, and keep talking about.



This is a pic similar to the one I got in RC Car Action. (They kept the actual pic) That's my Traxxas Stampede in the back. It was upgraded with bearings, slipper clutch, a Tekin Rebel reversing ESC, Trinity Ruby (17 turn triple machine wound) motor, topped off with a Parma '90 Blazer body, custom painted by me of coarse.

The car in front is my Team Losi XXT. A sport kit that I upgraded with bearings, titanium turnbuckles, purple anodized motor plate, Novak RacerEX ESC, Trinity Midnight, or Peak Nightmare 2 stock motors, various tires, springs, shock oils, etc. etc. depending on track conditions. I later added RPM 5-spoke rims that I never got a picture of. I had 5 battery packs, a couple Orion, the rest Ballistic Batteries.




The Stampede doing what it does best, flying. This is a mundane air, this thing regularly saw 5-6 ft. air. I had one good air pic, and I sent it to RC Car Action...they didn't use that one.



The XXT by itself. It's painted with Pactra poly-carbonate paint. Cheezoom Teal, and Lemans pink. I added shadow with candy purple, highlights with white, and I painted the windows Candy Purple too, for that cheezy ol' school purple tinting look.




This is my RC10DS that I set up for all out speed. It had a HPI 2-speed automatic transmission in it. (a centrifugal clutch), and a 10-turn handwound Reedy modified. (A freakin' 80 dollar motor!) Borrowing the radio, ESC and batteries from my Losi, I believe I came close to breaking 50 mph in this thing. it would pull wheelies when it clicked into second gear. First time I ran it, I tried to turn at top speed to avoid a wall, and the car lifted off the ground, flew about 3ft. in the air for about 20ft, then bounced and rolled another 20ft., I thought it was a goner, but I just ripped the boddy moints out. 3 dollar fix.

The Red Frog stickers on the side are some holographic ones from a gumball machine. I must have spent 15 bucks on frog stickers trying to get a matching one for the other side to base my theme off of.

That was most of them, I also had a Bolink Digger, and a Kyosho rally car, but neither one of those was ever worth taking pictures of.
...crazy man, crazy...dig it.
