I don't have micro xmods. If its ground clearance is lower than the old zipzaps, I prefer take zzse that has proportion control and swap body. What I don't like zipzaps (xmods also) is the chassis and body has a big different for ground clearance.

Yes I have x-trek pro. here is my review.
(some points may be pro or con for u)

-super fast and high torque
-Proportion control
-mini-z size transmitter
-small turning diameter
-steering and throttle is quite sensitive (maybe too sensitive)
-high ground clearance
-different size of front and rear wheel
-stuttering problem. lost control very often
-controllable range is 6 feet only
-the car handling level is medium to hard if zipzaps is easy
-only 4 different bodies to select
-no led head light and tail light

Just briefly about those SHEN trucks... I notice someone said the wheels are awful. Well they are actually one of the better micro r/c wheel designs.
