Post by LBRC » Mon Aug 01, 2005 11:19 pm

Definitely a different car the CRT is a 1:32 chassis, bigger than an Epoch but smaller than a Mini-Z, uses Revel slot car bodies, but unfortunately it's a little too big for some of the really cool 1:32 slot car boddies. I don’t have one but have driven it, definitely needs a track, preferably a good and large level, non pile, indoor outdoor carpet. Everything is exceedingly tight but there is almost no ground clearance so even on a RCP track you can have problems if one of the tabs is sticking up.
