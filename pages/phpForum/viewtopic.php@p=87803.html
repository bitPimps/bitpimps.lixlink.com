The overland is fully alloy with FET upgrades a modified motor oil shocks and countless other upgrades.

Thanks guys. Yeah, i was suprised how well the wheel wells hold the big ol swampers in and with no cutting . I didnt origionaly buy the body for the overland but it fit so well i couldnt help it.

As for the super swampers, yep, they will fit any of the first gen xmod/mini z line of wheels. Im using two sets of wide rear tires from the mini z) With the evo truck wheels you may be hard pressed to get them to fit well,, they are slightly larger diameter and a smaller width( and yes, you would need to grind down that lip) But of course any older xmod wheel will work on the new axles so that would fix your problem.
