hey guys, just got my body done so i thought will post up some pics of it

still not on the car yet but will be in next few day

truck has some mods done to :
-shorter wheelbase in front, stock in rear
-20wt oil all thugh shocks with 1 spring cut off the top edge of coil
-proline moabs 2.2 size
-5 cell, 1100MAH receiver pack for steering only
-2s 7.4v 1500mah lipo for motor, gives around 2 hour runtimes
-nosram hawk power reverse speedie

anyway, on with the pics
