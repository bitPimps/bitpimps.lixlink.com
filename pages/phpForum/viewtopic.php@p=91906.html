Scored these last week in a set:


They came on Good Friday. That day I took my son and one of his best buddies from his Martial Arts class out for the day. We went bowling, go-carting, did lunch, they dropped them off at the mall. About five minutes after I dropp'd them off, Lil Matrix had a reaction to something we ate at lunch so I picked them up and headed home. LM went to sleep so I was showing his buddy "Par" (Parnell) my collection, well my "Stash" of unopened goodies and we talked. He told me that he and his brother used to have a couple Micros. I showed him what I had gotten that day. He told me his father "loved" Speed Racer (old school) and it was his favorite cartoon. He has the matchbox and a couple 1/24 scale Mark5's. So I ended up giving Par a couple Nakamichis for he and his brother (Marcus - 9yrs) and the Mark5 that I had just gotten for his Dad. He said his Dad will freak when he gets it. Guess we may be getting a few couple new members here soon !!!
I guess Good Friday has it's mysterious charms in different ways....
