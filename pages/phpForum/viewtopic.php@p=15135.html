lol @ hog - my next mod is stepping on my car so the wheels are angled for better traction lol

crazydave, no trimming the chassis, just the tires.
I will tell ya this though - I got 3 sets of 10mm alloys.
each set came with completely different tires, in shape, in size, and in compound. On 2 of 3 sets, I had to shave down the tires, too tall. 1 set I'm going to have to cut the width down, they're way too wide and actually hang over the alloy rim alot.
All 3 sets, I have to make sure the tire is on the rim, flush with the backside of the rim, where the rim meets the chassis. If any tire is over hanging there, you're gonna get rubbing on the chassis.

In those particular shots, I didn't trim down the front wheel wells yet, and it is needed, I can't turn with the body on, the tires hit....
but, I shouldn't need to trim much since I've taken most of it off the tire, I doubt anyone would notice the difference unless I made them aware of it.

I like the 10mm's, they look dope, but I'm guessing they're just a tad more work to fit than 9mm's or 9.5mm's - can't say for sure though as I don't own anything but 10mm's.

oh, and by the way, 1 of my sets came stock with treaded tires.
