Post by LBRC » Wed Jul 06, 2005 2:43 pm

Yes always take them back immediately, now, as soon as possible, pronto. Returns benefit everyone, they tell RS what needs to be fixed, and provide a convent supply of spare project parts, as long as the store manager isn’t a buy the book Charley.

I just exchanged a Vette because the right front wheel wobbled just a hair. The funny thing is that the guys in the store will salvage a few parts off of it and in a week or so when I go looking for parts they will give me the “damaged chassis” back.

It’s one of the few things RS does right so you might as well make use of it, besides you have already paid for the privilege since the cost of the returns is already factored into the $24.99 price tag.

Is reverse just slower with the NX motor or with the red too? Most likely your CB is bad, there is a chance that replacing the transistors with FETs could fix the problem but maybe not, so exchange it and with a little luck someone will be able to scarf it up as project chassis.
