Thanks Shaun, glad ya like it. But my advice is if you want one, build it. It's not too hard, and I went with a more difficult way than I had to anyway. If you don't want to deal with a stupid retract spring, install the internal spool so it's edge is just slightly proud of a front fender. Brush finger along fender, and it magically retracts; pull on hook to spool it out. Not as clean, but more than good enough!
