Post by MadScott » Sat Oct 18, 2003 11:43 am

Just a little info on these boys. The wife picked one up for me about 2 weeks ago. Typical bullet clone style of controls as you can see. Great little charging mechanism though as they have a small cable that actually plugs into the bottom of the car from the controller. It takes just a hair longer than a normal bit/ZZ to charge but nothing outrageous.

Performance. The car has some great torque to it and the giant wheels and clearance helped it crawl across the carpet pretty well. That being said the steering is such a trip with the whole front end spinning that it is very hard to control and turn.

In all, it's kind of a cool micro to mess with but really nothing more than a a novelty toy.
