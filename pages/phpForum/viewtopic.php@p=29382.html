Welp, I couldn't wait any longer for reviews once I knew a RadioShack across town had 'em in stock. Picked up a red Supra and a white Supra for SuperFly and myself last night. Right outta the box, I'm shocked at what you get for $50. The thing drives great and it's pretty damn fast stock... probably about like a dual-celled bit.

There are some things I hadn't read about (I'm sure they've been said already), like you can adjust the battery boxes fwd and bkwd to adjust the weight distribution. Also, there are tie rods to adjust toe-in and toe-out, and a million other cool features.

The controller does seem a bit a little bit wussy, but I love the way feels... I instantly felt comfortable driving the car. I'll just be careful not to drop it cuz I don't think it will handle that too many times.

Anyway, most of this has already been said, so I'll stop rehashing... the main thing for me is that this car could have been $100 and I would have felt like I got a badass car for the money. Tonight, we'll race in a half-empty 50x100 ft. room with a smooth painted floor and I can't fuckin' wait!
