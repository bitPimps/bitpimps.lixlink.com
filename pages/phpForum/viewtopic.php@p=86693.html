Post by HACHI-RYOKU » Fri Jan 06, 2006 7:33 am

I've got a Xmod gen 1 with, bearings, steering resistor mod, 3 x 2 stack of 7317 fets and a plasma-atomic motor with a 9 ro 10 tooth pinion.

Then I have a Xmod EVO with ball bearing s and a stage 2 motor set with the highest rat shack gears.

They are both the same speed. Basically, the EVO is better. I've barely done anything to it and it's just as good as the Gen1 that I've modded the hell out of.

The gen 1 turns a little better, but this is after changing a few resistors and centering the steering servo.

Also, with the gen 1, I had to replace the reap axle cup with an alloy one because the stock one couldn't handle the increased power. I also had to repair the servo extensively when I took it apart because it came with wires that were already half broken and came off with barely any pressure. There were problems inside the servo too. The gears on the gen 1 don't mesh all that well compared to the EVO as well. The list can go on and on.

The Gen 1 Xmods can be a good car but they take a bit of work and the same effort on another car will make the other car much better.

Go with the EVO. Better yet, go with a mini-z. You wont be dissapointed.
