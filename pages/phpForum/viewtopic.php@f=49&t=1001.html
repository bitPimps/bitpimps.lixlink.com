If you've read This Thread Then you know that '5$ clones at biglots = ruling'. Only problem is the 2 goofy body styles don't = ruling.

I found out right away they are a tad too long like Cannonballs, so I salvaged a Cannonball body, but now I've got all these $5 cars, and I don't want to buy more crappy Cannonballs just for bodies.

I found out if you trim the front tab the way I show in the dotted lines, in the picture below, that bit bodies will fit perfectly.


I'm building an army of TC dual cells, because I love them so much, especially now that I'm making use of unused bodies.

The one on the right is a Cannonball body from my first micro RC that almost turned me off to the hobby. No trimming required to fit that body.

The one on the left is from my Skyline set, and those bodies are a little smaller and snugger than other bits. I had to trim the front wheel well just a tiny tad, after moving it back. I actually way over did it.

The one in the middle is a LXX RX-7 body that seems identical to my MS one but it sits a tad higher in front. It fit the TC chassis perfectly after trimming the front tab.
