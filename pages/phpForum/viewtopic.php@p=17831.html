Any pimps who have 2 speed bits or ms's, feel free to chime in yo...

OK, I finally broke down and picked up a MS Pro today at TRU. At 19.99, I figured I wouldn't get one much cheaper.

I took if for a spin and I am sitting here wondering what all the hype is or what I am missing? First thing I noticed, is the car when not boosting and just taking off, is SLOW to take off, much like first gen ZZ's. And it is NOISY as hell.....but when you hit the boost button, it is much quiter and if you hold the boost and then hit forwar, it takes off faster. Not meaning top speed, I mean take off speed.

I understand the concept of the boost and that the current is cut back and then boost button allows full potential, but I guess I was expecting more...

I haven't tried any other motors yet, could be the 2.2 that comes with, but man, I was kinda let down.

Any advice? Any others notice this or may I have a jacked up ride out the box?
