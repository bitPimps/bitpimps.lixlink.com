Hmm, that's odd. I submitted a review today for the hell of it, I just said "for under $20" and they censored it w/ (...), when others mentioned paying $14.95 and $20. I was nonspecific even.

I don't really care, I just thought it was odd.


I just realized, they took it entirely out of context, I didn't even say their car was under $20, i was just saying a car with these features for under $20 was unheard of. Now they made me look like an ass, those assholes.
A 4WD, fully suspended RC, w/ front and rear differential gears, and its upradeable with motors, gearing, bodies, wheels, and more (...)That's unheard of. That would make this a bargain. Especially now that its on clearance.

Out of the package, its a pretty fun car, decent speed, easy to drive. I also had issue with a gear breaking in the steering's gearbox, but I purchased a replacement car, and have had no problems, just don't run into things. It is not good for running in dirt, the suspension gets stiff and creaky after exposure to dirt. I bought the hop up kits mostly for the wheels and bodies, as they look awesome. The offroad kit would be good for a crawler, but a bit slow for my taste. The speed set was too sluggish off the line, but I found the speed motor was slightly faster with the stock gears. These make an excellent basis for projects, quite a few people on them intranet forums have modified these with hobby grade electronics, with good results.

I'd reccomend picking up some AA rechargable batteries to go with it.
