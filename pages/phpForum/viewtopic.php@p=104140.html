I finally broke down, and bought my first Xmod... It was a Nissan TITan. I wanted the F150, but they were out, so I spent an extra $10 just to not have the H2.

The good news is this horribly FUGLY picture, isn't the real product.


However, this is.



The bad news is, the wheelbase IS fucked up. Body sits too far forward on chassis. Rear tire nearly scrubs back of fender, front is obviously not centered.

Also, not sure if it's an X-mod Evo thing or what, but there is a useless molded in plastic bar under the bed that STOPS ANY ARTICULATION.

Body has potential, but out of the box looks bland and boring (much like the real Titan.)
Fit and finish isn't stellar, but not *too* far off.
Paint doesn't really match from body to rockers/bumpers.
Paint is a fairly decent hue of maroonish red, but without some kind of metallic or peral it just comes off ugly and flat.
Nice details, for a soccermom ride. Definately would have to "make a man out of it" with offroad accessories, and trail rash. yes...Lots of trail rash...

Now the "offroad kit" lift and tires.



-
Either way, I'm looking for more of either a Ford Ranger, Ford Explorer, Toyota singlecab, or a Suzuki Samuri / Suzuki-Geo KickTrack body, however that's a topic for a later thread.
