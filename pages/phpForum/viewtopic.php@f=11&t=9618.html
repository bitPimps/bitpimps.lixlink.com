I decided to start working on my Contest entry before December creeps up on me and I miss the deadline.

I started custom building the chassis and I am using a muscle machines front end with a custom built rear end. The back tires will be run using a slot car gear and a Super Slicks motor. I used the COE original wheels but I fastened the front wheels using screws and nuts.

The body is a COE mold (my first) that I custom pored myself and plan to add the step sides and the back. I will leave the back end as a surprise for now as I have a couple of plans in the works and not sure what one I am going to use.
