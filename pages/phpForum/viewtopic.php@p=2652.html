As promised, here's the wheel diameters...

Bit Rear wheel diameters (around the outside, where the tires fit only)...
- Outer diameter (the raised area that the inside of the tires rests on) = 12.65mm
- Inner diameter = 11.2mm

Bit Front wheel diameters
- Outer diameter = 11mm
- Inner diameter = 9.7mm

This is most interesting because I had previously thought the Nazi Rims were 9mm diameter...If they are, that's SMALLER than the stock front wheels of the Bit.
