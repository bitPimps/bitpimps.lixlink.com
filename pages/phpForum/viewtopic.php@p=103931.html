the 1/18th's have been keeping me poor and in trouble with the Mrs. lately This scale is like meth - addictive and turns me into a jabbering mess!

I spent more than i intended on the minizilla, so i've done a cheap job of resurrecting the half eight after i cooked the original electrics. i picked up a nanodragon ESC from ausmicro, but i couldn't help making a few mods while i was at it

I shifted the motor to the rear behind the servo and turned the centre diff 180*, so that i can run the packs for the minizilla down the other side. also knocked up a top deck out of scrap alloy and put in a RAD400 motor courtesy of Tuner1989
