Been thinking a while of what I should give to my little cousin. She's only like three and her interest in Cars is amazing! Her dad, my uncle, inspired me to get into RC's, so i am pretty sure the love for RC is inborn for my little cousin. lol. Anyway She loves the color pink, and when I showed her another Microcar she went crazy for them! So I wanted to make her something that she could use when all the older cousins come over and start messin around on my track, she could at least have something to practice or play with.

After finding the color, I wanted to give it a theme, and after the second thick coat of pink (made it a bit durable for the youngn) I decided, this would be one hell of a Pink Panther car!!

So once I was finished I got a little bored and I wanted to have some fun with this one before I said my goodbyes. So now I bring you pics of

THE PINK PANTHER PIMP MOBILE, including: A BONUS VIDEO COVERAGE!
