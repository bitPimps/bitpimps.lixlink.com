Well, I didn't win that auction..... so OK... I'll PM you about that...
It isn't that I don't like them, the Vendetta is pretty sweet for what it is. 1/18-ish scale is just wierd for me. I like higher speed offroad bashing and I'm not running at any tracks, so they get unused and collect dust. Even stadium trucks don't work for me because of the lower ground clearance.

As far as the motor, I'll get a pic up in a bit. But basically, look where the endbell attaches to the can. There are 3 marks on the can, and a indicator on the plastic part of the endbell that should be in line with the middle mark on the edge of the can. If you loosen the screws that attach the endbell, you can twist the endbell to either of the other marks. Moving it to one increases torque, but reduces speed. The other way reduces torque and response time, but increases speed. I can't remember which way does one or the other, but it was noticeable differences.

I got this info from 1/18th.com a long while back.
