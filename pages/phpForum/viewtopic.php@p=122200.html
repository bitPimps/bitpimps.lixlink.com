Micro T Track WIP (Now Fortified with Video)

  • Quote

Post by crazydave » Sun Apr 05, 2009 1:53 pm

When I moved into my new place I thought the patch off the side of the porch would make a great place for a Micro-T track. Here it is a month before we moved in.



Welp, by the time we moved in the grass was grown to almost a foot tall in some spots. I asked a several people how do I get rid of grass, and get it back to dirt, and everyone just said, spray grass killer on it. That killed the grass, but then I had a bunch of dead grass. After a back breaking afternoon with the rake and hoe, my friend helped me clear the grass out. But then the roots stuck out, and the Micro-Ts would get hung up on them. So I realized I was going to have to till up the soil and pull out the roots out, that would basically be a nightmare, so it sat for a few months. When I saw the grass start to grow back in, I decided it was time to do something before my previous work went to waste.

So what I'm doing is soaking it down, using piece of wood to scrape off the high spots, which exposes the roots, and then I dig those up with a garden shovel, and use the wood to smooth the dirt back into place. It's messy tedious work, but I'm starting to make progress, and its giving me hope. (It don't look like much, the shadow frames what I have done.)



I'm going to go back out now, and see what I can get done. I'm hoping i can get most of it leveled off today.
