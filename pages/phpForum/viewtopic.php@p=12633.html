Alright, after running the boats on Sunday I've got more info...

Steering
Just like a tank. There's 2 buttons, each controls the forward/reverse of each propeller under the boat. I was initially worried about the boats not going really straight when both motors are activated but for the most part it goes straight just fine.

Turning though was actually MUCH better than i expected. You can make wide or SUPER tight turns depending on how you use the steering...Even doing 360s while in place! Pretty sweet.

Speed
Just fast enough to stay fun, get a pair of 3.8s in there and this shit could be SERIOUS fun.

Here's a pair of vids I took, they're shitty quality (my little digicam ain't meant for doing good movies, but it takes little movies anyway) but you can kind of get the idea.
