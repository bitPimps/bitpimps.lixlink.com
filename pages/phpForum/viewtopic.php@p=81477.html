crazydave wrote:
SuperFly wrote:
SHAUN wrote:oh I for got wat grit sand paper should i use i was thinkin 300. But im asking you guys because your like pros at it.
Start with 400, finish with 800-1200. That's how I pre-primer sand anyway.
That's what the pros reccomend.

On bits, I lay the paint so thing, I think 400 grit sanding scratches still show. I like to use 600 grit before I paint. On the other hand, the "pros" say that anything finer than 400 and the paint might not stick, but I've never had a problem.
You're right, 400 scratches will show through thin paint. I only use 600 or finer after primer, and use very light pressure. I find that it's the pressure that causes the deep scratches that will show through the paint. Right before I paint, I use an old t-shirt and a fair amount of pressure and buff out the primer a little.

edit: BTW, I don't think I mentioned that I do my sanding in the basement sink with a little water dribbling over it. Wet-sanding keeps the paper from getting clogged and the water provides a little lubrication to help keep it schmoooov.
