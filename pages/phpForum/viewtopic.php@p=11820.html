Up until recently I've been strictly a ZipZap driver, however, I had so much fun with this little MS, I might just switch to Bits!

Anyhow, 2.2 MS Turbo, tab lowered, custom paint, custom spoiler, custom sunroof, sub array from MuscleMachines DieCast Tunerz (notice the dash board is the backplane where the antenna comes out), MS wheels front and back for that symatrical look, a little underbody detail work, and though the pics don't show it...tinted windows that allow you to just barely see the PCB.




...now off to the track to break in that paint job!
