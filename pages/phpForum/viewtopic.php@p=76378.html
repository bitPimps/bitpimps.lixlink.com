Awhile back DT and I made a small transaction with each other and I was a new owner of a MS speed set with a clear EVO body. Well I decided to finally do something with it. I used nothing but testors water based brush paint for the entire shell. I used the chassis I built awhile back that has 3 1/4 AAAs and a hardwired white endbell motor. Modded the wheels off of a muscle machine SSTuner and some offroad tires. I know it is still missing something and I am open for suggestions.
