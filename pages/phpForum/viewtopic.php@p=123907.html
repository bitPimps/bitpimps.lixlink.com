2009 Xmods Evo changes

  • Quote

Post by cj » Wed Jul 08, 2009 1:40 pm

Finally went into a R$ the other day and picked up a $29.99 Xmod Corvette and they gave me a Under car Lighting kit for free. Now those that have been here forever now I use-to work for R$ for 10 years and know a lot about their policies and products. This latest version of the cars have good, bad and ugly points.

Quality just feels cheaper and better at the same time!

The case they come in looks a lot nicer since they removed the foam and use molded plastic to hold everything. The old foam was in my opinion better in that you could cut it to hold the car with the wheels on it and sill put everything in the case for safe transport. With this new design you might as well throw the thing out because it will not protect the car unless you screw the body back down, remove the wheels, and put everything back in exactly the way you found it. Including the extras screws, wheel nuts, and wheel wrench back in the plastic bag and tape it to the body.

The Corvette body was molded as two pieces. Only the rear end is screwed on. It does come with extra mirrors. The mirrors are a flexible plastic/rubber.

Chassis at first glance looks the same until you notice the lack of a Crystal.


Preset channels. Can't even change it on the controller.

With some de-soldering/soldering you can change the channel.


I still have yet to run it but based on the defect it has out of the case I doubt the steering works 100%.

The motor is COVERED in beeswax type epoxy to limit your chances of desoldering the motor and mod the motor.

The battery cover now has a lock screw.?

The finish on this with the screws look like they were over torqued in some places with stress discolorations in the plastic around them.
