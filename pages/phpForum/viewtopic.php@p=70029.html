Post by sg219 » Tue May 10, 2005 7:35 pm

I forgot...sorry.

It comes unbuilt. If you like to know what its like to build a TYCO or NIKKO RC, then this is for you.

It comes in several parts and needs to be assembled. Not a hard procedure at all. Especially if you can built an Xmod.

The detail is really good.

The speed is definatly NOT there.

It makes sounds, like motor reving and shells being fired when you go forward.

The steering is tank-like. The front wheels are their own pod and contain 2-180 motors for the drivetrain and steering.

The front shocks are for show and the rear shocks are functionable.

Its front wheel drive.

Value for $16 USD....B-

It was cool to build a Tyco-like RC, and to see the parts involved with it. It has decent tires, and a cool body. The speed is absolutley overrated. Use it as a play car for the kids when they come over.
