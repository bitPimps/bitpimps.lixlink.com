I've used different varieties of tape, and think that all of them are good for certain things. Pro grade auto masking is great for windows, but has a tendency to be difficult to cut. I usually use this type for striping. I have yet to go wrong with plain masking tape, but I generally have to watch my spray.
The color tends to bleed easier with masking tape, but can generally be avoided by spraying lightly.
