Post by crazydave » Sat Jun 28, 2008 8:44 am

You say stock everything? So you are using the stock push on plastic pinion? I had the same problem with the stock pinion. I could've swore it was coming from inside the transmission. I discovered the clicking is from the pinion slipping. I ground the splines off the shaft, made a flat side, and picked up a Robinson Racing metal pinion with a grub screw to hold it on, and I never had another problem... with the tranny.
