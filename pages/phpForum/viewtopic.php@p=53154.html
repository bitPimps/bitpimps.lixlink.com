Yeah, it can turn into a big production pretty damned quick because I can’t leave anything out (got no damned space).

Here’s the shit I go through for them sexy pics…

- Pull out the poster boards that the stuff is going to be sitting on
- Move the knick-knacks and greeting cards and whatnot (women and their girly shit) off the short bookshelf I use to sit the stuff on
- Get the gear bag out, pull out the camera and slap in the lens I’m going to use, pull out the flash and mount it on its’ special stand or tripod, then mount the wireless transmitter on the camera
- Make sure all the batteries (in the flash and camera) have enough juice
- Make sure all the camera settings are right (ISO, Shutter, Aperture, White Balance, etc…)
- Take some test shots to make sure the exposures are right
- Finally, start taking the pictures

And when it’s done I gotta pack it all back up and put it away.

That’s just my ‘regular’ gear, I’ve got studio gear too (umbrellas, softboxes, strobes) but they’re just taking up space in the tiny-ass storage room we have because it’s just too much hassle to setup and breakdown.
