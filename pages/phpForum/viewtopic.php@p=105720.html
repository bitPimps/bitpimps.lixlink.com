Best way to describe the fence is to show it. I used materil from a plant package (webbed material) to make the fence. the post are wood dowls cut and the gates are made from plastic tube heated over a candle and bent into a square and the end glued togther.

I then painted all the fence in silver then used Ink washes from Reaper Miniatures and made the fence look rusted. I sanded the gate chain (made from necklace chain) then also rusted that up.



This is the material I made the fence out of.


Here are the overhead doors on the garage and the gas pump island and I also got the Toilets installed!


the office with counter, wallpaper and wanscot.



The gray outside walls are just primer they will be white and the trim needs another coat and the doors need to be finished painting and track finished. gas pumps will be next
