This has been a long time coming I did not realize that this project has been sitting in my “to get to pile” for almost a year now….

I picked this up the other day and well hated how it looked so I changed the chassis, wheels, got rid of the pipes and used more putty on this body then I have on any other body in the past (finished or unfinished).

The rear fins are all putty I was not going to put them on originally but I liked the look after I decided to change it up.

I have wore out my fingers sanding on this and I still have a ton more to do. I plan to finish this soon and not wait another year to finish it up. The Grill is still there its covered with tape as I primed the body to see where touch up was needed in the putty department.

First off, I've always thought a landau-style funeral coach on torque-thrusts looks badass! I really like the idea of fins, but I'm not really feeling those yet. It feels like you're trying to force several era's of car onto one: Boxy 70's, 60's horizontal roundy lights, modern commercial glass roof height, late-50's/early 60's fins; not saying it won't work, just that it's new to me. You might even wanna ditch the license plate mounted above rear bumper if you do make the rear door open, it'll probably interfere.
