I imagine the trick will be balancing the two motors. I figure one of the important things that pcb is doing is regulating the power distribution to make sure the tail rotor is getting not too much or too little power to counteract the rotation of the body due to the rotation of the main rotor. For every action there is an equal and opposite reaction, ya know.

So if you beef up one motor, you're going to have to beef up the other, or figure out how to use the trim pots and/or aerodynamics to get stable flight.
