Most of the buildings are made of paper. Thex're downloadable at www.carrera4fun.de - a German slotcar-site. I just had to scale them down to 1:55 (which fits the Microsizers best I think).

All Boards are made of medium density fiberboard. Each board has a groove and tongue to fit perfectly even. So I can race the track without having the cars bumping...
