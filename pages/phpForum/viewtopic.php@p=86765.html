Hmm good point.

- You see we thought long and hard about that corner. It started with a different design but then my uncle made me realize where that corner is located, that is the same spot we will have the sofa set up (Racing corner).

Which means when the car drives by we did not want them to just zip on past us, instead the view infront would be a super fast car slowing down and takin the turn like it should be taken!

- We blocked off that corner, and we used clay for the bricks and stuff. It is not too harsh on the bodies, but a smart driver does not want to total his car right? Even pro's don't (well some at least lol)

- This makes the racing much more realistic. And truly shows the skill of a driver, and not just the potential of a car.
