Post by crazydave » Tue Nov 16, 2004 8:44 pm

Gots me a second monster, just in case I kill my first trying to make it go fast.

Got the Monster Patrol, based entirely on the fact it has a Chevy body.


The Green had to go (sorry SG), so I was just painting it Blue. This supposed to be a light Saphire Blue Metallic, but apparently someone switched the can tops.

I'm getting off track, so anyways, I had the can of paint pointed at the truck with the Silver base coat on. It was late and I wanted to go to bed, but I thought, "Wait, I don't have anymore Silver. Do I want a Silver graphic? Quick, think, before it's too late!"

I decided I wanted to do something quick, like really quick, like no time to BS quick. So I did a lightening bolt, and... I like it, I'm really glad I did that, and that it matches the angle of the wing was entirely coincidence, since the wing was not on the truck, and the bolt was drawn on a separate sheet of paper.

I also detailed the chassis, which I like, because I think it makes the body look lower.






The wing comes right off, for your less flashy days.


Here it is with the Red Ford. Decided I like the Ford Body with Black wheels. Looks meaner and faster.
...crazy man, crazy...dig it.
