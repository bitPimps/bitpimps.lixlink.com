Hey My name's Bart and im new to this forum. I have a micro turbo racer which ive had for 3 or 4 years and I decided to mod it. So i went out to Dollarama and bought a cheap mini RC that had a poorly detailed skyline body and tossed everything out except the body itself.

Now here is a list of mods:
- Acura Integra front Conversion with working lights
- Custom front bumper with working fog lights molded in it
- Custom rear bumper with working tail lights
- Custom side skirts and vents
- Roof scoop
- Hood scoop
- Custom Wing
- Rim Swap with wide slicks and disc brakes
- Custom Wide bodywork
- Custom Exhaust
- Side View Mirrors
- Neon underglow

Pics are soon to come as ive already converted the front to acura. Ive got most of the front bumper done. Hood scoop, started the wide body in the front and roof scoop almost done...
