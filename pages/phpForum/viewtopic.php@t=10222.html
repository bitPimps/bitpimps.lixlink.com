Do you have a Harbor Freight Tools around? If so, go get one of their "precision drill sets" they cost about $4 and have very small dills, carefully enlarge the hole and then glue the wheels onto the axle. Or, you can enlarge then with small nails, pins etc. But, if you can get a set, the precision drills will come in handy. You can also take a standard drill bit of the appropriate size, wrap tape around the end, until makes enough of a handle to grab onto with your fingertips, and use that for as a finger drill. A power drill works too quickly, and if you find yourself off center, or crooked, there's no time to correct it. The idea here is to be as straight as possible so your wheels don't wobble.
