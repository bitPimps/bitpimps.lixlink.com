Pimpin' Green Trucks

I've been workin' on these for a little while now and finally got around to throwin' them up.

The Large truck is an IW02 chassis that has been streched with a custom made Clear lexan T-plate and the body from a $10 Nikko clearence RC truck from KB.

It has been painted with Green Metalcast Andodized Spray Paint and coated with Future Floor polish. You may notice a couple of spots on the front bumper that are chipped. That was due to my impatience. I wanted to play before I protected it with the Future.

I still have to make the custom vinyl decals or find them and also make the front body mount. Right now its just the side body clips holding it on. it will have a slight rake to it when the front mount gets installed.

The front inside grill was covered in Bare Metal Foil, compliments of Bird.

The small truck is a ZZSE body that had all the trim removed and painted with the same paint as the large truck. It has also been coated with Future a couple of time and will receive the same decal package as the larger truck.

It has the ZZ purple motor with the green ZZ gear package and wides in the back.

Each trucks rims match as close as possible.









You can see in this next pic that it is in need of the front body mount.



Here is the shot of some of the damage. The only time I can drive it is over at Bird's with his IW controller. So when I had the chance I took it. My IW controller is a POS!!! Thats why it's missing paint. Plus, it was the inagural track session that night.







More pics in my gallery.

I hope to have this done sometime before summer.
