Thanks again for the compliments, guys. That's why I do it. That and so I can beat hue35's ass in style.

Yep, HO slot car wheels on the Gloria, painted metallic gold.

Here's some info:

Porsche:
Mosfet modded single speed PCB
Teal gears, 4.2 King motor
Spring suspension
Tab lowered front and rear
Removed wing, filled and sanded wing holes
Mask, paint (originally dark blue), detail. Model Master Light Ivory
Painted wheels gloss black

Lancer:
Single speed PCB
Teal gears, 4.2 King motor
Black finned heatsink
Body kit added front, sides, rear with pass-through cusom exhaust
Mask, paint, detail, clearcoat. Custom mix metallic silver-blue over Boyd's Hi Gloss White
LXX Black wheels

I'm surprised no one commented on this one, it's one of my favorites. The body kit looks like it came from the factory. I may still put some decals on this one


Mazda RX-7:
Mosfet modded single speed PCB
KIT racer red gears, 3.8 perfection motor
Black finned heatsink
Spring suspension
Body kit added front, sides, rear
Reshaped wing, added custom exhaust
Model Master Metallizer metallic paint (aluminum plate). You buff this stuff out right after you put it on and it looks like polished metal.
Tinted windows from inside
Clearcoat
KIT racer wheels, front and rear

Gloria:
single speed Panther PCB
Orange gears, 2.2 Microsizer motor (slow and low, that is the tempo)
Spring suspension
Tab lowered front
Removed wing, filled and sanded wing holes
Gold chassis detail, mask, paint, detail, clearcoat. Boyd's Lime Gold Pearl or something like that, and Testor's Gold
Bangin' stereo on the package shelf/motor clip device
HO slot car wheels, front and rear, silicone tires

I added the stereo, metal flake upholstery, gold chassis detail and clearcoat since the first time I flossed this ride.

Esso Supra:
Mosfet modded two speed PCB
teal gears, 4.2 Pro motor
Spring suspension
Tab lowered front and rear, as low as it can go, slightly routed rear wheel wells
Painted wheels gloss white

I put this one in here mostly because it drives like a mofo, and Supras don't look half bad when you drop them. Also, the Supra used to handle like crap, but the mosfets driving a fast motor seem to make it all better. It's a pretty nice $4 body from Toy East

Skyline:
Mosfet modded two speed PCB
Orange gears, 3.8 perfection motor
Black finned heatsink
Spring suspension
Mask, paint, detail, clearcoat. Boyd's
Nakamichi racer wheels, front and rear

Hue35 and I, unknown to eachother, bought the same color of paint. He put his over white primer I think, while mine has the inside of the body painted bright yellow. They end up looking totally different in RL.

So, any advice on which 3 to enter in the contest?[/b]
