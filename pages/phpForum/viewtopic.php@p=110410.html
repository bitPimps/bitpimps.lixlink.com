Well I was going to wait to post this but after I saw a few other guys' build threads I figured it'd be alright to post it up here.

Well it all started a few weeks ago when I joked with my pal Porschelova(r7r) about him giving me his TLT axles and stampede transmission. And now I'm amidst an expensive TLT build trying to get it done by our first Northern Ohio Scale Trail Runners GTG on the 23rd.

Here's what she'll be running:

TLT axles w/Stampede Tranny
ripper7racing "HellCAT" chassis
LRP AI Quantum Runner ESC
Tower Pro MG945 servo (possibly 2 for 4ws)
Integy 55T
Associated XP3 tx/rx
Powerizer 3300 batts
E-Maxx Shocks/Springs
Masher 2K's on Axial Rockster Beadlocks
HPI Land Rover Defender 90 Body
and plenty of custom's

So here are the pics.

From the beginning



borrowed some rims and tires and trimmed the body







chose a 12" wheelbase and 3.5" transmission





thought my r/c needed an r/c













wheels and tires came =]







attached the spare tire with using a tire iron!



hexes and other shit are here!





so that's where she stands. Waiting on servos, paint, and chassis yet. I'll update when I have to. Till then. Enjoy!
