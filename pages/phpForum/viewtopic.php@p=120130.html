Post by crazydave » Fri Dec 05, 2008 9:11 am

sidewinder wrote:I never did get one of the big lots clones...were they any good? They don't still carry them do they?
The Thunder Chargers from BigLots were IMO the best clone ever. $5 at a time when clones were still commonly being sold for $12.99. They had the fastest out of box motor, and the PCB seemed to deliver more punch with FET mod than the name brand ones. Good tight steering. Decent QC. Grippy tires on cool 6 spoke wheels. The only drawback, ugly bodies, and you'd have to modify the body mounts to mount a bit body.

They do not still carry them, they were replaced with I think they were called Micro Speed, which only lasted a year, and those were similar quality, but slightly more expensive.

At one time my collection was mostly Thunder Chargers, but most of those are dead now, I stocked up on the name brand cars when they were on clearance, and many of them have the Thunder Charger electronics in them
