I also printed single sheet inkjet (no special settings, just printed at 600 dpi like normal)
I think laser is much, much, better than inkjet and bubblejet, there's no "dots" to see, and you will see them with inkjet depending on the graphic and surface its going on.
when I did my inkjet, I clear coated the sheet after it was dried. If you do use inkjet or bubblejet, definitaly coat it or it's gonna bleed when you put the water slide decal in the water.
laser doesn't seem to bleed, at all and doesn't seem to need a protective coating, problem there is color laser printers can get pretty pricey.
