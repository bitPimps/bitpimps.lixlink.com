Post by crazydave » Sat Dec 01, 2007 1:48 am

Just for a half a second, I saw my sig line, and thought, "what the...?", but I get what's going on now.

I'm so happy you took the time to snap your own photos, Hue. I've always loved how your pictures come out, and I love how you focused in on the front end, so you can see right down the snorkel. Wild Ride came out great too. I have trouble with Blue/Green colors, they always look too blue, or too green. You pretty much nailed it, and it looks so vibrant.

Now that everything is in place, I have to tell everyone. Prostocker I just fell together. It was literally made out of spare parts that were just laying on my desk. I had no idea it would work so well, and make such an impact on people. When I was commissioned to build a similar car, things did not want to work for me, compounded by the fact that I have a great deal respect for Hue's work, and I wanted him to see my best work. This was the second chassis I built. I went through three sets of wheels trying to make the wheels spin true, which was getting desperate because Hot Wheels stopped making the cars they came on, but luckily I found one more set on one of the 99 cent cars, months later. I shorted a steering coil, and it almost melted through the chassis. I smoked a PCB in my hand while FET modding it. That's never happed to me before.

Really I started to think PS I was a fluke, and it'll never be duplicated, but I finally got it, two years later. I threw in the gasser, because I felt horrible for taking so long, and honestly, the wheels need to be remounted before it's a driver, and I just didn't feel like dealing with it. I figured Hue would give it the love it deserved.

I'm sure you guys realize this was about more than just an Xmod. It was about friendship, and mutual respect. I never had a chance to drag race the two prostockers together, but it's a cool feeling knowing I've got competition waiting out there for me. You're welcome, Hue. I'm sure they couldn't have better home.
...crazy man, crazy...dig it.
