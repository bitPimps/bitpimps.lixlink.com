I would believe it to be either a really good custom job, or maybe even a prototype. If I were to be designing a prototype for a company and posted a vid if it, I would be afraid of the company repremanding me for unvailing it before preproduction.
The reasons I think it's a custom? The screws are all of different types, carbon fiber, front and rear A arms are different colors, Losi uses JR for the higher end RTR radios. But it does have the Losi brushless motor.

The problems with my thoughts of it being a custom job, are the wheels. They are concave, and I don't know of any 1/36 wheels like that. Also, the bulkheads and diffs I am unsure of. The differentals (if there are some) don't seem to be very smooth. Too much gear noise when he turned the tire.

I'd hate to see the price for that thing if it is a new Losi.
