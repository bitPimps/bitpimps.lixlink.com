I'm pretty sure I was one of the first to do this. I'm also pretty sure I was the last to show pictures of it. I got a Digi cam shortly before Christmas. I just opened it today...

Excuse the quality for now, I'm just getting used to it.





To paint the grill, I used a tooth pick instead of a brush. I dipped the toothpick in the paint, let the wood soak up a bit, then slid it across the grill. Did a pretty good job at not getting in the cracks.

The bumper is off of a Torino. I had to cut the rear of the truck body to get it to fit right.
