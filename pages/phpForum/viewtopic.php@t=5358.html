Post by HirotoR34 » Thu Jan 13, 2005 3:05 pm

Squadron putty from the LHS and a lot of patience. I use ZZ packaging on the inside of the shell to back the putty. I glop it on and shape it the best I can while it's still soft. Then after drying, I use files and sanding boards made from ZZ packaging with different grits of sandpaper glued to them to get the shape smooth. It's a lot of work, but worth the effort.

BTW: What did you make the rollbar and lights out of on your Silverado MT? I want to add those and side steps to a couple of my other MT's
