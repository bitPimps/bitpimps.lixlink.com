okay. if you take apart your zipzap SE on some of the screws it will have that red crap, right? to take it out use an exacto/model knife and in the front there is a bar with red crap on the screw. after you take the red crap off, you should unscrew it just a little bit just before the bar comes off. this will give you better travel in the front and helps you drift better by leaning on the side in the front adding more traction to help swing the car to the side. it doesnt drastically improve drift, but it helps. TIP: if you put slick tires on the car's rear and all-season tires in the front(you can find both in the custom tires and wheels package i think)it drifts better as well try it yourself and give me comments.
