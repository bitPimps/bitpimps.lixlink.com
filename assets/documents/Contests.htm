bitPimps Modding Contests Guidelines, Rules, Regulations, Plans, Schedules, and Judging Criteria.

 

Overview:

This document serves as a guide to help maintain the integrity and consistency of the bitPimps modding contests only, this does not include “quizzes” or “projects”. Each and every “modding” contest bitPimps hosts should follow these guidelines. As moderators, it is our responsibility to make sure the guidelines are practiced and preserved.

 

 

Rules and Regulations:

Rules and regulations for each contest may be refined if necessary, but only after agreement. All contests rules should be communicated to the community and must minimally abide by the following rules:

-         Contestants must be registered users of the bitPimps Forums.

-         Contestants may enter as many separately modified vehicles as they want.

-         Contestants are allowed to win more than a single place in a single contest. That is to say, 1 contestant can win 2 places in the same contest.

-         Contestants may post as many photos or videos of their entry as they feel necessary.

-         Contestants must have their “Username” and “bitPimps” on something (like a piece of paper) in at least 1 photo of their entry.

-         No previous bitPimps contest winning vehicle may enter.

 

 

Judging Criteria:

When it comes time to judge, we should follow at least these basic objectives, though others may play a factor, depending on the contest:
(In no specific order)

-         Modification job, content, originality, implementation, and functionality.
Contests may submit video to offer proof that can be considered at judging time.

-         Paint job, originality, and appeal.

 

 

Plans and Schedules:

In light of what we have learned from the past, we all have agreed to re-vamp how we run the modding contests as well as how regularly we run them. First, let’s discuss the “schedule” of contests:

-         bitPimps Year-End contest
Like normal, our signature contest, we’ll run this near the end of each year. We’ll start the contest at the beginning of October (?) and it will run until the end of December.
We can discuss the possibility of just “announcing” the contest at the beginning of October and not actually accepting entries until the beginning of December. However, it is not suggested, early entries will inspire others to bring their “A” game and get to work on something special.

-         Some other contest to be determined
This is when we’ll run all the other cool contests we’ve come up with, we can discuss whatever we come up with to decide when this will run from and to. Though it should be around 4-6 months after the New Year so as to keep a generally even interval between major contests.

 

 

Now let’s discuss some of the “planning” we’ll follow for the modding contests so as to allow a greater number of entries as well as allowing moderators to be participants. We’re going to follow a naturally occurring “rotation” of sorts that we’ll decide on before we announce a contest.

Once we’ve solidified contest details, moderators can elect to be a judge for that contest or a participant and should make that claim in the proper moderators thread.

Only us moderators can be judges for now, we’re not going to open up judging to the community.

We will create a new Usergroup and Forum Category called “Contest Judging” that only judges for the contest will have access to. Once the contest is over and places are chosen, all judges are removed from the usergroup and we are ready to choose new judges when it comes time for the next contest.
