bitPimps, Bigger, Better, and Stronger

 

Overview:

This document serves as an explanation of and a primer to the various new “guidelines” bitPimps will be adhering to. As bitPimps grows and evolves, so has our knowledge of solutions to potential issues within the various site features, such as contests, auctions, and general site management. Utilizing our past learning experiences and taking the initiative to improve on those is what is setting us apart from the rest. The documents are a first step in an effort to give us a good solid base to continue to build and expand from.

 

 

Guidelines:

The various guidelines were created for several reasons, all of which will help “bitPimps” run more smoothly, sufficiently, and will help make this place more fun for everyone including us moderators. Currently, we have in place the following guidelines:

-         Contest Guidelines
The majority of improvements discussed in this document were constructed from our past learning experiences. Most of which we have already agreed on solutions for the various issues encountered.

-         Auction Block Guidelines
The improvements discussed in this document were also constructed from our past learning experiences. The new improvements will help streamline the process, cause less stress, strain, and hassle, as well as making for some more exciting auctions.
