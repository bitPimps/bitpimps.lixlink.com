bitPimps Auction Block Guidelines, Rules, Regulations, Plans, and Schedules.

 

Overview:

This document serves as a guide to help maintain the integrity and consistency of the bitPimps auctions only and does not effect any other “projects”. Each and every auction bitPimps hosts should follow these guidelines. As moderators, it is our responsibility to make sure the guidelines are practiced and preserved.

 

 

Rules and Regulations:

There aren’t many to be observed, but the main ones are:

-         The item to be auctioned off should be complete and in possession before the auction is started.

-         Only registered users may bid on an item.

-         Each auctioneer is responsible for providing the item to the winning bidder. If something is lost or broken, that auctioneer is responsible for; returning the funds, fixing the item, or providing another item of equal value, which should be determined by the bidder as long as the bidder is reasonable – no outlandish requests.

 

 

Plans and Schedules:

Learning from our past, we are going to run the auctions slightly different so as to allow a more natural progression and to help eliminate stresses and strains caused by tight deadlines and meeting goals.

-         The “Auction Block” should stay open all year around.

-         Only us moderators are allowed to put an item up for auction.

-         Each moderator should run their own auctions, provide the prize, and collect the money, which they can then mail to the “Piggy Bank”. We can decide how much to use for hosting, prizes, whatever.

 

With the auction block being open year around, the only schedule to follow is the time given to bid on each item offered in an auction. Those schedules can vary slightly as need be. However, for the most part, we should stick to 1 week or less as we’ve done in the past.
