with all the outdoor driving and jumping my bits are getting bashed up reel good!  so to protect the front end from getting too scratched up (and mainly for looks!) i used the metal off a 1.4mb floppy to make skid pans. you could use an aluminium can if you want just so long as whatever you use is not magnetically attractive, or the steering will be rendered useless. this stuff can be cut with scissors and bent easily. i just glued them in place with good quality super glue.
